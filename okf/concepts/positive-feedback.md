# Positive Feedback

Positive feedback is a control and systems mechanism in which the output of a process amplifies the input that produced it, reinforcing change rather than damping it. While destabilising in control systems, it underlies self-reinforcing dynamics such as network effects, exponential growth, and adoption cascades. Its behaviour contrasts with negative feedback, which seeks equilibrium.
