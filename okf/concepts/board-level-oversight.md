# Board-Level Oversight

Board-Level Oversight refers to the governance responsibility of a corporate or institutional board of directors to monitor, evaluate, and guide the organisation's strategic direction, risk appetite, executive conduct, and compliance posture. It encompasses the board's duty to act as an informed check on executive management, reviewing material risks—including operational, financial, legal, reputational, and increasingly technology risks such as AI adoption—on behalf of shareholders and other stakeholders.
