# Retailer

A retailer is a business that sells goods to consumers. Eurostat's glossary describes retail trade as a form of trade in which goods are mainly purchased and resold to the consumer or end-user, generally in small quantities and in the state in which they were purchased by the retailer, or following minor transformations.[^1]
