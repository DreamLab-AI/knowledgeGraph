# Aquifer

An aquifer is a geological body with enough saturated, permeable material to yield significant quantities of water to wells and springs.[^1][^2] The University of Arizona guide adds that the water is held in fractures and cracks of rock or in the pores between grains of sand and gravel.[^3]
