# Spatial Annotation

Spatial annotation is the placement of digital notes, labels, or markers anchored to specific positions in a three-dimensional physical or virtual space, so that the content persists relative to real-world geometry as the viewer moves. It relies on spatial mapping and pose tracking to keep annotations registered to surfaces or objects. It is a foundational interaction for augmented reality collaboration, maintenance guidance, and shared spatial computing.
