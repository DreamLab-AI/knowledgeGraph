# Digital Commons

The digital commons are shared, non-rivalrous information resources that a community collectively produces, governs, and stewards under open licences and self-defined rules, rather than through private enclosure or state provision. Examples include open-source software, open data, free knowledge repositories, and open scientific outputs, often coordinated via peer production and commons-based governance. As a governance concept it bridges classical commons theory with digital public goods, examining how communities sustain shared resources, prevent enclosure, and align incentives in distributed and blockchain-supported settings.
