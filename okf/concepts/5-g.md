# 5G

5G (Fifth Generation) is the latest generation of mobile cellular network technology, standardised by 3GPP from Release 15 onwards, characterised by peak data rates exceeding 20 Gbps, radio latencies below 1 ms, and support for up to one million connected devices per square kilometre. It introduces three primary use-case families: enhanced mobile broadband (eMBB), ultra-reliable low-latency communications (URLLC), and massive machine-type communications (mMTC). 5G underpins advances in spatial computing, autonomous vehicles, industrial automation, and IoT at scale.
