# OFDM

OFDM (Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing) is a digital modulation scheme that transmits data in parallel over many closely spaced, mutually orthogonal subcarriers. Splitting a high-rate stream into many low-rate subcarriers makes the signal robust to multipath fading and allows simple frequency-domain equalisation. It is the physical-layer foundation of Wi-Fi, LTE, 5G, DVB, and many other modern broadband systems.
