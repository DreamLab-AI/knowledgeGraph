# Supply Chain Governance

Supply chain governance is the set of means by which a buyer or lead firm steers the behaviour of the firms in its supply network, and the sources opened here treat it in two ways that are related but not the same: as a theory of value chain structure and as a practice of codes, audits and standards.[^1][^2]
