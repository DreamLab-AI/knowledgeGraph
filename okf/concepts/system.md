# System

A bounded, purposeful arrangement of components—hardware, software, data, and processes—that interact to perform functions beyond those of any individual component. In AI contexts, a system integrates models, inference engines, data pipelines, and interfaces into deployable solutions that operate within broader sociotechnical environments.
