# Centralised Control

Centralised control is a governance arrangement in which decision-making authority, coordination, and enforcement are concentrated in a single entity or hierarchy rather than distributed among participants. It offers clarity of accountability and rapid, consistent decisions but introduces a single point of failure and dependence on the controlling party's competence and intentions. Centralised control is the principal foil against which decentralised and distributed governance models define themselves.
