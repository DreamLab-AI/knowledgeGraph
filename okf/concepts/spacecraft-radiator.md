# Spacecraft Radiator

A spacecraft radiator is a thermally conductive surface arranged to emit internally generated heat towards a cold radiative sink. Equipment may mount directly to its panel or connect through a spreader, strap, heat pipe or fluid loop. The transport path moves heat to the radiator; the exposed surface releases it by infrared radiation.[^1][^2][^3]
