# TradeLens

TradeLens was a blockchain-enabled shipping platform announced in 2018 and jointly developed by IBM and GTD Solution, a division of Maersk, to promote more efficient and secure global trade; Maersk and IBM announced its discontinuation on 29 November 2022.[^1]
