# GRI Standards

The GRI Standards are a globally adopted framework published by the Global Reporting Initiative for organisations to disclose their economic, environmental, and social impacts. They provide a modular set of universal, sector, and topic standards that standardise sustainability and ESG reporting for comparability across firms. Widely referenced by regulators and investors, they form a backbone of corporate non-financial disclosure.
