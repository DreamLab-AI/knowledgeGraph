# Master Data Management

A discipline and set of processes for creating and maintaining a single, consistent view of an organisation's core business entities such as customers, products, and suppliers. It governs how master data is defined, stored, and shared across systems to establish authoritative records and support reporting, compliance, and operational consistency.
