# Computational Complexity Theory

The mathematical study of the resources — principally time and memory (space) — required to solve computational problems, and the classification of problems according to their inherent difficulty into complexity classes such as P, NP, PSPACE, BPP, and EXPTIME. It establishes which problems are tractable (solvable efficiently in polynomial time) and which are intractable, and investigates the relationships between complexity classes, most famously the unresolved P vs NP question.
