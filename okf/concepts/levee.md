# Levee

A levee is a human-made barrier built to reduce the frequency or probability of flooding in a protected part of a floodplain.[^1][^2] The US Army Corps of Engineers says most levees in the U.S. are earthen embankments made primarily of local soil materials, although many include more than one type of material.[^1]
