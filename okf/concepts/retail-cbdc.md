# Retail CBDC

A retail central bank digital currency (CBDC) is a digital form of sovereign money issued directly by a central bank for use by the general public in everyday payments. Unlike wholesale CBDCs, which serve interbank settlement, retail CBDCs target consumers and merchants and may use account-based or token-based designs over distributed or centralised ledgers. They aim to provide a public digital payment instrument with central-bank credit safety.
