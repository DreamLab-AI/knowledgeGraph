# Policy Framework

A policy framework is a structured set of principles, rules, standards, and processes established by a government body, standards organisation, or institution to guide decision-making, regulate behaviour, and ensure accountability within a defined domain. It translates high-level objectives — such as safety, fairness, or interoperability — into operational requirements and compliance mechanisms. Policy frameworks provide the normative scaffolding that enables coordinated action across organisations and jurisdictions.
