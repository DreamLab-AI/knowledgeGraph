# Sustainable Finance

Sustainable finance refers to the integration of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria into financial decision-making, product design, and market regulation with the goal of channelling capital flows toward activities that support a sustainable economy. It encompasses green bonds, sustainability-linked loans, ESG investing, climate risk management, and regulatory taxonomies that define what qualifies as environmentally sustainable economic activity.
