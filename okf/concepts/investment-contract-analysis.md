# Investment Contract Analysis

Investment contract analysis is the legal evaluation of whether a financial arrangement constitutes a security, most commonly by applying the Howey test's criteria of an investment of money in a common enterprise with an expectation of profit derived from others' efforts. In crypto-asset regulation it determines whether a token offering falls under securities law and its attendant registration and disclosure obligations. It is a decisive step in assessing regulatory exposure for digital-asset issuers.
