# Physical Presence

Physical presence is the perceptual sense of being bodily situated in a remote or virtual environment, including the ability to act on and feel that environment. In telepresence it is produced by combining spatial audio-visual immersion with embodiment cues such as haptic feedback and a controllable remote body or robot. Strong physical presence makes a mediated location feel as though the user is genuinely there.
