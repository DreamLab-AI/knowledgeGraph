# Safety Fine Tuning

Safety Fine Tuning is a specialised training stage applied after general capability training to reduce harmful outputs and align model behaviour with safety principles. It employs safety-curated datasets, RLHF, and constitutional AI objectives to harden models against adversarial misuse and emergent misalignment.
