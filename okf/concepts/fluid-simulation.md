# Fluid Simulation

Fluid Simulation is the computational modelling of liquid and gas dynamics using numerical methods — notably computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and Navier-Stokes solvers — to produce physically plausible representations of water, smoke, fire, and atmospheric phenomena in real-time or offline rendering pipelines. In spatial computing and metaverse environments it contributes to environmental immersion and interactive physics, running on GPU compute shaders for performance-critical applications.
