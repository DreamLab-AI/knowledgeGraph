# SCARA Robot

Selective Compliance Articulated Robot Arm — a four-axis industrial robot with two rotary joints in the horizontal plane that give high compliance laterally whilst providing rigid vertical stiffness along the Z-axis. SCARA robots excel at high-speed pick-and-place, assembly, and packaging tasks that require precise horizontal positioning but resist vertical forces.
