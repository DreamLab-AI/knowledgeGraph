# Proprietary Protocol

A proprietary protocol is a communication or data-exchange protocol owned and controlled by a single organisation, whose specification is closed, licensed, or undocumented rather than openly published. Such protocols can enable tight vendor integration and rapid iteration but tend to inhibit interoperability and create dependence on the controlling vendor. They contrast with open standards, which are publicly specified and freely implementable to promote cross-vendor compatibility.
