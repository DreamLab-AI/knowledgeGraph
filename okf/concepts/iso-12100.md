# ISO 12100

ISO 12100 is the foundational international standard for the safety of machinery, setting out general principles for design, risk assessment and risk reduction. It defines a structured methodology in which designers identify hazards, estimate and evaluate risk, then apply inherently safe design, safeguarding and information-for-use in a defined order of priority. As a Type-A standard it provides the overarching framework that more specific Type-B and Type-C machinery standards build upon.
