# TOE Framework

The Technology-Organization-Environment (TOE) framework is an organisational-level theory explaining how a firm's technological context, organisational characteristics, and external environment jointly shape the adoption and assimilation of technological innovations. It is widely applied in information-systems research to model the determinants of enterprise uptake of emerging technologies such as AI and cloud computing.
