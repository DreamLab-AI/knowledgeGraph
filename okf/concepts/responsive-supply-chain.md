# Responsive Supply Chain

In Fisher's framework a responsive supply chain, which he calls a market-responsive chain, is one designed to respond quickly to unpredictable demand in order to minimise stockouts, forced markdowns and obsolete inventory, and it is the chain he matches to “innovative products”.[^1][^2]
