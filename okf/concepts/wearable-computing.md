# Wearable Computing

Wearable computing is the design and use of computing devices worn on or close to the body, integrating sensing, processing and display into garments, accessories and head-mounted units. These devices provide continuous, context-aware interaction and data capture without occupying the hands, enabling hands-free assistance, health monitoring and immersive experiences. It is a foundational layer of spatial and ambient computing.
