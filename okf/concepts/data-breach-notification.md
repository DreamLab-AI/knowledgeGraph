# Data Breach Notification

Data breach notification is the legal and operational obligation to inform supervisory authorities and, where there is a high risk, affected individuals after a breach of personal data. Frameworks such as the UK and EU GDPR require controllers to report qualifying breaches to the regulator without undue delay, typically within seventy-two hours of becoming aware, and to document the breach regardless of whether it is reportable. The duty turns an internal security incident into a regulated disclosure event, with timing, content and assessment of risk all prescribed by law.
