# Financial Transactions

Financial Transactions are records of the transfer of monetary value or digital assets between parties, executed through centralised payment systems, blockchain networks, or Layer-2 protocols such as the Lightning Network. Within spatial computing and metaverse contexts, financial transactions underpin virtual economies, in-world purchases, NFT trades, and cross-platform value exchange, requiring robust settlement, fraud detection, and regulatory compliance mechanisms.
