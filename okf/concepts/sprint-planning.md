# Sprint Planning

Sprint planning is the Scrum event in which a development team selects items from the product backlog and defines a goal and plan for the upcoming time-boxed iteration. The team negotiates scope against capacity, decomposes chosen items into actionable work, and commits to a realistic sprint backlog. It establishes shared understanding of what will be delivered and how, anchoring the iteration's focus.
