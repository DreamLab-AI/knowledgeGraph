# RegTech

RegTech (regulatory technology) is the application of technologies such as data analytics, machine learning, cloud computing and automation to help regulated firms meet compliance obligations more efficiently and accurately. It streamlines tasks including identity verification, transaction monitoring, regulatory reporting and risk surveillance. RegTech reduces the manual cost of compliance while improving the timeliness and consistency of regulatory outcomes.
