# Zero Knowledge

Cryptographic proof systems enabling a prover to convince a verifier that a statement is true without disclosing any information beyond the validity of the statement itself. zk-SNARKs and zk-STARKs are the dominant proof systems, with applications spanning blockchain scalability through ZK-rollups, privacy-preserving identity verification, confidential DeFi transactions, and verifiable AI model outputs. Foundational to privacy-preserving computation and Layer 2 scaling architectures.
