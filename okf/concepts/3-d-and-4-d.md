# 3D and 4D

The domain of three-dimensional and four-dimensional content creation, covering tools, techniques, and AI-powered pipelines for generating, editing, and rendering 3D assets and temporally dynamic (4D) representations. Encompasses text-to-3D systems (NVIDIA Edify, Luma Genie, Point-E), neural radiance fields, Gaussian splatting, and diffusion-based mesh generation, as well as 6D pose estimation, scene-scale generation, and integration with AR/VR workflows.
