# Authorised Economic Operator

An Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) is, in the World Customs Organization (WCO) definition, a party involved in the international movement of goods in whatever function that a national customs administration has approved, or that has been approved on its behalf, as complying with WCO or equivalent supply chain security standards.[^1][^2]
