# Autonomous Mobile Robots

Robotic platforms capable of self-directed locomotion through environments, navigating without fixed infrastructure guidance by using onboard sensing, mapping, localisation, and path-planning capabilities. Autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) operate in dynamic, human-shared spaces and are distinguished from automated guided vehicles (AGVs) by their ability to adapt routes in real time rather than following pre-defined paths.
