# W3C PROV

W3C PROV is a family of W3C Recommendations that define a domain-agnostic data model and serialisations for representing provenance, the records of entities, activities, and agents involved in producing or influencing a piece of data. Centred on the PROV-DM data model and PROV-O OWL ontology, it enables interoperable description of how artefacts came to be, supporting trust, reproducibility, and auditing. It is widely used in scientific workflows, data catalogues, and metadata management systems.
