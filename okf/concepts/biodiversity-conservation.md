# Biodiversity Conservation

Biodiversity conservation is the protection, restoration and sustainable management of the variety of life on Earth — genes, species, habitats and ecosystems — to maintain ecological function and resilience. It combines protected areas, species recovery, habitat restoration, sustainable use and policy instruments, increasingly informed by monitoring data and remote sensing. As a pillar of environmental sustainability, it is closely linked to climate change mitigation, ecosystem services and emerging biodiversity finance markets.
