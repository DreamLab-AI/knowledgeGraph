# SHA-256

Secure Hash Algorithm producing a deterministic 256-bit digest from arbitrary input, forming the cryptographic foundation of Bitcoin's proof-of-work, Merkle tree construction, block header chaining, and address derivation in most major blockchain systems. Produces avalanche-effect collision resistance with 2^128 preimage security. Standardised in FIPS 180-4 by NIST as part of the SHA-2 family.
