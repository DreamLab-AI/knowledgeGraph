# Virtual Group Dynamics

The psychological and behavioural patterns of interaction, role formation, norm development, decision-making, conflict, and cohesion within groups operating in virtual environments, shaped by unique affordances of digital mediation such as anonymity, persistence, and spatial distribution. Key phenomena include the online disinhibition effect, avatar-mediated identity, emergent leadership hierarchies, and scalable broadcast communication structures.
