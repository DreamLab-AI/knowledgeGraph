# Cross-DAO Benchmarking

Cross-DAO benchmarking is the comparative analysis of decentralised autonomous organisations against shared metrics such as treasury size, voter participation, proposal throughput, and contributor activity. It aggregates on-chain governance and financial data to rank or contextualise a DAO's performance relative to peers. It informs governance design, delegate accountability, and treasury strategy.
