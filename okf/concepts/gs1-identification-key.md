# GS1 Identification Key

A GS1 identification key is a numeric or alphanumeric string that incorporates a GS1 Prefix, GS1 Company Prefix or GS1-8 Prefix and is the basis of the unique GS1 identifiers defined for areas such as trade, logistics and asset management.[^1]
