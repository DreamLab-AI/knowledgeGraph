# SBTi

The Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) is a body that defines and validates corporate greenhouse-gas reduction targets against climate science, specifically the goal of limiting warming to 1.5C. It publishes sector-specific methodologies and independently certifies that company targets are consistent with the Paris Agreement. SBTi validation has become a widely referenced benchmark in ESG reporting.
