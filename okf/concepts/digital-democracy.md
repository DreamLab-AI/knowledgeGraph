# Digital Democracy

Digital democracy is the use of digital technologies to broaden and deepen citizen participation in political decision-making, including online deliberation, e-petitions, participatory budgeting, and novel voting mechanisms. It seeks to make governance more transparent, inclusive, and responsive by lowering the barriers to engagement. It encompasses both incremental e-government tools and experimental collective-choice systems such as quadratic voting.
