# Collective Action

Coordinated behaviour by a group of individuals or organisations toward a shared goal that no single actor could achieve unilaterally. It is characterised by interdependence, the risk of free-riding, and the need for mechanisms — such as incentives, norms, or contracts — to align individual contributions with the group objective.
