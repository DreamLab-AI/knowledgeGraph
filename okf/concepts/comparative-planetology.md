# Comparative Planetology

Comparative planetology examines the same physical and chemical processes across several planets, moons or small bodies. Differences in mass, composition, atmosphere, distance from the Sun and geological history act as natural experiments. Comparing their effects helps explain why related worlds followed different paths and tests whether an explanation developed for one body works elsewhere.
