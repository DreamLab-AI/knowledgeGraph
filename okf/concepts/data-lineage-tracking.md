# Data Lineage Tracking

Data lineage tracking is the automated capture and maintenance of provenance metadata as data is created, transformed and moved across pipelines and platforms. It instruments transformations and queries to build a continuously updated lineage graph rather than a static map. Often expressed using provenance vocabularies such as PROV-O, it underpins reproducibility, trust and governance in data fabric architectures.
