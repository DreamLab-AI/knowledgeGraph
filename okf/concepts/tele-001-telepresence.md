# TELE 001 telepresence

TELE 001 Telepresence is a foundational concept within the telecollaboration domain that defines the sense of physical presence transmitted across a distance through audio-visual, haptic, and immersive communication technologies. It serves as the baseline specification for how distributed participants experience co-presence in collaborative environments, underpinning higher-order telecollaboration and virtual meeting systems.
