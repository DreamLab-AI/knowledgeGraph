# Industrial Symbiosis

Industrial symbiosis is a collaborative approach within industrial ecology in which geographically proximate companies exchange materials, energy, water, and by-products to achieve collective environmental and economic benefits that are unattainable individually. By treating the waste or surplus of one facility as a resource input for another, industrial symbiosis operationalises circular economy principles at an industrial park or regional scale.
