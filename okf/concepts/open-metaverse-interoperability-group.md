# Open Metaverse Interoperability Group

The Open Metaverse Interoperability Group (OMI) is a community-driven body that develops open protocols and schemas for moving identity, avatars, and virtual objects between independent virtual worlds. It produces specifications for portable avatars, glTF extensions, and cross-platform identity so that metaverse experiences are not locked into single proprietary platforms. Its work aims to make virtual environments composable and interoperable rather than walled gardens.
