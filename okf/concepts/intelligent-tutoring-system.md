# Intelligent Tutoring System

An AI-powered software system that delivers personalised instruction by modelling a learner's knowledge state, selecting appropriate pedagogical strategies, and adapting content difficulty in real time without requiring a human instructor. Intelligent tutoring systems integrate domain knowledge, learner models, and tutoring strategies to provide one-on-one instructional support at scale.
