# AI Talent

The human capital pool comprising individuals with specialised skills in machine learning research, AI engineering, data science, and related disciplines who design, build, evaluate, and govern artificial intelligence systems. AI talent is a scarce and strategically contested resource, with demand from frontier laboratories, technology companies, and public-sector institutions consistently outpacing the supply produced by academic programmes. The composition of the talent pool—including its geographic distribution, diversity, and specialisation—directly shapes the pace and direction of AI progress.
