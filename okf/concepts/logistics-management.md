# Logistics Management

Logistics management is the managerial practice of planning, implementing and controlling the logistics of an organisation. The CSCMP glossary defines it as that part of supply chain management that plans, implements and controls the efficient, effective forward and reverse flow and storage of goods, services and related information between the point of origin and the point of consumption in order to meet customers' requirements.[^1]
