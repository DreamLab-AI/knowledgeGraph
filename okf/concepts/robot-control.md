# Robot Control

Robot Control encompasses the systems, algorithms, and methodologies that enable robots to execute tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously through sensing, decision-making, and actuation. It integrates perception, planning, and actuation into closed-loop systems operating in dynamic environments.
