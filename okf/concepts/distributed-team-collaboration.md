# Distributed Team Collaboration

Distributed team collaboration encompasses the practices, tools, and organisational patterns that enable geographically dispersed individuals working across multiple time zones to coordinate effectively on shared goals without co-location. It integrates asynchronous communication protocols, synchronous video conferencing, shared digital workspaces, version-controlled artefacts, and social norms around documentation and decision-making transparency to replicate — and often surpass — the coordination bandwidth of co-located teams. The discipline has grown from a niche practice of open-source communities and multinational corporations into a mainstream organisational capability following the global shift to remote work accelerated by the 2020 pandemic.
