# Energy Efficiency

Energy efficiency is the property of a system that achieves a given output or function while consuming the least possible energy. In computing, robotics, and control systems it is measured as useful work per unit of energy, and is improved through better algorithms, hardware, scheduling, and motion optimisation. It is a core sustainability objective because reductions in energy draw lower operating cost and carbon footprint without sacrificing performance.
