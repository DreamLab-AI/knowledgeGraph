# Data

Data is the recorded representation of facts, observations or measurements in a form suitable for storage, processing and communication. It is the raw material from which information and knowledge are derived through interpretation and analysis, and may be structured, semi-structured or unstructured. Across computing and analytics, data is captured, modelled, stored, transformed and governed throughout a lifecycle that determines its usefulness and trustworthiness.
