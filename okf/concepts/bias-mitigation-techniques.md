# Bias Mitigation Techniques

Methods and interventions designed to reduce algorithmic bias and improve fairness in AI systems through modifications at pre-processing (data reweighting, resampling), in-processing (fairness constraints, adversarial debiasing), and post-processing (threshold optimisation) stages of the machine learning pipeline. Each approach involves tradeoffs between fairness improvement and predictive accuracy.
