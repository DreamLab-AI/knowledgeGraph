# Real World Asset

A real-world asset (RWA) is a tangible or off-chain financial asset, such as real estate, commodities, invoices, bonds or equities, whose ownership or economic rights are represented on a blockchain as a token. Tokenisation links the on-chain representation to legal claims and custody arrangements in the physical or traditional financial world. RWAs bridge decentralised finance with regulated, conventional asset markets.
