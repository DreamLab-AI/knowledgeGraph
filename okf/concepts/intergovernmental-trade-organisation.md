# Intergovernmental Trade Organisation

An intergovernmental trade organisation is an international organisation, established by governments, that sets or administers rules for trade or transport. No opened source defines the combined term, so the page builds it from the definition of an international organisation. The UN International Law Commission defines an international organisation as one established by a treaty or other instrument governed by international law and possessing its own international legal personality, and adds that international organisations may include as members, in addition to States, other entities.[^1]
