# Net Zero Transition

The net zero transition is the structural shift of economies and financial portfolios toward balancing residual greenhouse-gas emissions with removals, in line with limiting global warming. It mobilises capital toward low-carbon technologies, retires or retrofits high-emitting assets, and aligns corporate strategy and disclosure with decarbonisation pathways. Transition finance provides the funding and instruments that allow hard-to-abate sectors to decarbonise credibly over time.
