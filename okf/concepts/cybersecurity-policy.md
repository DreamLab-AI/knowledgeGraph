# Cybersecurity Policy

A cybersecurity policy is a formal set of rules, roles, and expectations that govern how an organisation or jurisdiction protects information systems, data, and networks from threats. It translates risk appetite and legal obligations into actionable standards covering access, incident handling, data protection, and acceptable use. Cybersecurity policy operates at organisational level as internal governance and at national level as regulation and strategy.
