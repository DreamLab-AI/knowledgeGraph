# Information Integrity

Information integrity is the property and governance objective of ensuring that information remains accurate, complete, authentic and resistant to unauthorised or deceptive alteration across its lifecycle. In the digital-content context it spans provenance, authenticity verification and resilience against misinformation and disinformation. It draws on technical measures such as digital watermarking and cryptographic provenance alongside institutional trust-and-safety practices.
