# Perpetual Futures

Perpetual futures are derivative contracts that track an underlying asset's price without an expiry or settlement date. A periodic funding-rate payment between long and short holders tethers the contract price to the spot index, replacing the convergence that expiry provides in traditional futures. They are a dominant instrument in crypto derivatives markets, enabling leveraged directional exposure that can be held indefinitely.
