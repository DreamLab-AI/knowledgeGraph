# User Monitoring

User monitoring is the observation and logging of user behaviour and interactions within a platform to detect policy violations, abuse, safety risks or anomalous activity. In metaverse and online environments it supports trust-and-safety enforcement and agreement compliance, balancing protective oversight against privacy and consent. It typically feeds moderation, incident response and compliance reporting workflows.
