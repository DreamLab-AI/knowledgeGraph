# UK Tech Ecosystem

The United Kingdom's technology industry landscape, ranked as Europe's leading tech ecosystem with a combined market valuation of $1.2 trillion and over 17,000 VC-backed startups; the third-largest AI market globally after the US and China, encompassing unicorns, regional clusters (London, Cambridge), venture capital flows, and national policy frameworks for digital innovation.
