# Transfer Of Funds Regulation

The Transfer of Funds Regulation is a body of financial regulation requiring that information about the payer and payee accompany transfers of funds and certain crypto-asset transfers, so that transactions remain traceable for anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing purposes. It implements the Financial Action Task Force travel rule within a regulatory framework, obliging payment service providers and crypto-asset service providers to collect, transmit, and screen originator and beneficiary data. It is a central pillar of payment-chain transparency.
