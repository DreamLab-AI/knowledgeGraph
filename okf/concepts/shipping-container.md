# Shipping Container

A shipping container is a freight container: an article of transport equipment of a permanent character, strong enough for repeated use, specially designed to carry goods by one or more modes of transport without intermediate reloading, and fitted with corner fittings so that it can be secured and handled.[^1][^2]
