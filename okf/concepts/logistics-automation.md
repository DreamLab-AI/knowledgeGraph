# Logistics Automation

Logistics automation is the application of robotics, software, and AI to the physical and informational tasks of moving, storing, and tracking goods across supply chains — including goods-to-person fulfilment, autonomous transport, and intelligent route optimisation — with the goal of reducing labour costs, improving throughput, and increasing reliability.
