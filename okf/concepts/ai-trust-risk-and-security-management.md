# AI Trust Risk and Security Management

A comprehensive governance framework — popularised by Gartner and aligned with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework — for managing the trustworthiness, security, and ethical risks of AI systems throughout their lifecycle. It operationalises three pillars: Trust (transparent, auditable, reliable AI behaviour), Risk (proactive identification and mitigation of algorithmic bias, data quality issues, and regulatory non-compliance), and Security (protection against adversarial attacks, data poisoning, and model integrity threats).
