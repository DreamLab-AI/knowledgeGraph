# Universal Avatar

A cross-platform digital representation of a user that maintains consistent identity, appearance, and asset customisations across multiple metaverse applications, games, and virtual environments. Universal avatars rely on interoperability standards such as glTF and VRM to enable a single avatar to function across thousands of compatible platforms without requiring recreation.
