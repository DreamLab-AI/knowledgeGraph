# Computational Linguistics

Computational Linguistics is the interdisciplinary study of language from a computational perspective, developing formal models and algorithms that enable computers to analyse, generate and understand human language. It draws on theoretical linguistics, computer science and statistics to model phenomena such as syntax, semantics and discourse. The field underpins practical natural language processing systems and provides the scientific grounding for language technologies.
