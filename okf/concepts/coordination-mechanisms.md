# Coordination Mechanisms

Coordination Mechanisms are the structural arrangements, protocols, and incentive designs that enable multiple autonomous agents—whether human organisations, software processes, or robotic systems—to align their actions toward shared goals whilst managing conflicts, resource contention, and information asymmetries. They include market-based mechanisms, hierarchical authority structures, consensus protocols, and shared state systems.
