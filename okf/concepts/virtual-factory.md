# Virtual Factory

A physically accurate digital twin representation of a manufacturing facility that enables modelling, simulation, analysis, and optimisation of production processes, resources, and operations without physical prototypes or pilot plants. It integrates IoT sensor data, AI-driven analytics, and real-time bidirectional synchronisation; platforms such as NVIDIA Omniverse (OpenUSD) and Siemens Tecnomatix are primary implementation environments. Virtual factories are a core Industry 4.0 concept supporting predictive maintenance, layout optimisation, and safe robotics training.
