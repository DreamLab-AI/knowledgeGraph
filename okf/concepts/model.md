# Model

In artificial intelligence, a Model is a mathematical or computational structure learned from data that encodes a mapping from inputs to outputs (or latent representations). Models range from classical machine learning models (linear, tree-based) to deep neural networks, generative models, and large language models, and constitute the core artefact produced by training and consumed during inference.
