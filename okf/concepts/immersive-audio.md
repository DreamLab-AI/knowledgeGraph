# Immersive Audio

Immersive audio refers to audio reproduction and processing technologies that create a convincing three-dimensional sound field, enveloping the listener in a spatially accurate sonic environment. It encompasses object-based audio formats, binaural rendering, ambisonics, and head-related transfer function (HRTF) personalisation, enabling the perception of sounds positioned above, below, and around the listener rather than confined to a flat stereo plane.
