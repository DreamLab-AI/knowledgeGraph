# Algorithm

A finite, deterministic sequence of instructions or rules that solves a computational problem or performs a transformation on data. In AI and blockchain contexts algorithms encompass learning procedures, consensus rules, cryptographic primitives, and optimisation methods that underpin intelligent systems and distributed ledgers.
