# Accessible Experience

An Accessible Experience is a virtual environment or application delivering equivalent functionality and engagement to users with disabilities through multimodal access pathways, assistive technology integration, and Accessible Design principles, ensuring feature parity across visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive modalities.
