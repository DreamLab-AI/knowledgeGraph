# Pose Tracking

Pose tracking is the continuous estimation of the position and orientation (6-DoF pose) of a device, user, or body over time. In spatial computing it underpins head, hand, and controller tracking for AR and VR, fusing camera, IMU, and depth data at low latency. Accurate, drift-free pose tracking is essential for stable, comfortable immersive experiences.
