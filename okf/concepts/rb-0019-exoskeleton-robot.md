# rb 0019 exoskeleton robot

An exoskeleton robot is a wearable robotic device that attaches to the human body and augments, supports, or replaces the wearer's physical capabilities. Exoskeletons may be powered (actuated) or passive (spring-and-damper based), and are used for physical rehabilitation after stroke or spinal cord injury, industrial worker fatigue reduction, and military load-carrying augmentation. Safety is paramount because the robot shares the mechanical structure with a human; ISO 13482 governs safety requirements for personal care robots including exoskeletons.
