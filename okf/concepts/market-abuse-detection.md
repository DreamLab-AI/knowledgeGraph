# Market Abuse Detection

Market abuse detection is the use of surveillance systems and analytics to identify illegal trading behaviours such as insider dealing, spoofing, layering, and price manipulation. It analyses order-book activity, trade patterns, and communications to flag anomalies for compliance review and regulatory reporting. It is a core obligation under regimes such as the EU Market Abuse Regulation and is increasingly powered by machine learning.
