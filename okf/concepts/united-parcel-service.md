# United Parcel Service

United Parcel Service (UPS) is a package delivery and logistics company. Its own site says that the story of UPS, which it calls the world's largest package delivery company, began more than a century ago with a $100 loan to jumpstart a tiny messenger service, and that it serves 200+ countries and territories.[^1] The European Commission described UPS in a 2013 decision as one of the world's largest logistics providers that operates mainly in the small package delivery, freight transport and contract logistics sectors.[^2]
