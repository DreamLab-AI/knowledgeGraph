# BSI Germany

The Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI), or Federal Office for Information Security, is Germany's national authority for cybersecurity, responsible for protecting digital infrastructure, certifying IT products, and developing security standards. It functions as a central advisory, regulatory, and technical body for both public administration and the private sector across Germany and the European Union.
