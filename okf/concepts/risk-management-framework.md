# Risk Management Framework

A Risk Management Framework is a structured methodology for identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring risks to an organisation's objectives, assets, or systems. It provides a repeatable process and governance structure that links risk appetite, control selection, and assurance activities into a coherent programme aligned with recognised standards such as ISO 31000, NIST RMF, or the NIST AI RMF.
