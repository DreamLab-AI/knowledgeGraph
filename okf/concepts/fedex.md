# FedEx

FedEx is an express and parcel carrier and logistics company headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee. The European Commission described FedEx in a 2016 decision as providing customers and businesses worldwide with a broad portfolio of transportation, e-commerce and business services, listed on the New York Stock Exchange and headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee.[^1]
