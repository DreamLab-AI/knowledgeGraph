# OpenRAIL

OpenRAIL (Responsible AI License) is a family of model licenses that grant broad permission to use and distribute AI models while imposing use-based behavioral restrictions to prevent harmful applications. Unlike traditional open-source licenses, it couples openness with enforceable conditions prohibiting specified misuse such as discrimination or disinformation. OpenRAIL has become a common licensing framework for openly released foundation models and their training artifacts.
