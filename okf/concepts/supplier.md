# Supplier

A supplier is an organisation or individual that supplies a product or service to a buyer under an agreement. NIST's glossary gives the systems-engineering wording, taken from ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288: an “Organization or an individual that enters into an agreement with the acquirer for the supply of a product or service”.[^1]
