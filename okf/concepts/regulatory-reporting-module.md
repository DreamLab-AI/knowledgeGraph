# Regulatory Reporting Module

A regulatory reporting module is a self-contained software component that collects, formats, and dispatches the disclosures an entity owes to its regulators. It encapsulates jurisdiction-specific schemas, validation rules, and submission channels so they can be reused across systems. As a discrete subsystem it plugs into compliance-monitoring and tax-compliance pipelines to produce audit-ready outputs.
