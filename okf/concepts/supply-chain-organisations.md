# Supply Chain Organisations

Supply chain organisations are the persons and business organisations that take part in a supply chain. The Industrial Ontologies Foundry calls such a party a supply chain agent and defines it as a “person or business organization who supplies material products or commercial services to other agents in a supply chain”, with a manufacturer in a supply chain and a carrier in a supply chain as examples.[^1]
