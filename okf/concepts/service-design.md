# Service Design

Service design is the activity of planning and orchestrating the people, infrastructure, communication, and touchpoints of a service to improve its quality and the interaction between a provider and its users. It takes a holistic, end-to-end view that spans front-stage customer experiences and the back-stage processes and systems that enable them. Service designers use tools such as journey maps and service blueprints to align organisational capabilities with user needs across every channel.
