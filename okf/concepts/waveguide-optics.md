# Waveguide Optics

Waveguide optics is the optical-engineering approach used in augmented- and mixed-reality displays to channel projected light from a microdisplay into the user's eye via total internal reflection in a thin transparent substrate. Diffractive, reflective, or holographic gratings couple light in and out, enabling compact, see-through eyewear with a wide eyebox. It is the dominant optical architecture for consumer AR glasses, trading manufacturing complexity for form factor and transparency.
