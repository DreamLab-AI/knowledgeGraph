# Robotics Process

A Robotics Process encompasses the systematic procedures, methodologies, and workflows for robot development, deployment, operation, and maintenance, spanning requirements engineering, system design, hardware-software integration, verification and validation, commissioning, and lifecycle management. Agile and iterative development methods accommodate uncertainty and enable rapid prototyping; simulation-driven approaches using digital twins support virtual commissioning. Compliance with process standards such as ISO 9001 and IEC 61508 is required for safety-critical robotic applications.
