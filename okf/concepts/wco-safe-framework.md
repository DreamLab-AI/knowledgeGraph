# WCO SAFE Framework

The WCO SAFE Framework of Standards to Secure and Facilitate Global Trade is an instrument that WCO Members adopted at the World Customs Organization (WCO) Council Sessions in Brussels in June 2005, setting standards for supply chain security and facilitation.[^1]
