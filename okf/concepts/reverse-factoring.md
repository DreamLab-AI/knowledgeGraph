# Reverse Factoring

Reverse factoring is a buyer-led financing arrangement in which suppliers can obtain early payment on invoices that the buyer has approved. The Global Supply Chain Finance Forum's standard terminology calls it payables finance and lists reverse factoring and approved payables finance among the synonyms.[^1][^2]
