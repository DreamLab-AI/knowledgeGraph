# Immersive Display Infrastructure

The hardware and software infrastructure used to install, calibrate, and drive immersive visual displays in XR, digital signage, and spatial computing contexts. This encompasses projection mapping, light-field displays, LED walls, head-mounted display optics, and the rendering pipelines that feed them.
