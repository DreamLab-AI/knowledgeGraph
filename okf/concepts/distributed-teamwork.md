# Distributed Teamwork

Distributed teamwork is the coordinated effort of team members who are geographically dispersed and collaborate primarily through digital and immersive tools rather than co-located presence. It addresses the challenges of time-zone separation, reduced informal contact, and asynchronous decision-making by combining shared artefacts, communication norms, and presence technologies. In spatial computing, distributed teamwork increasingly leverages virtual environments that recreate co-presence and shared spatial context for remote collaborators.
