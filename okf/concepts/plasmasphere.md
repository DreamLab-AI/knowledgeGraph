# Plasmasphere

A reservoir of very cold, fairly dense plasma of ionospheric origin fills the inner magnetosphere and co-rotates with Earth as the plasmasphere.[^1][^2] NASA describes it as made of low-energy particles that drift up from the ionosphere, and as carried around with the planet by the magnetic field lines.[^1][^3] ESA calls it the innermost part of the magnetosphere, a doughnut-shaped region of cold plasma whose toroidal shape is determined by Earth’s magnetic field.[^2]
