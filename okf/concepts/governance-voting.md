# Governance Voting

Governance voting is the mechanism by which holders of governance tokens or members of a decentralised organisation cast votes on proposals to direct a protocol's decisions. Voting power is typically proportional to token holdings or delegated stake, and outcomes can execute automatically on-chain. It is the primary means by which token holders exercise collective control over protocol changes.
