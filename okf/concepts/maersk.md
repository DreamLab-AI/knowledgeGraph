# Maersk

Maersk, formally A.P. Moller - Maersk, is a transport and logistics group headquartered in Copenhagen whose core activity is container liner shipping. The European Commission described it in 2022 as an integrated transport and logistics company headquartered in Copenhagen with worldwide activities, whose core activities comprise container liner shipping through its subsidiaries Maersk A/S, SeaLand and Hamburg Süd, container terminal services, inland transportation, inland services, supply chain management services, harbour towage and reefer container manufacturing.[^1]
