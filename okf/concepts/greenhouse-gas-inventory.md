# Greenhouse Gas Inventory

A greenhouse gas inventory is a systematic, periodic accounting of the greenhouse gas emissions and removals attributable to an organisation, activity or jurisdiction over a defined period, expressed in carbon-dioxide-equivalent units. It categorises emissions by source and by scope, applies emission factors to activity data, and forms the quantitative basis for target-setting, reporting and reduction strategies. Inventories underpin compliance, disclosure and progress toward net-zero commitments.
