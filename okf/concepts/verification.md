# VERIFICATION

Verification is the process of confirming that information, claims, and digital artefacts meet specified standards for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with constraints through cryptographic proofs, rule-based validation, consensus mechanisms, and user acceptance testing. Within metaverse and blockchain contexts it encompasses transaction authenticity, smart contract correctness, ontology schema conformance, and digital asset provenance, establishing trust between stakeholders in decentralised systems.
