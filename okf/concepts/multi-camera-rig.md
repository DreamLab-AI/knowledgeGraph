# Multi-Camera Rig

A multi-camera rig is a physical arrangement of multiple synchronised cameras positioned around a subject or scene to capture it from many viewpoints simultaneously. It is the primary capture hardware for volumetric capture and volumetric video pipelines, where images from all cameras at a given instant are fused into a single 3D or free-viewpoint representation. Camera count, placement and synchronisation precision directly determine the achievable reconstruction quality.
