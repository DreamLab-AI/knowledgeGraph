# ICVCM Core Carbon Principles

The ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (CCPs) are a set of ten threshold standards established by the Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market to define what constitutes a high-quality carbon credit. They cover governance, emissions impact, and sustainable development dimensions, requiring that credits be real, additional, quantified, permanent, independently verified, and contribute positively to sustainable development goals without causing harm to local communities or ecosystems.
