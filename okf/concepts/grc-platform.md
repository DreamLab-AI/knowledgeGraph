# Grc Platform

A GRC platform is an integrated software system that unifies governance, risk management and compliance activities across an organisation. It maintains a common library of controls, policies and risks, maps them to regulatory frameworks, and automates assessment, evidence collection and reporting. By consolidating these functions it gives leadership a consistent view of risk posture and control effectiveness while reducing duplicated manual effort.
