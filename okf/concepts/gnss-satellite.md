# GNSS Satellite

A GNSS satellite is a spacecraft in the space segment of a satellite-navigation system. It broadcasts precisely timed, coded radio signals and navigation data that allow a receiver to estimate signal travel time and the satellite's position. Several satellites, a ground control segment and a user receiver are required for a complete navigation solution.[^1]
