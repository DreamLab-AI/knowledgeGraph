# Ethereum Improvement Proposal

An Ethereum Improvement Proposal (EIP) is a formal design document that describes a proposed change to the Ethereum protocol, its standards, or its processes, providing the technical specification and rationale for community review. EIPs are categorised into Core proposals affecting consensus, Networking proposals, Interface proposals, and Application-level standards known as ERCs. They follow a defined lifecycle from Draft through Review, Last Call, and Final, serving as the canonical mechanism for coordinating decentralised protocol evolution.
