# Ai Generated Content

AI Generated Content (AIGC) refers to text, images, audio, video, code, or other media artefacts produced autonomously or semi-autonomously by artificial intelligence systems, particularly large generative models. The content may be indistinguishable from human-created output and spans creative, informational, and functional applications. Its proliferation raises governance questions around attribution, intellectual property, misinformation, and authenticity verification. Regulatory frameworks are evolving to mandate disclosure and provenance tracking of AI-generated material.
