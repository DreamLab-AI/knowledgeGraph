# Logistics Service Provider

A logistics service provider is a business that provides logistics services to other organisations. The Industrial Ontologies Foundry gives the Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals wording for the role: “Any business which provides logistics services”, adding that it “Includes those businesses typically referred to as 3PL, 4PL, LLP, etc” and that the services “may include provisioning, transport, warehousing, packaging, etc”.[^1]
