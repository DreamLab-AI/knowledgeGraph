# Economic Layer

The Economic Layer is the abstraction level at which concrete economic mechanisms are implemented in blockchain and distributed systems, encompassing fee markets, reward distribution formulae, automated market makers, staking yield calculations, and game-theoretic incentive structures. It is distinct from conceptual economic theory in that it specifies exact parameters, formulas, and on-chain behaviours.
