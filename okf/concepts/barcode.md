# Barcode

A barcode is a machine-readable optical representation of data that encodes information in the widths and spacings of parallel lines (one-dimensional) or in patterns of dots, squares and other geometric shapes (two-dimensional). Barcodes are scanned by optical readers or cameras to retrieve an identifier that links a physical item to a record in an information system. They underpin automatic identification and data capture in retail, logistics and inventory control.
