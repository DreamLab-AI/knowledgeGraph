# Gesture Recognition

Gesture recognition is the computational process of identifying and interpreting human physical movements—typically of the hands, arms, head, or full body—as meaningful symbolic inputs to a computing system, enabling touchless, natural interaction paradigms. The field encompasses multiple sensing modalities including optical cameras, depth sensors, inertial measurement units, and electromagnetic field detectors, combined with machine learning pipelines that extract skeletal or surface features and classify movement sequences into semantic categories. Gesture recognition underpins human-computer interaction in extended reality, robotics, automotive interfaces, and accessibility technology, replacing or augmenting physical input devices with embodied motion vocabulary.
