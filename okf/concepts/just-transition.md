# Just Transition

A just transition is a framework for shifting economies toward low-carbon and sustainable production while ensuring that the burdens and benefits of that shift are distributed fairly. It seeks to protect workers, communities and developing regions dependent on carbon-intensive industries through retraining, social support and inclusive planning. The concept links climate policy with social equity and labour rights.
