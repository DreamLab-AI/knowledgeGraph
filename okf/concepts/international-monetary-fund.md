# International Monetary Fund

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) is an international financial institution, established at the 1944 Bretton Woods conference, whose mandate is to promote global monetary cooperation, exchange-rate stability, balanced trade and financial stability among its member countries. It provides macroeconomic surveillance, technical assistance and conditional lending to members facing balance-of-payments difficulties, and manages the Special Drawing Rights reserve asset. As a central pillar of the international monetary system, the IMF shapes monetary policy advice and crisis response worldwide.
