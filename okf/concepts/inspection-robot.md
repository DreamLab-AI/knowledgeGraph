# Inspection Robot

A specialised robotic system designed to autonomously examine physical structures, components, or manufactured products for defects, faults, or deviations from specification. Inspection robots combine computer vision, sensor fusion, and AI-driven anomaly detection to replace or augment manual quality-assurance processes in hazardous or high-throughput environments.
