# Initial Public Offering

An initial public offering (IPO) is the process by which a privately held company first sells its shares to the public on a regulated stock exchange, transitioning into a publicly traded entity. It is intermediated by investment banks that underwrite the issue, set a price range, and allocate shares to institutional and retail investors under securities regulation. In blockchain discourse the IPO serves as the regulated, equity-based reference point against which token-based fundraising mechanisms are contrasted.
