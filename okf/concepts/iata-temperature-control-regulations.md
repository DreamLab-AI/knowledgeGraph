# IATA Temperature Control Regulations

The IATA Temperature Control Regulations (TCR) are a manual published by the International Air Transport Association that sets out requirements and standards for the transport and handling of pharmaceutical products, with temperature-sensitive shipments by air as its subject.[^1][^2]
