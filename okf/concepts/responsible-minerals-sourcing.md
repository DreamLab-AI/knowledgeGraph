# Responsible Minerals Sourcing

Responsible minerals sourcing is the practice of buying minerals under supply chain due diligence designed to avoid financing armed conflict and serious abuses, and the clearest legal statement in the sources opened here is the European Union's Conflict Minerals Regulation.[^1]
