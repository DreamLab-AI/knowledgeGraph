# Virtual Labor

Work performed in virtual environments that generates economic value, spanning content creation, service provision, virtual construction, platform governance, and social labour. Virtual labour is often platform-dependent, contract-based, and remunerated via virtual currency or revenue-sharing models, raising significant questions around labour rights, taxation of cross-border virtual income, and platform exploitation.
