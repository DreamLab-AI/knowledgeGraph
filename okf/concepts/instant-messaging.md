# Instant Messaging

Instant messaging is a form of real-time, text-based direct communication between two or more participants over a network. Messages are delivered with minimal latency, enabling synchronous or near-synchronous conversation without the formality of email. It serves as a foundational channel for informal coordination and rapid decision-making in distributed teams.
