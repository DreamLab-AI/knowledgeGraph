# Delivered Duty Paid

Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) is an Incoterms 2020 rule under which the seller delivers the goods when they are placed at the buyer's disposal, cleared for import, on the arriving means of transport ready for unloading at the named place of destination.[^1]
