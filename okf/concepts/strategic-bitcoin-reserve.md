# Strategic Bitcoin Reserve

A strategic Bitcoin reserve is a holding of bitcoin maintained by a state, corporation, or institution as a long-term store of value and hedge against fiat debasement, analogous to gold or foreign-currency reserves. Proponents argue that bitcoin's fixed supply and censorship resistance make it a credible reserve asset, while critics cite volatility and custody risk. The concept gained prominence as governments and large treasuries began formal accumulation programmes.
