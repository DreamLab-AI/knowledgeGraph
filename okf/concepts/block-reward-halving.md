# Block Reward Halving

The block reward halving is the protocol rule in Bitcoin and similar proof-of-work chains that cuts the newly minted coin subsidy paid to miners in half at fixed block intervals, roughly every four years for Bitcoin. This geometric reduction enforces a predictable, disinflationary issuance schedule that converges on a fixed total supply of 21 million bitcoin. Halvings are central to Bitcoin's scarcity narrative and have historically been focal points for market and security-budget analysis.
