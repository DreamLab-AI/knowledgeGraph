# National Ai Strategy

A national AI strategy is a government-led policy framework setting a country's vision, priorities and coordinated actions for developing, adopting and governing artificial intelligence. It typically addresses research and innovation funding, talent and skills, data and compute infrastructure, ethical and regulatory guardrails, public-sector adoption and economic competitiveness. Such strategies align ministries, industry and academia around shared objectives and measurable outcomes.
