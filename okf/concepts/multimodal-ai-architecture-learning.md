# Multimodal Learning

Multimodal learning is a sub-field of machine learning concerned with building models that can process, align, and reason over data from two or more sensory modalities — such as text, images, audio, video, and structured data — within a unified representation space. Models trained multimodally acquire richer, grounded representations than unimodal counterparts by exploiting cross-modal correlations and complementarity.
