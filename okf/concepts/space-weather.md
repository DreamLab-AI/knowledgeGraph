# Space Weather

Space weather describes changing conditions from the Sun, through the solar wind, to Earth's magnetosphere, ionosphere and upper atmosphere. It includes solar flares, energetic particles, coronal mass ejections and changes in the background solar wind. Their effects form a connected physical system, but they travel at different speeds and do not produce a single, uniform warning time.[^1]
