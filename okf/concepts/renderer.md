# Renderer

A renderer is the software or hardware component that converts a scene description, such as geometry, materials, lights, or a markup specification, into a final image or visual output. Renderers range from real-time rasterisers and ray tracers to text-and-diagram engines that draw from declarative source. The choice of renderer determines visual fidelity, performance, and the formats a system can produce.
