# Cryptoasset Regulatory Framework

Crypto Legal from DigiSoc examines the legal, regulatory, and governance landscape surrounding cryptocurrency and digital assets within digital society contexts. It covers UK and EU regulatory positions on crypto-assets as property, stablecoin frameworks, AML/KYC obligations, and the intersection of Web3 economics with metaverse commerce. The field addresses how legacy legal systems must adapt to support low-friction, globally scalable value exchange in virtual social spaces.
