# Free Trade Zone

A free trade zone is an area where goods that are brought in are generally regarded, as far as import duties and taxes go, as outside the customs territory; the Revised Kyoto Convention calls it a free zone, and the United States calls the equivalent a foreign-trade zone.[^1][^2]
