# Cross-Border Data Transfer

Cross-border data transfer is the movement of personal or regulated data across national jurisdictions, governed by data-protection laws that restrict where and how such data may be processed. Compliance mechanisms include adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules, and localisation requirements. It is a central concern of privacy regimes such as GDPR and various Asia-Pacific frameworks.
