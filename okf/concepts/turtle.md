# Turtle

Turtle (Terse RDF Triple Language) is a W3C-standardised serialisation syntax for expressing RDF graphs in a compact, human-readable plain-text form. It extends Notation3 (N3) by providing a concise prefix-based shorthand for IRIs, support for blank nodes, and literal datatypes, enabling structured linked-data documents to be authored and exchanged without the verbosity of RDF/XML.
