# Economic Mechanism

An Economic Mechanism is a structured system of incentives, penalties, and resource allocation rules designed to align participant behavior with desired network outcomes, drawing from mechanism design theory to create game-theoretic environments where rational self-interested actors produce collectively beneficial outcomes without central coordination.
