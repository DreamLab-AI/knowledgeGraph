# Electric Propulsion

Electric propulsion uses electrical power to accelerate a propellant. It produces much higher exhaust velocity than most chemical systems, so a spacecraft can obtain a given total impulse with less propellant. The trade is low thrust: manoeuvres that take minutes with chemical propulsion may require days or months with an electric thruster.[^1]
