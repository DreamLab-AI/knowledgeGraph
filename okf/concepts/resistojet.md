# Resistojet

A resistojet is an electrothermal thruster. Electrical current heats a solid resistive element or heat exchanger; propellant takes up that heat and expands through a nozzle to produce thrust.[^1][^3] The propellant need not be ionised. This separates the mechanism from Hall-effect and gridded ion thrusters, which accelerate charged particles with fields, and from an arcjet, which transfers electrical energy through an arc in the gas.
