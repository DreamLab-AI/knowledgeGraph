# Notified Body

An independent third-party conformity assessment organisation formally designated by a national notifying authority under the EU AI Act (Articles 29–39) to audit and certify high-risk AI systems — particularly biometric identification and product-safety-component systems — against technical documentation, quality management, and harmonised standards requirements.
