# rb 0075 range finder

A range finder is an exteroceptive sensor that measures the distance between a robot and surrounding objects or surfaces by emitting a signal (laser, ultrasonic, or infrared) and timing its return. Range finders are fundamental to obstacle detection, safety zone monitoring, and environment mapping in mobile and collaborative robots.
