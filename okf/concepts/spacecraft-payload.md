# Spacecraft Payload

A spacecraft payload is the equipment that performs the mission's intended scientific, commercial, operational or technology-demonstration function. The spacecraft bus supports it with structure, power, thermal control, pointing, communications and data handling.[^1] The boundary depends on mission purpose: a star tracker is usually a bus attitude sensor, but can be flown as an experimental payload when testing the tracker itself.
