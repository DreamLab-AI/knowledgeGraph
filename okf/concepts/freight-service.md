# Freight Service

A freight service is a commercial offering to carry freight or to arrange its carriage for customers; the sources separate carriers, which carry goods for remuneration, from intermediaries such as freight forwarders and brokers, which organise transport on behalf of customers.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
