# Portable Identity

Portable identity is the property of a digital identity that allows it to be carried by its owner across services, platforms, and trust domains without being locked to any single provider. It relies on user-held credentials and identifiers that any relying party can verify cryptographically. Portability is a core goal of decentralised and self-sovereign identity systems.
