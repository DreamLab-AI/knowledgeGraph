# Data Redundancy

Data redundancy is the deliberate storage of duplicate or recoverable copies of data so that information survives hardware failure, corruption or loss. Implemented through replication, mirroring, RAID and erasure or forward-error-correction codes, it trades extra capacity for durability and availability. It is a foundational reliability technique in storage systems and distributed infrastructure.
