# Fixed Supply Monetary Policy

A fixed supply monetary policy is a rule embedded in a cryptocurrency protocol that caps the total quantity of units that will ever exist, removing discretionary issuance. By making the maximum supply algorithmically predetermined and credibly enforced by consensus, it produces verifiable scarcity and predictable issuance schedules. Bitcoin's 21-million-coin cap is the canonical example, underpinning narratives of digital scarcity and store of value.
