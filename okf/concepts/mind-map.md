# Mind Map

A mind map is a diagram that organises information hierarchically around a central concept, with branches radiating to related topics and sub-topics. It is used for brainstorming, note-taking, and knowledge organisation, leveraging spatial and associative structure to aid recall and ideation. Digital mind-mapping tools support collaborative editing and links to external resources.
