# Safety (OECD)

Under the OECD AI Principles (Principle 1.4, updated 2024), AI systems must operate without posing unacceptable risks of physical, psychological, or environmental harm. This requires lifecycle-spanning hazard analysis, fail-safe design, continuous monitoring, and incident response mechanisms, with responsibilities shared between providers who design safety in and deployers who maintain operational safety.
