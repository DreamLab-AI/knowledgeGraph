# Case Management

A business-process discipline for handling unstructured, knowledge-intensive work items — 'cases' — whose resolution path cannot be fully predetermined, such as compliance investigations, insurance claims, legal matters, and customer disputes. Each case aggregates documents, tasks, participants, deadlines, and an audit trail around a single subject, and progresses through investigation, decision, and resolution stages guided by worker judgement rather than a rigid predefined flow.
