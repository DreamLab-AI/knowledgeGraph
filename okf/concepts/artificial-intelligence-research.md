# Artificial Intelligence Research

Artificial intelligence research is the scientific and engineering discipline concerned with understanding and building systems that exhibit intelligent behaviour, including perception, reasoning, learning, planning, and language. It spans theoretical foundations, algorithmic innovation, and empirical evaluation, and feeds discoveries into subfields such as machine learning, computer vision, and natural language processing. The field advances through hypothesis-driven inquiry using the scientific method, publication and peer review, standardised benchmarking, and reproducible experimentation, generating knowledge that flows into applied AI systems and deployed products across industry and government.
