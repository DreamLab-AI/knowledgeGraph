# CubeSat

A CubeSat is a [[Small Satellite|small-satellite]] form factor built around standardised units, written as **U**. Under the CubeSat Design Specification Revision 14.1, a 1U spacecraft is based on a ten-centimetre cube and may have a mass up to two kilograms; larger configurations combine units into forms such as 2U, 3U, 6U or 12U.[^1] The standardised spacecraft-to-deployer interface is the defining feature. CubeSat is therefore not a synonym for every nanosatellite.
