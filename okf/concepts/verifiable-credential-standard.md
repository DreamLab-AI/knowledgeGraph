# Verifiable Credential Standard

The W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model specification defines a structured, cryptographically verifiable format for expressing claims about subjects. Credentials are issued by a trusted issuer, held by a subject, and verified by relying parties using digital signatures and Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs), enabling portable, privacy-preserving identity across metaverse and web platforms without centralised databases.
