# Cognitive Psychology

Cognitive psychology is the scientific discipline that investigates the internal mental processes underlying intelligent behaviour, including perception, attention, memory, language, problem-solving, reasoning, and decision-making, treating the mind as an information-processing system and using controlled experiments, computational models, and neuroimaging to reveal its representations and operations.
