# TNFD

The Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) is a global framework that guides organisations in assessing, reporting, and acting on nature- and biodiversity-related risks, dependencies, and impacts. Modelled on the climate-focused TCFD, it provides recommended disclosures structured around governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics so that capital can be redirected toward nature-positive outcomes.
