# Rights Protection

Rights protection encompasses the institutional, legal, and technical mechanisms through which individual and collective entitlements—civil liberties, digital rights, property rights, privacy rights, and human rights—are identified, defended, and remedied when violated. It spans constitutional and statutory safeguards, judicial and administrative enforcement, technical privacy-by-design measures, and advocacy infrastructure. In digital and AI contexts, rights protection addresses algorithmic discrimination, surveillance overreach, automated decision-making without human review, and the erosion of informational self-determination. Effective rights protection requires both upstream prevention and downstream accountability pathways.
