# Mechanical Interface

A mechanical interface is the standardised physical coupling between two components that defines their geometric fit, fastening, and load transfer. In robotics it specifies how an end-effector or tool attaches to a robot wrist or actuator, governing alignment, rigidity, and quick-change capability. Well-defined mechanical interfaces enable modularity and interchangeability of subsystems.
