# AI Upscaling and Super-Resolution

AI Upscaling and Super-Resolution is a family of deep learning techniques that synthesise plausible high-frequency detail when enlarging images or video frames beyond their native resolution. Methods range from convolutional neural network regressors (SRCNN, ESRGAN) and generative adversarial approaches to diffusion-based reconstruction, enabling applications from consumer photography enhancement to real-time game upscaling (DLSS, FSR) and medical imaging.
