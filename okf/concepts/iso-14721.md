# Iso 14721

ISO 14721 is the international standard defining the Open Archival Information System (OAIS) reference model, a conceptual framework for the long-term preservation of digital information. It specifies the functional entities, information packages and responsibilities of an archive committed to preserving content and making it accessible to a designated community over time. As the foundational model for digital preservation, it underpins repositories, audit and certification frameworks, and cultural heritage and scientific data archives.
