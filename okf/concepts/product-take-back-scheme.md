# Product Take-Back Scheme

A product take-back scheme is an arrangement under which producers, distributors or retailers accept end-of-life or used products from their holders and manage their recovery or disposal, and the sources opened here are both EU legislation, which describes such arrangements without defining the term.[^1][^2]
