# Contract Manufacturer

A contract manufacturer is a business that carries out manufacturing operations for another business. The United Nations ISIC describes the arrangement as one in which units sell goods or services under their own name but the actual production is carried out fully or in part by others through specific contractual arrangements.[^1]
