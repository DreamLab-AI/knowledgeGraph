# Dynamic Pricing

Dynamic Pricing is a strategy in which the price of a good or service is adjusted in real time based on demand, supply, competitor prices, inventory, and customer signals. It is typically driven by machine-learning models that forecast willingness to pay and optimise revenue or other objectives. Common in e-commerce, ride-hailing, travel, and logistics, it relies on continuous data feeds and automated decisioning.
