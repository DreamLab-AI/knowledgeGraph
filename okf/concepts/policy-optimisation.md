# Policy Optimisation

Policy optimisation is the process of searching over a parameterised space of decision-making policies to maximise a scalar objective, typically a cumulative reward signal in a reinforcement learning context. It encompasses both gradient-based methods such as proximal policy optimisation and gradient-free approaches such as evolutionary strategies, applied across discrete and continuous action spaces.
