# Propellant

Propellant is material carried or acquired by a vehicle and expelled to produce thrust. In a chemical rocket it also stores or participates in energy release. Cold-gas systems discharge pressurised fluid, while electric thrusters use electrical or magnetic fields to accelerate reaction mass.[^1][^3] “Fuel” names a combustible or energy-bearing constituent and is therefore not a synonym for every propellant.
