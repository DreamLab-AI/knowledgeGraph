# Third Party Certification

Third-party certification is a conformity-assessment arrangement in which an independent accredited body, with no commercial interest in the outcome, evaluates a product, system or process against a defined standard and issues a certificate attesting to conformity. Its independence distinguishes it from first-party self-declaration and second-party customer assessment, lending the result greater credibility and market trust. In artificial intelligence it is increasingly proposed as a mechanism to assure that systems meet safety, fairness and governance requirements.
