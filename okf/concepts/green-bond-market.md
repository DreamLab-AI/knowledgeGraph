# Green Bond Market

The green bond market is the segment of fixed-income capital markets in which debt instruments are issued specifically to finance projects with environmental or climate benefits, such as renewable energy, clean transport, or sustainable infrastructure. Proceeds are ring-fenced and reported against recognised frameworks like the ICMA Green Bond Principles. It channels institutional capital toward decarbonisation while offering investors verifiable sustainability exposure.
