# Hard Money

Hard money refers to a form of money whose supply is difficult to expand, giving it a high stock-to-flow ratio and resistance to debasement. Historically embodied by gold, the concept extends to assets like Bitcoin whose issuance is algorithmically constrained. Hard money is valued as a store of value because its scarcity cannot be diluted by discretionary issuance.
