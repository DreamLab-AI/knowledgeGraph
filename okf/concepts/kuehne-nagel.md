# Kuehne+Nagel

Kuehne+Nagel is a freight forwarding and logistics group headquartered in Switzerland. Its own site says that in 1890 August Kuehne and Friedrich Nagel founded a freight forwarding company in Bremen, Germany, and that Kuehne+Nagel is today headquartered in Switzerland with a presence all over the world.[^1]
