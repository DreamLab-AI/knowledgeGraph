# Remittances

Remittances are cross-border transfers of money, typically sent by migrant workers to family in their home country, representing a major global flow of funds to developing economies. Traditional rails impose high fees and slow settlement, which cryptocurrencies and the Lightning Network aim to reduce. As a use case, remittances anchor much of the argument for Bitcoin and stablecoins as practical money.
