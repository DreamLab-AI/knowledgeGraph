# Meta AI

Meta AI is the artificial intelligence research and applied AI division of Meta Platforms, Inc., encompassing the Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) laboratory for long-horizon academic research and multiple applied AI teams that embed machine-learning capabilities into Meta products including Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. The division is best known for its open-weight large language model series LLaMA, the zero-shot image segmentation model Segment Anything (SAM), the multimodal joint-embedding model ImageBind, and early contributions to the broader AI ecosystem including the PyTorch deep-learning framework and fastText word embeddings. Meta AI's open-weight release strategy has materially shaped the open-source AI ecosystem by enabling community fine-tuning, on-device deployment, and proliferation of derivative models without dependence on proprietary inference APIs.
