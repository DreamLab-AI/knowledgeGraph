# ZK-STARKs

Zero-Knowledge Scalable Transparent Arguments of Knowledge: a cryptographic proof system enabling verification of computations without revealing underlying data. Unlike ZK-SNARKs, STARKs require no trusted setup, are conjectured to be quantum-resistant, and scale efficiently for large witness sizes, making them foundational for Layer 2 blockchain rollups and privacy-preserving computation.
