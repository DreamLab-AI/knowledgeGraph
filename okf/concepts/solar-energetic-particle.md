# Solar Energetic Particle

A solar energetic particle is a charged particle, most importantly a proton, accelerated in the solar atmosphere to a large fraction of the speed of light by a large-scale magnetic eruption.[^1] NOAA describes such eruptions as often producing a coronal mass ejection and an associated solar flare. When the fast protons reach Earth they can penetrate the magnetosphere that shields the planet from lower energy charged particles.[^1]
