# Radiology AI

Radiology AI comprises artificial intelligence systems designed for automated interpretation and quantitative analysis of radiological imaging modalities — X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound. These systems perform lesion detection, organ segmentation, and structured reporting at radiologist-level accuracy, integrating with PACS workflows and validated through prospective clinical trials.
