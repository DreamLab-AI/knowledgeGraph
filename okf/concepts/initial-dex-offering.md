# Initial Dex Offering

An initial DEX offering (IDO) is a token fundraising and distribution model in which a new token is launched directly on a decentralised exchange, with liquidity provided into an automated market maker pool so trading begins immediately. Unlike centralised or regulated offerings, an IDO is permissionless and settled on-chain through smart contracts, often using launchpads that manage allocation and anti-bot measures. It contrasts with the initial coin offering and the regulated initial public offering by emphasising immediate liquidity and decentralised access.
