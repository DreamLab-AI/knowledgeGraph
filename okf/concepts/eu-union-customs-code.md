# EU Union Customs Code

The EU Union Customs Code (UCC) is Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013, which lays down the general rules and procedures applicable to goods brought into or taken out of the customs territory of the Union.[^1]
