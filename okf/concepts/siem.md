# SIEM

SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) is a platform that aggregates, normalises, and correlates log and event data from across an organisation's IT estate to detect threats and support compliance. It combines real-time alerting, anomaly detection, and historical search with dashboards and audit reporting. It is a foundational tool of security operations centres and regulatory compliance programmes.
