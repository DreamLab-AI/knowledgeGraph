# Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is a family of wireless networking technologies based on the IEEE 802.11 standards that allow devices to connect to a local area network and the internet without physical cabling. It is governed and certified by the Wi-Fi Alliance and operates primarily in the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz radio bands.
