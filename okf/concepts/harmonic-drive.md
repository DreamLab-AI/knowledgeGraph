# Harmonic Drive

A harmonic drive is a strain-wave gearing mechanism that achieves very high reduction ratios in a compact, lightweight package with near-zero backlash. It uses a flexible spline deformed by an elliptical wave generator to mesh with a rigid outer gear, transmitting motion with high precision. Harmonic drives are widely used in robot joints, where accuracy and torque density are critical.
