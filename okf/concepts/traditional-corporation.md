# Traditional Corporation

A traditional corporation is a centralised legal entity owned by shareholders and directed by a board, granting limited liability and a hierarchical management structure under company law. Decision-making authority and capital are concentrated, with accountability mediated through fiduciary duties and regulatory oversight. It is the conventional organisational form against which decentralised autonomous organisations are contrasted.
