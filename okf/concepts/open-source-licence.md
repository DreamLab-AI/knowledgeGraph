# Open Source Licence

An open source licence is a legal instrument that grants users the rights to use, study, modify, and redistribute software source code, subject to conditions defined by the licence. Such licences fall broadly into permissive families, which impose few obligations, and copyleft families, which require derivative works to remain under compatible terms. Recognition typically follows the Open Source Initiative's definition, and licences are identified by standard SPDX identifiers to support automated compliance.
