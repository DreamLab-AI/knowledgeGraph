# Virtual Museum Tour

An immersive digital experience that enables users to explore museum collections, exhibitions, and cultural heritage sites through VR, AR, 360-degree imagery, or interactive 3D environments, providing global access to art and artefacts regardless of physical location. Delivery formats range from photogrammetry-reconstructed 3D galleries (Smithsonian, British Museum) to fully navigable VR spaces, with platforms such as Google Arts and Culture and Matterport enabling broad public access across device types.
