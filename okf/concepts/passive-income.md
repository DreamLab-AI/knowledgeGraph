# Passive Income

Passive Income is earnings generated from an asset or position with minimal ongoing active effort from the holder, as distinct from wages earned through direct labour. In decentralised finance it typically refers to yield accrued from lending, liquidity provision, or staking, where deployed capital earns returns algorithmically rather than through active trading. The yield rate and risk profile depend on the underlying protocol mechanism generating the return.
