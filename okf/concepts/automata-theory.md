# Automata Theory

Automata theory is the branch of theoretical computer science that studies abstract computing machines (automata) and the classes of formal languages they can recognise. It classifies machines such as finite-state automata, pushdown automata, and Turing machines by their computational power, establishing a hierarchy that defines what problems are decidable. The theory provides the formal foundations for compiler design, regular expressions, protocol verification, and model checking.
