# Ownership Token

An Ownership Token is a blockchain token that represents and confers title to an underlying asset, whether digital or real-world. Typically implemented as a non-fungible or semi-fungible token, holding it in a wallet constitutes provable control of the represented item, and transferring it transfers ownership. Ownership tokens enable trading, collateralization, and programmable rights over digital goods and tokenized property.
