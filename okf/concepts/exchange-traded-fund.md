# Exchange-Traded Fund

An exchange-traded fund (ETF) is a pooled investment vehicle whose shares trade on a stock exchange throughout the day at market prices. It typically tracks an index, asset, or basket of assets and uses a create-redeem mechanism with authorised participants to keep its price close to net asset value. ETFs combine the diversification of funds with the intraday liquidity and accessibility of listed securities.
