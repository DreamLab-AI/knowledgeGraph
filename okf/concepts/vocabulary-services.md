# Vocabulary Services

Technical infrastructure and APIs that provide programmatic access to controlled vocabularies, taxonomies, and ontologies, enabling applications to retrieve concept definitions, navigate broader/narrower hierarchies, validate terminology, and perform cross-vocabulary mapping for consistent semantic interpretation across platforms. Common interfaces include SPARQL endpoints, SKOS APIs, and REST services backed by platforms such as PoolParty or Metaphactory.
