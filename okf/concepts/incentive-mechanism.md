# Incentive Mechanism

An incentive mechanism is a designed system of rewards and penalties that aligns the self-interested behaviour of participants with a desired collective outcome. In distributed systems and blockchain networks it uses tokens, fees and slashing to motivate honest participation, resource provision and protocol-conformant behaviour. Drawing on game theory and mechanism design, it makes cooperation the rational strategy even among mutually distrustful actors.
