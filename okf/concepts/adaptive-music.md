# Adaptive Music

Adaptive music is a compositional and audio-engineering approach in which a musical soundtrack modifies its structure, instrumentation, tempo, or emotional register dynamically in response to real-time contextual signals such as player actions, narrative state, or environmental parameters within interactive media. It replaces the static looping of pre-composed tracks with a system that maintains musical coherence while reflecting the moment-to-moment state of an interactive experience. The technique is foundational to game audio design and is increasingly applied in extended reality, therapeutic, and generative AI contexts.
