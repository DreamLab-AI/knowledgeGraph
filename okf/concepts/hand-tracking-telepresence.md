# Hand Tracking Telepresence

Hand Tracking Telepresence is the real-time capture, transmission, and rendering of hand pose and gesture data from a remote participant into a local or shared virtual environment. It enables natural gestural communication — such as pointing, waving, or manipulating shared objects — between geographically distributed collaborators. By preserving expressive hand movements, this technology significantly enriches the non-verbal communication channel in remote collaboration systems.
