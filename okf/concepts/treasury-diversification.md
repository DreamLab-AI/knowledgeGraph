# Treasury Diversification

Treasury diversification is the practice of spreading an organisation's or DAO's reserves across multiple asset types, such as stablecoins, native tokens, blue-chip crypto, and yield-bearing positions, to reduce concentration risk and stabilise runway. It mitigates the volatility and correlation exposure that arises when a treasury holds predominantly its own governance token.
