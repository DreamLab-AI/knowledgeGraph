# Crypto Regulation

Crypto regulation is the body of laws and supervisory rules governing the issuance, trading, custody and use of crypto-assets, including authorisation of service providers, disclosure requirements, market conduct rules, client-asset custody standards, and anti-money-laundering obligations.
