# GHG Protocol Corporate Standard

The GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard is the most widely used international framework for measuring and reporting an organisation's greenhouse-gas emissions. Developed by the World Resources Institute and the World Business Council for Sustainable Development, it organises emissions into Scope 1 (direct), Scope 2 (purchased energy), and Scope 3 (value-chain) categories, establishing consistent boundaries, accounting principles, and disclosure rules. It underpins corporate climate reporting, science-based targets, and most national and regional emissions-disclosure regulations.
