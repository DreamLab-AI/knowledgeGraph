# MIMO Antenna

A MIMO (multiple-input multiple-output) antenna is an array of multiple transmit and receive elements that exploits spatial multiplexing to send several independent data streams over the same frequency channel. By leveraging multipath propagation, it increases spectral efficiency and link reliability without additional bandwidth, and is foundational to Wi-Fi and 4G/5G physical layers. Massive MIMO scales this to dozens or hundreds of elements with beamforming.
