# Workspace Templates

Workspace Templates are pre-configured layouts, page structures, or project scaffolds that teams can instantiate to quickly start a new collaboration session with consistent conventions. They encode best practices for recurring activities such as sprint planning, meeting notes, or design reviews, reducing setup time for distributed teams. Templates are a standard feature in platforms such as Notion, Confluence, and Miro.
