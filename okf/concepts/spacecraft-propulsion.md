# Spacecraft Propulsion

Spacecraft propulsion produces controlled forces or impulses after launch. It changes a spacecraft's orbit or trajectory, maintains its assigned orbit, supports attitude control, avoids collisions, performs rendezvous and provides an end-of-life disposal manoeuvre. The propulsion architecture is chosen with the mission because thrust, total impulse, manoeuvre duration, power, mass, volume, safety and lifetime cannot be optimised independently.[^1]
