# Computational Creativity

Computational creativity is the subfield of artificial intelligence concerned with building systems that exhibit behaviours regarded as creative, such as generating novel and valuable artefacts in art, music, design, or narrative. It studies both the algorithmic generation of original output and the evaluation of novelty, surprise, and value. The field informs generative models, procedural content systems, and AI-assisted creative tooling.
