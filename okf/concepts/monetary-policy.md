# Monetary Policy

Monetary policy is the set of decisions made by a monetary authority — traditionally a central bank — to manage the supply of money, credit conditions, and interest rates in order to achieve macroeconomic objectives such as price stability, full employment, and sustainable economic growth. In traditional finance it encompasses tools such as open market operations, reserve requirements, discount-rate setting, and unconventional measures including quantitative easing and forward guidance. In decentralised systems and blockchain protocols, monetary policy is encoded algorithmically into protocol rules that govern token emission schedules, inflation rates, halving events, and burn mechanisms, removing discretionary human intervention and replacing it with transparent, auditable on-chain governance.
