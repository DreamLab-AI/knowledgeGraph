# Accessibility Standards

Accessibility Standards for the metaverse and extended reality (XR) encompass guidelines, technical specifications, and design principles that ensure virtual environments, interfaces, and interactions are usable by people with diverse disabilities including visual, auditory, physical, cognitive, and neurological impairments. Key bodies include the W3C XR Accessibility User Requirements (XRAUR), WCAG 2.2/3.0 adaptations, ADA digital interpretations, and inclusive immersion frameworks covering multi-modal input, hardware ergonomics, and spatial interface design.
