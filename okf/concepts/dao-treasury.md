# Dao Treasury

A DAO treasury is the pool of on-chain assets collectively owned and governed by a decentralised autonomous organisation, typically held in smart contracts and controlled through member governance. It funds operations, grants and incentives, with disbursements authorised by token-weighted votes and executed by multisignature or programmatic controls. Treasury management balances runway, diversification and accountability without a central custodian.
