# Digital Forensics

Digital forensics is the scientific discipline concerned with the identification, preservation, extraction, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence from computing systems, networks, storage media, and connected devices in a manner that is legally admissible and reproducible. It applies structured methodologies — including write-blocked acquisition, cryptographic hashing for evidence integrity, and chain-of-custody documentation — to support criminal investigations, civil litigation, incident response, and regulatory compliance inquiries. Sub-disciplines include network forensics, mobile forensics, memory forensics, and cloud forensics, each requiring specialist tools and legal frameworks adapted to the peculiarities of the evidence medium. Practitioner outputs must survive rigorous judicial scrutiny, compelling both technical rigour and defensible, auditable process documentation.
