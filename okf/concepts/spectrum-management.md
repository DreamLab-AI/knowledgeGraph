# Spectrum Management

Spectrum management is the regulatory and technical process of allocating, assigning, and coordinating use of the radio-frequency spectrum to minimise interference and maximise societal and economic value. It encompasses licensing, frequency planning, enforcement, and the design of sharing arrangements between competing wireless services. Spectrum management is administered by national regulators and harmonised internationally to enable cross-border wireless communication.
