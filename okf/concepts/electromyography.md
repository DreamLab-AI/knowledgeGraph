# Electromyography

Electromyography is the measurement of the electrical activity produced by skeletal muscles, captured by surface or intramuscular electrodes. The resulting signals reflect motor-unit recruitment and can be processed to infer intended movement and muscular effort. In robotics it provides a biosignal interface for prosthetic control, exoskeleton actuation and gesture recognition.
