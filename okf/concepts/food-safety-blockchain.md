# Food Safety Blockchain

Food Safety Blockchain is the application of distributed ledger technology to establish immutable, multi-party farm-to-fork audit trails that enable near-instantaneous contamination source identification and surgical product recalls. By recording each Critical Tracking Event—harvest, cooling, packing, shipment, receipt—as a cryptographically linked on-chain entry, the approach reduces trace-back from days to seconds, minimises the volume of food unnecessarily recalled, and provides regulators and consumers with verifiable provenance data.
