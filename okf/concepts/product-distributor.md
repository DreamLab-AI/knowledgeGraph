# Product Distributor

A product distributor is a business that buys products, usually in quantity from manufacturers, and resells them without making them itself. APICS, quoted in the Industrial Ontologies Foundry ontology, calls it a “business that does not manufacture its own products, but purchases and resells these products” and that “usually maintains a finished goods inventory”.[^1]
