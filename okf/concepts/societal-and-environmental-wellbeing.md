# Societal and Environmental Wellbeing

Societal and Environmental Wellbeing is a trustworthiness dimension ensuring AI systems consider broader impacts on communities, environments, democratic processes, and human flourishing beyond immediate functional objectives. It promotes sustainable development, social cohesion, and alignment with the UN Sustainable Development Goals across the AI lifecycle.
