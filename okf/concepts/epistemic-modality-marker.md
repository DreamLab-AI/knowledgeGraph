# Epistemic Modality Marker

An Epistemic Modality Marker is a lexical or grammatical element — such as the modal verbs may, might, could and must, or hedges like perhaps and possibly — that encodes a speaker's degree of certainty or commitment toward a proposition. Detecting and generating such markers is central to hedge detection, uncertainty-aware natural language processing and calibrated dialogue systems.
