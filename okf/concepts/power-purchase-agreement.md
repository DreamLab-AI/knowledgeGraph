# Power Purchase Agreement

A Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) is a long-term contract between an electricity generator and a buyer that fixes the price, volume and delivery terms for electrical energy over a defined period. PPAs underpin the financing of generation assets, particularly renewable plants, by guaranteeing a stable revenue stream that de-risks capital investment. They allocate market, volume and curtailment risk between producer and offtaker and may be physical or virtual (financial).
