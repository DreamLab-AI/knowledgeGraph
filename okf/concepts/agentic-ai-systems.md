# Agentic AI Systems

Agentic AI systems are AI systems that autonomously plan and execute multi-step tasks by invoking tools, calling external APIs, and adapting their strategy based on intermediate results, rather than producing a single response to a single prompt. They combine a reasoning model with memory, tool use, and control loops that allow sustained operation toward a goal with limited human intervention. Agentic AI systems are a major focus of frontier AI development and a key axis of competition among AI labs.
