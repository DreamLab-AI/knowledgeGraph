# ETSI Domain Identity and Trust

An ETSI governance domain establishing identity management, authentication, and trust infrastructure for virtual environments. It integrates decentralised identity models, verifiable credentials, biometric authentication, zero-trust architecture, and multi-party trust platforms aligned with eIDAS 2.0 and ISO/IEC 24760, enabling secure, privacy-respecting identity assurance across distributed metaverse platforms and consortia.
