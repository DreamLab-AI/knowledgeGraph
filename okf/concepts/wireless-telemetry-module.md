# Wireless Telemetry Module

A wireless telemetry module is an embedded hardware subsystem that acquires sensor measurements and transmits them over a radio link to a remote receiver without a wired connection. It combines signal conditioning, analogue-to-digital conversion, a microcontroller, and a low-power radio, and is used where wired tethering is impractical, such as implanted devices or remote field sensors. Power efficiency, antenna design, and link reliability are its central engineering constraints.
