# World Customs Organization

The World Customs Organization (WCO) is an international organisation that develops the Harmonized System and promotes customs co-operation. The European Commission says that the WCO plays an important role in promoting international customs co-operation and addressing new challenges for customs and trade, and that the European Union joined it from 1 July 2007 on an interim basis, with rights and obligations akin to those of WCO Members.[^1]
