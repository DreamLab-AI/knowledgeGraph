# Digital Identity Verification

Digital Identity Verification is the remote, automated process of confirming that a person's claimed identity corresponds to a real individual and that the claimant is who they say they are, typically through document analysis, biometric matching, liveness detection, and database cross-referencing. It underpins Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance, onboarding workflows, and access control in digital services.
