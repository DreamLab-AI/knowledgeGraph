# Securities Exchange Act 1934

The Securities Exchange Act of 1934 is the US federal law that governs secondary trading of securities and created the Securities and Exchange Commission. It establishes registration, disclosure, and anti-fraud requirements for exchanges, brokers, and listed companies. It is a foundational reference for determining how digital assets and investment products are regulated in the United States.
