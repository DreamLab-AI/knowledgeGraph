# Stablecoins on Bitcoin

Stablecoins on Bitcoin are price-stable tokens issued and transferred on Bitcoin-based protocols such as Taproot Assets, Liquid, or RGB, rather than on smart-contract chains like Ethereum. They aim to bring dollar-denominated value transfer to the Bitcoin ecosystem, leveraging Bitcoin's settlement security and, where combined with the Lightning Network, near-instant low-cost payments. They extend Bitcoin's utility from a single monetary asset toward a broader settlement layer for fiat-pegged value.
