# Secure Storage

Secure storage is the persistence of data with protections for confidentiality, integrity, and controlled access throughout its lifecycle. It combines encryption at rest, access control, tamper-evidence, and key management, sometimes anchored in hardware security modules or trusted execution environments. It is essential where stored records must remain trustworthy, such as audit trails and chains of custody.
