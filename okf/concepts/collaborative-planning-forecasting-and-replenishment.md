# Collaborative Planning, Forecasting and Replenishment

Collaborative Planning, Forecasting and Replenishment (CPFR) is a business model in which trading partners jointly plan, forecast and replenish stock, developed in the United States by VICS in 1997 and published in guidelines in 1998.[^1]
