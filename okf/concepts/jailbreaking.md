# Jailbreaking

Jailbreaking of large language models (LLMs) is the practice of crafting inputs—prompts, instruction sequences, encoded payloads, or multi-turn conversational strategies—that cause a model to produce outputs that circumvent its safety training, content policies, or alignment objectives.
