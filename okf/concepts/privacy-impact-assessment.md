# Privacy Impact Assessment

A structured evaluation process identifying, analysing, and mitigating privacy risks associated with data processing activities, particularly AI systems handling personal information. Mandated by GDPR Article 35 for high-risk processing, it covers necessity assessment, risk identification, severity evaluation, and documentation of technical and organisational controls.
