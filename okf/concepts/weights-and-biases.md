# Weights and Biases

Weights & Biases (W&B) is a machine-learning experiment-tracking and MLOps platform that logs metrics, hyperparameters, model checkpoints, datasets, and system telemetry to enable reproducible and comparable training runs. It provides dashboards, artifact versioning, hyperparameter sweeps, and model-registry features that integrate with common training frameworks. W&B is widely adopted for managing and visualising the lifecycle of deep-learning experiments.
