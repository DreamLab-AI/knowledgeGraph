# Ice Core

An ice core is a cylindrical sample drilled from accumulated snow and ice that preserves trapped air bubbles from earlier periods.[^1][^2] It is a physical sample of an ice mass, not a mass of ice in the landscape, so it differs from the glacier-type pages in this batch.
