# Compliance Carbon Market

A compliance carbon market is a regulated market in which emitters covered by a mandatory cap must surrender allowances or credits equal to their emissions, created under law to enforce climate targets. Prices are set by the supply of capped allowances and demand from regulated entities, creating a financial incentive to abate. It stands in contrast to voluntary carbon markets, where participation and credit purchase are discretionary.
