# Auditability

Auditability is the property of a system, process, or record that allows an independent party to examine and verify its actions, decisions, and state against expected behaviour or policy. It depends on complete, tamper-evident records — logs, audit trails, and provenance — that establish who did what, when, and why, enabling accountability and compliance review. Auditability is a foundational governance requirement for trustworthy financial, blockchain, and AI systems.
