# Trustworthy AI Framework

Comprehensive governance and standards framework establishing principles, requirements, and assessment processes to ensure AI systems are lawful, ethical, and technically robust throughout their lifecycle. Defined by the EU High-Level Expert Group on AI and formalised in the EU AI Act, it implements a risk-based approach with seven trustworthiness dimensions covering human oversight, safety, privacy, transparency, fairness, societal wellbeing, and accountability.
