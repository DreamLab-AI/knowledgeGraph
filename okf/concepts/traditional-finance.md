# Traditional Finance

Traditional Finance (TradFi) denotes the incumbent financial system comprising central banks, commercial and investment banks, securities exchanges, clearing houses, custodians, and regulated intermediaries that operate under sovereign legal frameworks. It relies on trusted third parties to record ownership, settle transactions, and extend credit, in contrast to decentralised or blockchain-native alternatives. Its architecture encompasses fractional-reserve banking, fiat currency issuance, and multilateral settlement rails such as SWIFT and RTGS systems. TradFi institutions are subject to prudential regulation, capital adequacy requirements (Basel III/IV), and conduct oversight by bodies such as the SEC, FCA, and ECB.
