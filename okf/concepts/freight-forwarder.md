# Freight Forwarder

A freight forwarder is a person or business that arranges the carriage of goods on behalf of a shipper or consignee, usually without operating the transport itself. The Industrial Ontologies Foundry records the GS1 wording: “The party that arranges the carriage of goods including connected services and/or associated formalities on behalf of the shipper (consignor) or consignee”.[^1]
