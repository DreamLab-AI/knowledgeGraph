# Agent Relationship

An agent relationship is a relationship between two or more agents.[^1] It is declared as `AgentRelationship` in the delegation module, and visionGraph places it under the AI Research Area hub and as part of [[Multi-Agent System]]. The schema.org mapping aligns it with `schema:Role`.[^2]
