# Transaction Ledger

A transaction ledger is an ordered, append-only record of value transfers or state changes that serves as the authoritative history of activity within a financial or blockchain system. Each entry captures the parties, amounts, and timing of a transaction, and in distributed settings the ledger is cryptographically chained and replicated to guarantee integrity and auditability.
