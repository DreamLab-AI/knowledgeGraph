# Mentions and Notifications

Mentions and notifications are mechanisms that direct a participant's attention to specific messages, tasks, or events by referencing their identity with an @-symbol or equivalent trigger. They bridge asynchronous communication gaps by alerting individuals even when they are not actively monitoring a channel. Effective notification design balances urgency with focus protection to avoid alert fatigue in distributed teams.
