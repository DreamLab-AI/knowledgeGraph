# Third-Party Logistics Provider

A third-party logistics provider (3PL) is an outside firm to which a company outsources part of its logistics activities. The European Commission says that enterprises often outsource their logistics activities to third party logistics providers, and estimates that long-term contractual relationships, which it calls contract logistics, make up 16% of total global logistics.[^1]
