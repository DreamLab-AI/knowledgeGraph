# Document Comments

Document Comments are annotations anchored to specific sections of a shared document that allow collaborators to ask questions, suggest changes, or provide feedback without altering the primary content. They support threaded replies, @mentions, and resolution states so discussions are traceable and actionable. This asynchronous feedback mechanism is essential for distributed review workflows across time zones.
