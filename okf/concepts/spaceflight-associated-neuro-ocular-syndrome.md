# Spaceflight-associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome

Spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS) is the name NASA gives to a collection of eye and brain changes seen in astronauts, including optic nerve swelling, folds in the retina and flattening of the back of the eye.[^1][^2]
