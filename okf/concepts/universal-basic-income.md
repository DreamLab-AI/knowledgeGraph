# Universal Basic Income

Universal basic income (UBI) is an economic policy under which all members of a population receive a regular, unconditional cash payment from the state or another institution, independent of employment status or means. It is debated as a mechanism to reduce poverty, buffer labour displacement from automation and AI, and address structural inequality. Proponents view it as a redistributive instrument while critics question its fiscal sustainability and incentive effects.
