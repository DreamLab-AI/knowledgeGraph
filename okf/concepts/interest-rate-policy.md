# Interest Rate Policy

Interest rate policy is the use of a central bank's control over short-term policy interest rates to steer borrowing costs, credit demand and inflation. By raising or lowering its target rate, the central bank influences money-market rates, lending, investment and consumption throughout the economy. It is the primary monetary-policy instrument in most modern economies, transmitted through the financial system to output and prices.
