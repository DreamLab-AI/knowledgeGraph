# ConsenSys

ConsenSys is a Brooklyn-founded blockchain software company, established in 2014 by Ethereum co-founder Joseph Lubin, that builds developer tooling, infrastructure, and end-user products for the Ethereum ecosystem. Its product portfolio spans the MetaMask browser wallet, the Infura node-as-a-service API network, the Truffle and Hardhat-adjacent development suite, and the Linea zkEVM Layer 2 network. ConsenSys acts as a principal driver of Ethereum protocol adoption by providing the production-grade infrastructure on which the majority of decentralised application front-ends depend. The company also engages in enterprise blockchain consulting and contributes to Ethereum standards through active participation in the Ethereum Foundation and EIP processes.
