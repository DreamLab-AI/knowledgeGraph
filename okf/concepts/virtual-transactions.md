# Virtual Transactions

Virtual transactions are exchanges of value conducted entirely within digital environments, transferring virtual currency, tokens or digital goods between participants. They power in-world economies in games and metaverse platforms and rely on digital payment systems and currencies for settlement. Their integrity depends on secure accounting, fraud prevention and clear ownership semantics.
