# User-Generated Content

User-generated content (UGC) is media and information created and published by the users of a platform rather than by its operators or professional producers. It encompasses text, images, video, 3D assets, reviews, and virtual-world artefacts contributed by a community. UGC drives engagement and network effects on social and metaverse platforms while creating moderation, rights, and quality-control obligations.
