# Predictive Modelling

Predictive modelling is the practice of building statistical or machine-learning models that estimate unknown or future outcomes from observed input features. It spans data preparation, feature engineering, model training and validation, and is evaluated on its generalisation to unseen data. Predictive modelling powers forecasting, classification and risk assessment across applied domains.
