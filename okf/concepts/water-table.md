# Water Table

Water table is the upper surface of the saturated zone.[^1][^2] The USGS glossary words it as the top water surface of an unconfined aquifer at atmospheric pressure, and the British Geological Survey as the surface of a body of unconfined groundwater at which the pressure equals that of the atmosphere.[^3][^2]
