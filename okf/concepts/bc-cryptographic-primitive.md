# Cryptographic Primitive (Blockchain)

Taxonomy hub for the foundational cryptographic building blocks underpinning blockchain systems, including hash functions, digital signatures, asymmetric encryption, zero-knowledge proofs, and cryptographic commitments. These primitives collectively provide the security properties of integrity, authenticity, and privacy that make distributed ledger technology trustworthy.
