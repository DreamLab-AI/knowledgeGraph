# Cross Border Authentication

Cross border authentication is the verification of a person or entity's identity across national or jurisdictional boundaries, so that credentials issued in one country are trusted and accepted in another. It depends on interoperable identity schemes, mutual-recognition agreements and trust frameworks that reconcile differing legal and technical regimes. The capability enables seamless access to services, regulated transactions and travel without re-establishing identity from scratch in each jurisdiction.
