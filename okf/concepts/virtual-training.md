# Virtual Training

The use of VR, AR, and simulation technologies to create immersive learning experiences for workforce development, enabling employees to practise skills, rehearse high-stakes scenarios, and learn procedures in safe, repeatable virtual environments with measurable outcomes. Empirical evidence — including Walmart's programme covering over one million employees — demonstrates 75% material retention and up to 4x focus improvement over e-learning. The global market was valued at USD 380 billion in 2024 and is projected to reach USD 1.42 trillion by 2034.
