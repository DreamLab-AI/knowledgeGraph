# Standards Conformance Testing

Testing or verification activities that determine whether a process, product, or service complies with the requirements of a specification, technical standard, contract, or regulation. Conformance testing verifies that implementations faithfully meet specified requirements through structured test suites, automated harnesses, and formal certification regimes — and is distinct from functional testing in that it judges compliance against an external normative reference rather than internal design intent.
