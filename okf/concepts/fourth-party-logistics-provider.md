# Fourth-Party Logistics Provider

A fourth-party logistics provider (4PL), also called a lead logistics provider (LLP), is usually described as an integrator that coordinates the other logistics providers serving a client. Maersk describes a traditional 4PL provider as an orchestrator that oversees all transport management, taking ownership of the coordination and management of a variety of 2PL and 3PL players in an end-to-end logistics offering, as well as coordinating the vendors.[^1]
