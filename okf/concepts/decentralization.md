# Decentralization

The architectural property of distributing control, data storage, and decision-making across many independent network participants rather than concentrating them in a single authority. In blockchain systems, decentralisation is achieved through open participation, distributed consensus, and cryptographic enforcement of rules, providing censorship resistance, fault tolerance, and permissionless access.
