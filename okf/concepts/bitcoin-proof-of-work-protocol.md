# Bitcoin Proof-of-Work Protocol

Bitcoin is the first decentralised peer-to-peer electronic cash system, introduced by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto in a 2008 white paper and launched in January 2009. It operates on a public, permissionless blockchain secured by proof-of-work consensus, with a fixed supply cap of 21 million BTC enforced by its protocol. Bitcoin is simultaneously a payment network, a monetary asset, and the foundational reference implementation for the broader cryptocurrency and blockchain industry.
