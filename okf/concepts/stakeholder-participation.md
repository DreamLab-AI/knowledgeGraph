# Stakeholder Participation

Stakeholder participation is the structured involvement of affected and interested parties in the deliberation, design, and decision-making processes that govern a system, organisation, or protocol. It encompasses mechanisms for consultation, representation, and shared authority that aim to surface diverse interests and confer legitimacy on collective decisions. In governance contexts it is a precondition for accountable, inclusive, and durable outcomes.
