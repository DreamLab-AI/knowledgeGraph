# Seoul Declaration

The Seoul Declaration is an international AI-safety commitment adopted at the 2024 AI Seoul Summit, in which participating states and companies affirmed principles of safe, innovative, and inclusive AI and pledged cooperation on frontier-model risk. It built on the Bletchley Declaration and advanced commitments to risk thresholds and safety frameworks for advanced AI systems. It is a governance instrument shaping Asia-Pacific and global AI regulation.
