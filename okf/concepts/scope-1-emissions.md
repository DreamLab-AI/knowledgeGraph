# Scope 1 Emissions

Scope 1 emissions are direct greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from sources that are owned or controlled by a reporting organisation, as defined by the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard. They include combustion of fuels in owned vehicles, boilers, and furnaces; process emissions from chemical or physical reactions in production; and fugitive emissions from refrigerant leaks, methane from waste operations, and similar inadvertent releases. Scope 1 emissions are denominated in CO2-equivalent (CO2e) tonnes and represent the most controllable category of an organisation's carbon footprint.
