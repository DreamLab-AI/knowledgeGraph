# Message Reactions

Message reactions are lightweight emoji or icon-based acknowledgements that participants attach to individual messages without composing a reply. They convey sentiment, agreement, or status in a low-friction manner that reduces notification noise. In distributed teams, reactions serve as asynchronous micro-feedback signals that keep conversation threads concise.
