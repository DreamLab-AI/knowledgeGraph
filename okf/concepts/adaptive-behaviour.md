# Adaptive Behaviour

Adaptive behaviour refers to the capacity of an agent, system, or organism to modify its actions, strategies, or responses in real time based on feedback from its environment, internal state, or prior experience. In computational and robotics contexts, it describes dynamic adjustment of policies or control laws to optimise performance under changing conditions. The concept underpins reinforcement learning, autonomous agents, and self-organising systems.
