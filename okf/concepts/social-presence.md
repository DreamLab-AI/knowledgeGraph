# Social Presence

Subjective experience of feeling connected to and aware of other people in a mediated communication environment, fostering social interactions and relationships. Social presence is heightened by richer media, avatar fidelity, spatial audio, and shared immersive spaces, and is a key quality metric for telecollaboration and virtual world platforms.
