# Control Law

A control law is the mathematical rule that maps measured system state and reference signals to the control inputs applied to a plant. It is the algorithmic core of a feedback controller, expressed as functions such as PID, state feedback, or impedance laws. The choice of control law determines stability, tracking accuracy, and disturbance rejection of the closed-loop system.
