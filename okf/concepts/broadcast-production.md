# Broadcast Production

Broadcast Production is the end-to-end process of creating, processing, and distributing audio-visual content for transmission to audiences over television, radio, or internet broadcast channels, encompassing pre-production planning, live or recorded acquisition, signal processing, editing, graphics integration, encoding, and playout to distribution networks. It operates under stringent real-time constraints and technical quality standards defined by regulatory and industry bodies.
