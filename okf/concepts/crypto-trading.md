# Crypto Trading

Crypto trading is the buying and selling of cryptocurrencies and digital assets for profit, conducted on centralised exchanges, decentralised exchanges, and over-the-counter desks. It spans spot, margin, derivatives, and algorithmic strategies, with prices driven by liquidity, sentiment, and on-chain activity. It is a primary use case and liquidity source for the broader crypto-asset market.
