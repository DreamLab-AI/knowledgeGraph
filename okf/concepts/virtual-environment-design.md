# Virtual Environment Design

Virtual Environment Design is the multidisciplinary practice of creating the spatial, visual, auditory, and interactive properties of computer-generated environments intended for exploration or habitation through virtual reality, augmented reality, or mixed reality systems. It integrates principles from architecture, industrial design, game design, cognitive psychology, and human-computer interaction to produce environments that are usable, compelling, and safe, balancing perceptual fidelity, performance constraints, and the unique ergonomics of immersive display hardware.
