# Creative Expression

Creative expression is the human activity of producing original artistic, narrative, or aesthetic artefacts to communicate ideas, emotions, or identity. In virtual and open-world environments it manifests as user-generated content, world-building, avatar customisation, and emergent play. It is a primary driver of engagement and value in persistent metaverse spaces.
