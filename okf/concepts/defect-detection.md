# Defect Detection

Defect Detection is the automated identification of flaws, anomalies, or deviations from specification in products, materials, or processes, typically performed using machine vision and machine-learning models on images or sensor data. It localises and classifies defects such as cracks, scratches, contamination, dimensional errors, or assembly faults, often in real time on a production line. Modern systems combine high-resolution imaging, deep-learning object detection and anomaly detection, and feedback to robotic or process controls. Defect detection underpins quality assurance and industrial inspection, reducing waste and ensuring consistency.
