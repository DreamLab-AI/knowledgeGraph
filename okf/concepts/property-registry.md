# Property Registry

Blockchain-based land title recording systems employing immutable distributed ledgers, cryptographic signatures, and timestamp verification to create tamper-proof property ownership records, reduce fraud, and accelerate transaction processing from 30–90 days to 72 hours. Dubai processed 188,000+ transactions worth AED 625 billion in 2024; Georgia was the first country to adopt blockchain land administration at national scale.
