# Impact Metrics

Impact metrics are quantitative and qualitative indicators used to measure the social, environmental, or economic outcomes produced by an intervention, project, or organisation. They translate a theory of change into measurable outputs, outcomes, and longer-term impacts, enabling comparison, accountability, and decision-making. Standardised metric catalogues such as IRIS+ promote comparability across programmes.
