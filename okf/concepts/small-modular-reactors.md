# Small Modular Reactors

Small modular reactors (SMRs) are nuclear fission reactors with an electrical output typically under 300 MWe whose components are factory-fabricated as standardised modules and assembled on site. Their smaller scale, passive safety features, and modular construction aim to lower capital cost and deployment time relative to gigawatt-scale plants. They are an emerging low-carbon power-infrastructure option, including for energy-intensive data centres.
