# Safety Integrity Level

Safety Integrity Level (SIL) is a discrete measure of the risk-reduction provided by a safety function, defined by the IEC 61508 family of standards on four levels (SIL 1 to SIL 4). Each level corresponds to a target probability of dangerous failure on demand or per hour. SIL is used to specify, design, and verify that safety-related control systems achieve a tolerable level of risk.
