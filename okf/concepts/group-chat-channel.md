# Group Chat Channel

A group chat channel is a persistent, named messaging space where multiple participants can send and receive messages collectively. Channels are typically organised by topic, project, or team, providing a shared context for ongoing conversation and record-keeping. They support both synchronous and asynchronous communication patterns within distributed organisations.
