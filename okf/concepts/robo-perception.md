# Perception and Sensing

Taxonomy hub for all robotic perception and environmental sensing technologies, covering sensor hardware (LiDAR, cameras, IMUs, tactile sensors), sensor-fusion algorithms, SLAM, object detection, and visual odometry. These capabilities provide robots with the situational awareness required for autonomous navigation and manipulation.
