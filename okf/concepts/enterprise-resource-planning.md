# Enterprise Resource Planning

Enterprise resource planning (ERP) is an integrated category of business software that unifies core organisational processes - finance, procurement, manufacturing, inventory, human resources and sales - around a shared data model and central database. By recording transactions once and propagating them across modules, ERP systems eliminate data silos and give a consistent, real-time view of operations. Modern ERP is frequently delivered as a cloud service and extended with analytics and supply-chain capabilities.
