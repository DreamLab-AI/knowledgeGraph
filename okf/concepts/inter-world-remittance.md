# Inter-world Remittance

The transfer of economic value, digital currency, or tokenised assets across distinct virtual worlds, metaverse platforms, or between virtual and physical economies via blockchain bridges, smart-contract escrow, or centralised exchange mechanisms. Inter-world remittance enables labour mobility, cross-platform portfolio management, and arbitrage within interconnected digital economies.
