# Financial Regulation

Financial Regulation comprises the body of statutory rules, supervisory frameworks, licencing regimes, and oversight institutions that govern the conduct of financial markets, intermediaries, and participants. It encompasses prudential regulation (capital adequacy, liquidity, systemic risk), conduct regulation (market integrity, consumer protection, disclosure), and increasingly the oversight of digital asset ecosystems including tokens, stablecoins, and decentralised protocols. Regulatory mandates are administered by national and supranational authorities that establish binding standards, enforce compliance, and coordinate across jurisdictions to address cross-border capital flows, financial crime, and systemic interconnectedness.
