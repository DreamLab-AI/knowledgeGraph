# De Fi Services

Decentralised Finance (DeFi) Services are financial products and protocols—including lending, borrowing, automated market-making, and derivatives—implemented as smart contracts on programmable blockchains without centralised intermediaries. DeFi Services enable permissionless, non-custodial access to financial primitives and are a key component of virtual economies within metaverse and spatial computing platforms.
