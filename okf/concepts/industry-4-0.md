# Industry 4.0

Industry 4.0 denotes the fourth industrial revolution, characterised by the integration of cyber-physical systems, industrial Internet of Things, cloud computing, advanced robotics, and artificial intelligence into manufacturing and supply chain operations to create smart, autonomous, and highly flexible production environments. Originating from a German government initiative, it represents a strategic framework for digitalising industrial production through real-time data exchange, machine intelligence, and networked automation.
