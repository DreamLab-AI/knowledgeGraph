# Privacy-Preserving Compliance

Privacy-preserving compliance is the use of cryptographic and selective-disclosure techniques to satisfy regulatory requirements such as KYC, AML, and audit without exposing the underlying personal data. It lets a party prove a fact (e.g. age, jurisdiction, sanction-list status) to a regulator or counterparty while revealing nothing more. It is central to reconciling decentralised identity and digital-currency systems with financial regulation.
