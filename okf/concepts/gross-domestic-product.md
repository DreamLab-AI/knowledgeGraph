# Gross Domestic Product

Gross domestic product (GDP) is the total monetary value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders over a given period. It is the principal aggregate measure of economic activity, computed via the production, income, or expenditure approaches, and is reported in nominal and real (inflation-adjusted) terms. GDP underpins growth measurement, fiscal and monetary policy, and cross-country comparison.
