# Regulatory Enforcement

Regulatory enforcement is the set of actions through which a competent authority compels compliance with laws and rules and sanctions those who breach them. It encompasses investigation, supervision, market surveillance, and the imposition of remedies such as fines, restitution, licence revocation or criminal referral. In financial markets it deters misconduct, protects consumers and investors, and sustains confidence in the integrity of the system.
