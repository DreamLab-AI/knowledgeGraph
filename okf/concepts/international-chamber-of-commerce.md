# International Chamber of Commerce

The International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) is a business organisation that publishes the Incoterms rules. It describes itself as the world's business organisation and as the institutional representative of 45 million companies in more than 170 countries, making it easier for businesses to trade internationally.[^1]
