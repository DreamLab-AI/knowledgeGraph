# Public Consultation

Public consultation is a formal process by which government bodies or organisations solicit input from affected individuals and groups before finalising a policy, regulation, or major decision, typically through published proposals, comment periods, and hearings. It is a specific, structured mechanism of stakeholder participation intended to widen legitimacy and surface concerns that internal deliberation might miss. Public consultation outcomes are commonly published alongside a summary of how the feedback shaped the final decision.
