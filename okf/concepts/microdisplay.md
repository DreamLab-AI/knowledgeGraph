# Microdisplay

A microdisplay is a miniature display panel, typically under an inch diagonal, with very high pixel density, used in near-eye optical systems such as headsets, viewfinders and projectors. Built on technologies including micro-OLED, liquid-crystal-on-silicon (LCoS) and microLED, it produces a small bright image that magnifying or waveguide optics expand into a large virtual image for the eye. Microdisplays are a critical enabling component for augmented and virtual reality head-mounted displays, where size, brightness, resolution and power efficiency are paramount.
