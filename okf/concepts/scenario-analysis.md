# Scenario Analysis

Scenario analysis is a structured method for exploring how a system behaves under a set of plausible alternative futures rather than a single forecast. It defines coherent scenarios over key drivers and evaluates outcomes, risks, and decisions across each. It is widely used in financial planning, climate disclosure, and operational risk to stress-test strategies against uncertainty.
