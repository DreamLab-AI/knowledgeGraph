# Venture Capital

Venture capital is a form of private financing in which investors provide funding to early-stage, high-growth companies in exchange for equity or, in crypto markets, for token allocations. It accepts elevated risk in pursuit of outsized returns, typically deploying capital in staged rounds with active governance and mentorship. In the blockchain sector, venture capital frequently coexists with and contrasts against token-sale mechanisms as a route to capitalising protocols.
