# Hyperledger Foundation

The Hyperledger Foundation is an open-source collaborative initiative hosted by the Linux Foundation, founded in December 2015, that stewards a portfolio of enterprise-grade distributed ledger frameworks, identity toolkits, and interoperability libraries. Its flagship projects include Hyperledger Fabric (a modular permissioned DLT with pluggable consensus and chaincode-based smart contracts), Hyperledger Besu (an Ethereum-compatible client for public and private enterprise networks), Hyperledger Aries (a decentralised identity and verifiable credentials stack), and Hyperledger Cacti (a cross-ledger interoperability framework). The Foundation operates under an open-governance model with a Technical Steering Committee, project lifecycle governance (Incubation, Graduated, Dormant), and membership tiers spanning major technology vendors, financial institutions, and government bodies worldwide.
