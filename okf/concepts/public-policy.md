# Public Policy

Public policy is the set of decisions, principles, and courses of action that governments and public institutions adopt to address societal problems and pursue collective goals. It encompasses the formulation, adoption, implementation, and evaluation of measures spanning law, regulation, spending, and service delivery, informed by evidence and shaped by competing interests and values. In digital contexts it determines how states govern technology, data, infrastructure, and rights.
