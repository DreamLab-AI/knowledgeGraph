# Protein Structure Prediction

Protein structure prediction is the computational determination of a protein's three-dimensional folded structure from its amino-acid sequence. Deep-learning systems such as AlphaFold, built on attention-based architectures, achieved near-experimental accuracy and transformed structural biology. It is a landmark AI application with deep impact on drug discovery, molecular biology, and rational enzyme design.
