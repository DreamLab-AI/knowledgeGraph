# Supply Chain Due Diligence Instrument

A supply chain due diligence instrument is a law, regulation or standard that sets out the duties of companies to identify and address adverse impacts in their supply chains, and the instruments described here differ in subject, in who they bind and in what they require.[^1][^2]
