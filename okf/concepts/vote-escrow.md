# Vote Escrow

Vote escrow (ve) is a tokenomics mechanism in which holders lock governance tokens for a chosen duration in exchange for non-transferable, time-decaying voting power and often boosted protocol rewards. Popularised by Curve's veCRV model, it aligns voter incentives with long-term protocol health by rewarding commitment over short-term speculation. Locked positions decay linearly to zero at unlock, requiring periodic re-locking to maintain influence.
