# Howey Test

The Howey Test is a four-part legal framework established by the U.S. Supreme Court in SEC v. W.J. Howey Co. (1946) to determine whether a transaction qualifies as an 'investment contract' and therefore constitutes a security subject to federal securities regulation. An instrument is a security if it involves (1) an investment of money, (2) in a common enterprise, (3) with an expectation of profits, (4) derived from the efforts of others.
