# Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) is a semiconductor foundry that manufactures chips designed by its customers. TSMC's own site says it was established in 1987 by Dr. Morris Chang and that it pioneered the pure-play foundry business model with an exclusive focus on manufacturing its customers' products.[^1]
