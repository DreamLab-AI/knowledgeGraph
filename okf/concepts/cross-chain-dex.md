# Cross-Chain DEX

A cross-chain DEX is a decentralised exchange that lets users swap assets native to different blockchains without a centralised custodian. It relies on bridges, atomic swaps, or liquidity-network protocols to coordinate settlement across chains while preserving non-custodial trading. It extends decentralised finance beyond single-chain liquidity silos.
