# Procure-to-Pay

Procure-to-pay (P2P) is the typical set of business functions used to manage business-to-business purchasing, from requisition and purchase order, through receipt of goods or services and the purchase invoice, to supplier payment and the recording of transactions in the financial accounts.[^1]
