# Financial Infrastructure

Financial Infrastructure comprises the foundational systems, networks, institutions, and regulatory frameworks that enable the creation, transfer, settlement, and custody of financial value at scale. It encompasses payment clearing and settlement networks, central securities depositories, central counterparty clearinghouses, correspondent banking rails, central bank digital currency platforms, and blockchain-based settlement layers. These components collectively underpin both legacy financial systems and emerging decentralised finance ecosystems, ensuring liquidity, finality, and systemic resilience. The integrity of financial infrastructure is a prerequisite for efficient capital allocation, monetary policy transmission, and macroeconomic stability.
