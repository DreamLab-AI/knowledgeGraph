# Exchange Rate

An exchange rate is the price of one currency expressed in terms of another, determining how much of one monetary unit is required to purchase a unit of the other. Exchange rates are set in foreign-exchange markets through supply and demand, influenced by interest rates, inflation, trade balances, and central-bank policy, and may float freely, be pegged, or be managed. In crypto and stablecoin contexts the same concept governs the peg between a token and a fiat reference. Exchange rates are fundamental to international trade, monetary policy transmission, and cross-border value transfer.
