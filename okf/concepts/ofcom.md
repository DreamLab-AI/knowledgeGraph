# Ofcom

Ofcom (the Office of Communications) is the independent statutory regulator and competition authority for the UK communications industries, established by the Communications Act 2003. Its remit covers broadcasting, telecommunications, postal services, and — following the Online Safety Act 2023 — online platforms. It enforces spectrum management, sets licence conditions for broadcasters and telecoms operators, adjudicates on consumer complaints, and oversees compliance with the UK Online Safety Act framework requiring platforms to implement systems preventing access to illegal content.
