# Holographic Telepresence

Holographic Telepresence is a form of remote presence technology that projects three-dimensional, life-size representations of remote participants into a shared physical or virtual space. It combines light field capture, display, and rendering techniques to create the illusion that a remote person is physically co-located with local participants. This approach enables natural interaction cues such as eye contact, spatial positioning, and gestural communication that are absent in conventional video conferencing.
