# Communication Infrastructure

The physical and logical substrate — including fibre, wireless networks, data centres, protocols, and switching equipment — that enables the transmission of information between nodes in a networked system. It underpins digital services by providing reliable, low-latency, and high-bandwidth connectivity at scale, from local area networks to global internet backbones.
