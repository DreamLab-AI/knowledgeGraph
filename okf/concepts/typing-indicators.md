# Typing Indicators

Typing indicators are real-time signals that inform participants when another user is actively composing a message in a shared conversation thread. They create a sense of conversational presence and reduce the likelihood of duplicate or crossed messages in synchronous exchanges. As a social cue, they contribute to turn-taking norms similar to those in face-to-face dialogue.
