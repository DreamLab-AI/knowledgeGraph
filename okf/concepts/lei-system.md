# LEI System

The LEI System is the Global Legal Entity Identifier System, a federated infrastructure overseen by the GLEIF that issues, maintains, and publishes Legal Entity Identifiers and their reference data. It comprises a Regulatory Oversight Committee, accredited Local Operating Units, and an open global directory queryable by anyone. The system provides a single authoritative source for identifying legal entities in financial markets.
