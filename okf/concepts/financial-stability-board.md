# Financial Stability Board

The Financial Stability Board (FSB) is an international body established in 2009 by the G20 that monitors and makes recommendations about the global financial system to promote financial stability. It coordinates the work of national financial authorities and international standard-setting bodies, and develops and promotes effective regulatory, supervisory, and other financial sector policies to reduce systemic risk.
