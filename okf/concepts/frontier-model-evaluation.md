# Frontier Model Evaluation

Frontier model evaluation is the systematic assessment of the capabilities, limitations, and risks of the most advanced AI systems, including dangerous-capability and safety testing. It uses benchmarks, red-teaming, and threat-model-driven evaluations to inform deployment decisions and regulation. Such evaluation is increasingly required by AI governance regimes before high-capability models are released.
