# Augmented Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital content onto the real world in real-time, enhancing users' perception of their physical environment through smartphones, head-mounted displays, or smart glasses. AR systems require coupling of real and virtual environments, real-time interaction, and precise 3D registration of virtual objects aligned with physical space.
