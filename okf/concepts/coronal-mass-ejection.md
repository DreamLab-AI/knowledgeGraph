# Coronal Mass Ejection

A coronal mass ejection, or CME, is an eruption of magnetised plasma from the Sun's corona into interplanetary space. A CME is physically distinct from a solar flare: a flare is a burst of electromagnetic radiation, whereas an ejection is material and embedded magnetic field moving through the solar wind. The two can occur together.[^1]
