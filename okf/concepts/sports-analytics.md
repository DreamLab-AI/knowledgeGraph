# Sports Analytics

Sports analytics is the application of data capture, statistical modelling, and machine learning to athletic performance, tactics, and injury prevention, drawing on tracking data, biomechanics, and event logs. Computer-vision pipelines increasingly supply the underlying movement data by extracting player positions and body pose from video. It informs coaching decisions, talent evaluation, broadcast insight, and load management.
