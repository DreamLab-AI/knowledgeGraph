# Cryptocurrency Remuneration

Cryptocurrency remuneration is the practice of paying remote workers, freelancers, or distributed team members in digital assets — including Bitcoin, Ethereum, or fiat-pegged stablecoins — rather than traditional currency. It enables borderless, near-instant settlement at low transaction cost, bypassing correspondent banking infrastructure and extending financial access to unbanked populations. Compliance with local payroll tax, AML/KYC, and employment law obligations remains essential.
