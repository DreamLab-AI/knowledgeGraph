# ALBERT

A Lite BERT: a parameter-efficient transformer variant that uses factorised embedding parameterisation and cross-layer parameter sharing to achieve 18x fewer parameters than BERT-large whilst matching or exceeding its performance on NLP benchmarks such as GLUE, SQuAD, and RACE.
