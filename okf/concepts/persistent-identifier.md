# Persistent Identifier

A persistent identifier (PID) is a long-lasting, globally unique reference to a digital object that remains stable even as the object's location or custodian changes. PIDs such as DOIs, Handles, and ARKs are resolved through a managed indirection service that maps the identifier to current metadata and access endpoints. They are foundational to citation, provenance, and interoperability in scholarly and digital-object infrastructure.
