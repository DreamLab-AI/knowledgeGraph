# National Competent Authority

A body designated by an EU Member State under the AI Act — encompassing market surveillance authorities, notifying authorities, and data protection authorities — responsible for supervising provider and deployer compliance with the Act's requirements, enforcing penalties, and coordinating with the AI Office on cross-border and GPAI model matters.
