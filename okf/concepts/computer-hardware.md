# Computer Hardware

Physical computing components optimised for artificial intelligence and spatial computing workloads, encompassing GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, ASICs, and neuromorphic chips. Modern AI hardware emphasises parallel matrix processing, high-bandwidth memory, mixed-precision arithmetic, and energy-efficient inference at scale, enabling both large-model training and real-time rendering in immersive applications.
