# Critical Capability

A critical capability is a kind of [[Agent Capability]] whose exercise is consequential enough to need distinct handling. It is the sibling of [[Restricted Capability]], and the two classes are disjoint. The Agent Ontology declares `CriticalCapability` without a definition; this one follows from its position and its one axiom.
