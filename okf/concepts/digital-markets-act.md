# Digital Markets Act

The Digital Markets Act (DMA) is a European Union regulation that imposes ex ante obligations and prohibitions on large online platforms designated as gatekeepers to ensure contestable and fair digital markets. Rather than waiting for case-by-case competition enforcement, it sets binding rules on designated core platform services covering interoperability, data use, self-preferencing, default settings, and access for business users. Gatekeepers must comply with a list of do's and don'ts, with the European Commission as sole enforcer empowered to levy substantial fines and structural remedies for non-compliance.
