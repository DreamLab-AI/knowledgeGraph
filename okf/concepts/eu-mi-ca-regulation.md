# EU MiCA Regulation

The EU Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation is a European Union framework establishing harmonised rules for crypto-asset issuance, trading and service provision, covering asset-referenced tokens, e-money tokens, and crypto-asset service providers across EU member states.
