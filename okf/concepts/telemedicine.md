# Telemedicine

Telemedicine is the delivery of clinical healthcare services — including diagnosis, consultation, monitoring, and treatment guidance — at a distance using telecommunications technology such as video conferencing, secure messaging, and remote sensing devices. It encompasses synchronous consultations (live video visits), asynchronous store-and-forward exchanges (images, test results), and remote patient monitoring (RPM) using wearable sensors and IoT devices. Telemedicine dramatically expands access to specialist care for patients in rural, underserved, or mobility-constrained situations.
