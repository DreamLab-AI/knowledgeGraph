# Think Aloud Protocol

The Think Aloud Protocol is a qualitative usability research method in which participants verbalise their thought processes continuously while performing tasks with a system or interface. Originating in cognitive psychology, it provides direct access to users' mental models, expectations, and confusion points. The method is widely used in human-computer interaction research and UX design to uncover usability issues that observation alone cannot reveal. Two variants exist: concurrent think-aloud (verbalising during the task) and retrospective think-aloud (recalling thoughts after completing it).
