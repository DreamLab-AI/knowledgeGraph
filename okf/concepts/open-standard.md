# Open Standard

An open standard is a publicly accessible specification for a technology, protocol, format, or interface that is developed through a transparent, consensus-based process and can be implemented and used without discriminatory restrictions. Open standards enable interoperability between systems from different vendors by providing a shared, stable technical baseline. They are maintained by recognised standards bodies and are typically available royalty-free or under FRAND (Fair, Reasonable And Non-Discriminatory) licensing terms. Open standards contrast with proprietary specifications controlled by a single vendor and are foundational to open, competitive ecosystems.
