# User Awareness

The level of understanding and knowledge that users possess about digital systems, privacy practices, security risks, and their rights when interacting with metaverse platforms and virtual environments. Effective user awareness encompasses privacy literacy, recognition of security threats, platform policy comprehension, and knowledge of available remedies, forming a prerequisite for informed consent and safe participation.
