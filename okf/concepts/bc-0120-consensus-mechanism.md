# BC-0120-consensus-mechanism

BC-0120-consensus-mechanism is an ontology catalogue entry representing the knowledge domain of blockchain consensus mechanisms — the family of distributed protocols by which a peer-to-peer network of nodes agrees on a canonical ordering of transactions and the current state of a shared ledger without a trusted central authority. It serves as an organising concept node within a blockchain knowledge graph taxonomy.
