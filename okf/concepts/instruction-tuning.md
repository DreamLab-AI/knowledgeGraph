# Instruction Tuning

A fine-tuning technique that trains language models to follow natural language instructions by learning from diverse instruction-response pairs. Instruction tuning enables models to generalise to new tasks described through instructions without task-specific training data, bridging raw language modelling and practical assistive behaviour.
