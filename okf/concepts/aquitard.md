# Aquitard

An aquitard is a confining bed that retards but does not prevent the flow of water to or from an adjacent aquifer, which is why it is also called a leaky confining bed.[^1] It does not readily yield water to wells or springs, but it may serve as a storage unit for groundwater.[^1]
