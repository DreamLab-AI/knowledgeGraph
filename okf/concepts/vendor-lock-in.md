# Vendor Lock-in

Vendor lock-in is a situation in which a customer becomes dependent on a particular supplier's products or services and cannot switch to an alternative without substantial cost, effort or disruption. It arises from proprietary formats, non-portable data, integration dependencies and incompatible interfaces that raise switching costs. Open standards, data portability and interoperability are the principal countermeasures that preserve customer choice.
