# Blockchain Analytics Platform

A blockchain analytics platform is software that ingests, clusters, and analyses on-chain transaction data to trace fund flows, attribute addresses to entities, and assess risk. Platforms such as Chainalysis and Elliptic apply heuristics, machine learning, and labelled datasets to support anti-money-laundering, sanctions screening, and investigations. They are central tooling for exchange compliance teams, regulators, and law-enforcement agencies.
