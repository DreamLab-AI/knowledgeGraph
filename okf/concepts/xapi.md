# Xapi

xAPI (the Experience API, also known as Tin Can API) is an e-learning interoperability specification that captures learning experiences as actor-verb-object statements sent to a Learning Record Store. Unlike SCORM, it records learning that occurs outside a single course or platform, including informal, mobile and simulation-based activity. xAPI underpins rich learning analytics by providing a flexible, standardised data model.
