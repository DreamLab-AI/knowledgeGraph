# Russell Group

The Russell Group is a self-selecting association of leading research-intensive universities in the United Kingdom that advocates collectively on research funding, policy, and higher-education strategy. Its members are characterised by substantial research output, strong postgraduate provision, and extensive industry and international links. Membership is frequently used as a shorthand for institutions with high research standing.
