# Permissionless Trading

Permissionless trading refers to the ability to execute financial trades — buying, selling, or swapping digital assets — without requiring authorisation from a centralised intermediary, gatekeeper, or identity provider. Enabled by on-chain smart contracts and automated market makers (AMMs), permissionless trading removes the account approval, KYC, and whitelist requirements of traditional financial markets, allowing any wallet address to interact directly with liquidity pools. It is a foundational property of decentralised finance (DeFi) protocols.
