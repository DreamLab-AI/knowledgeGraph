# Net Zero Targets

Net Zero Targets are formal, time-bound commitments made by governments, corporations, subnational jurisdictions, or other entities to reduce gross greenhouse gas emissions as deeply as feasible and to balance any residual emissions with equivalent, verified carbon removals, thereby reaching a net-zero contribution to atmospheric greenhouse gas concentrations by a specified date. They typically distinguish near-term absolute reduction milestones across Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions from longer-term residual-offset strategies, and are calibrated against the IPCC's 1.5 °C-consistent pathways. Credible targets are characterised by transparent interim milestones, independent third-party verification, and a clear accounting boundary that separates genuine emission reductions from carbon offsetting.
