# Control Signal

A control signal is the command output computed by a controller and applied to actuate or steer a system toward a desired state. In robotics and electromechanical contexts it is the voltage, current, or set-point delivered to an actuator; in generative pipelines it is the spatial conditioning input that guides synthesis. In both senses it is the carrier of intent between a decision process and the system it drives.
