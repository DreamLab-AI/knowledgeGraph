# ETSI Domain Security and Privacy

The ETSI Domain Security and Privacy is a governance framework protecting metaverse ecosystems through comprehensive controls spanning cryptography, access management, data governance, and compliance measures. It addresses threat surface mapping, post-quantum cryptographic resilience, privacy-enhancing computation, digital evidence chain of custody, and psychological profiling safeguards for users of immersive environments.
