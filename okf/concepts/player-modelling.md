# Player Modelling

Player modelling is the computational construction of representations of a player's preferences, skill, behaviour, and emotional state from in-game data. These models enable games to personalise difficulty, content, and recommendations, and to predict future actions. It is a foundational technique for adaptive and AI-driven game systems.
