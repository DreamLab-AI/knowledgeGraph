# Scientific Discovery Acceleration

Scientific discovery acceleration is the application of artificial intelligence to compress the cycle of hypothesis generation, experimentation, and analysis across scientific disciplines. It includes AI-driven literature synthesis, simulation surrogates, automated experiment design, and prediction of structures or materials. It is often cited as a transformative potential outcome of advanced and general-purpose AI systems.
