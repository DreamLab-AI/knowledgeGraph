# Haptic Feedback Telepresence

Haptic Feedback Telepresence is the integration of tactile and kinesthetic feedback devices — such as force-feedback gloves, exoskeletons, and haptic controllers — into telepresence systems, enabling bidirectional touch sensation between a remote operator and a distant physical or virtual environment. It closes the sensorimotor loop beyond visual and auditory channels, allowing operators to feel forces, textures, weight, and vibration, which significantly enhances manipulation precision and immersive presence in teleoperation, surgical robotics, and VR collaboration contexts.
