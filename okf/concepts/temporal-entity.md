# Temporal Entity

A temporal entity is an ontological category for anything that exists in or is defined by time, such as an instant, an interval, an event, or a process. In knowledge representation it serves as a shared superclass that anchors time-bearing concepts so they can be related by ordering, duration, and containment. The notion follows established time ontologies like the W3C OWL-Time vocabulary.
