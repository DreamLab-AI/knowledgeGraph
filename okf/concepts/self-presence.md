# Self Presence

The subjective sense that one's virtual or avatar representation in a mediated environment is a genuine extension of the physical self — encompassing body ownership, proprioceptive coherence, and self-location within virtual space. Self presence is a component of overall presence and a prerequisite for authentic immersive collaboration, as it governs how naturally users interact with and move through shared virtual environments.
