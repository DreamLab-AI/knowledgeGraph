# Immersion Cooling

Immersion cooling is a thermal-management technique in which electronic hardware is submerged directly in a thermally conductive but electrically insulating dielectric fluid to dissipate heat. It offers far higher heat-transfer efficiency than air cooling, enabling denser deployments and lower cooling energy overhead. The technique is widely adopted in high-density data centres and cryptocurrency-mining facilities.
