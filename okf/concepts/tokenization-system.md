# Tokenization System

A tokenization system is the end-to-end technical and operational infrastructure that converts rights, assets, or value into blockchain tokens and manages their lifecycle of issuance, transfer, custody, and redemption. It combines smart-contract templates, identity and compliance modules, and registry services to ensure that tokens reliably represent and preserve their underlying claims.
