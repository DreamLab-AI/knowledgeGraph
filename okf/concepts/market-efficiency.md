# Market Efficiency

Market efficiency is the degree to which asset prices fully and rapidly reflect all available information, leaving no systematic opportunity for risk-adjusted excess returns. The efficient-market hypothesis frames it in weak, semi-strong, and strong forms according to the information set incorporated. Higher efficiency implies accurate price signals, lower transaction costs, and effective capital allocation.
