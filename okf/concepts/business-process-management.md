# Business Process Management

Business Process Management (BPM) is a discipline that uses methods, techniques, and software tools to discover, model, analyse, measure, improve, and automate business processes — end-to-end workflows that produce value for internal stakeholders or external customers. It combines organisational management practice with process automation technology to make organisational operations more efficient, agile, and aligned with strategic goals.
