# Disaster Mapping

Disaster mapping converts satellite imagery and other geospatial information into products for preparedness, emergency response and recovery. Depending on the request, a product may show the pre-event setting, the observed extent of a hazard, exposed assets, or an interpreted grade of damage. Each map is tied to an area, an acquisition time and the evidence available to its producer.
