# Blockchain Provenance

Blockchain Provenance is the application of distributed ledger technology to record and verify the complete historical lineage of an asset, document, or data artefact, such that its origin, custody chain, and transformations are cryptographically authenticated and tamper-resistant. Each provenance event is anchored as an immutable transaction on-chain, enabling any party to independently reconstruct and audit the full lifecycle of the item without relying on a central authority.
