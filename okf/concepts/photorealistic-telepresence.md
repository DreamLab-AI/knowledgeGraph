# Photorealistic Telepresence

Photorealistic telepresence is remote real-time communication in which participants appear as lifelike, volumetrically accurate representations rather than video tiles or stylised avatars. It reconstructs a person's appearance and motion using techniques such as neural avatars and 3D Gaussian splatting, rendering them convincingly in a shared spatial scene. The goal is a sense of co-presence that closely matches being physically together.
