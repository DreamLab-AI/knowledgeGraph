# Programmatic Advertising

Programmatic advertising is the automated buying and selling of digital advertising inventory through software platforms and real-time auctions, replacing manual insertion orders with algorithmic matching of advertiser bids to publisher ad slots. It encompasses demand-side platforms, supply-side platforms, ad exchanges, and data management systems that collectively orchestrate the delivery of targeted advertisements at scale.
