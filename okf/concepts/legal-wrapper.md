# Legal Wrapper

A legal wrapper is an off-chain legal entity, such as a foundation, LLC, or association, established to give a DAO or on-chain protocol recognised legal personality. It enables the organisation to enter contracts, hold assets, limit member liability, and interface with regulators while preserving on-chain governance. Jurisdictions like Wyoming (DAO LLC), the Marshall Islands, and the Cayman Islands offer purpose-built wrapper forms.
