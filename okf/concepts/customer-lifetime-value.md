# Customer Lifetime Value

Customer lifetime value is a predictive metric estimating the total net revenue a business can expect from a customer over the duration of their relationship. It is computed from historical purchase frequency, average order value, and retention rates, often refined with machine-learned models that account for individual customer behaviour. Customer relationship management and customer experience management systems use lifetime value estimates to prioritise retention and personalisation investment.
