# Signal Amplifier

A signal amplifier is an electronic circuit that increases the power, voltage, or current of a weak signal while ideally preserving its waveform. In sensing applications it boosts low-amplitude transducer outputs above the noise floor so they can be digitised, with gain, bandwidth, noise figure, and linearity as key parameters. It is an essential front-end component of sensors and biosignal acquisition systems.
