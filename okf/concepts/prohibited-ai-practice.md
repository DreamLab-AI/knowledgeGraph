# Prohibited AI Practice

Prohibited AI Practices are categories of AI deployment banned under EU AI Act Article 5 (effective 2 February 2025), covering subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerability groups, social scoring by public or private bodies, and real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces. Violations carry fines up to €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover.
