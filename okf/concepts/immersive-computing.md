# Immersive Computing

Immersive computing is the broad discipline of designing and operating computing systems that surround users with synthetic or blended sensory environments — encompassing virtual reality, augmented reality, and mixed reality — to create a subjective sense of presence within a digitally mediated space. It integrates real-time 3D rendering, spatial tracking, haptic feedback, and multi-modal interaction to replace or augment the user's natural perceptual field.
