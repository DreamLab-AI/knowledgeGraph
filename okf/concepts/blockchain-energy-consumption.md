# Blockchain Energy Consumption

The electrical power and computational resources consumed by blockchain networks during transaction validation, block creation, and network security operations, varying significantly across consensus mechanisms from energy-intensive Proof-of-Work (Bitcoin: ~140 TWh/year, 0.65% of global electricity) to energy-efficient Proof-of-Stake (Ethereum post-Merge: 99.95% reduction), with ongoing research into sustainable consensus algorithms, renewable energy mining, carbon credit tokenization, and environmental impact measurement frameworks.
