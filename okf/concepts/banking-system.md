# Banking System

An organised network of financial institutions, regulatory bodies, payment infrastructure, and legal frameworks that collectively facilitate the creation, custody, transfer, and lending of money within an economy. A banking system includes central banks, commercial banks, investment banks, and clearing houses, all operating under a body of prudential regulation. It underpins economic activity by providing mechanisms for capital allocation, risk distribution, and monetary policy transmission. Modern banking systems increasingly integrate digital and real-time payment rails.
