# US Regulatory Framework

The US Regulatory Framework for cryptocurrency operates through a fragmented multi-agency structure in which the SEC applies securities law to tokens, FinCEN supervises money transmission and anti-money laundering compliance, the CFTC regulates commodity-classified digital assets and derivatives, and the OCC addresses banking integration. This jurisdictional overlap creates significant compliance complexity for exchanges, DeFi protocols, and digital asset issuers absent comprehensive Congressional legislation.
