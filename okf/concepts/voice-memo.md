# Voice Memo

An asynchronous audio communication artefact that allows users to record and share spoken messages, conveying prosodic nuance, emotional tone, and contextual richness not available in text. Voice memos occupy a middle tier of communication fidelity between text messages and synchronous video calls, enabling remote teams to maintain natural communication rhythms across time zones without requiring real-time scheduling.
