# Bluetooth LE

Bluetooth LE (Low Energy), also known as Bluetooth Smart, is a short-range wireless communication protocol introduced in Bluetooth 4.0 (2010) and standardised by the Bluetooth SIG, designed to transmit small amounts of data at low duty cycles while consuming a fraction of the power of Classic Bluetooth. It operates in the 2.4 GHz ISM band with 40 channels, using frequency-hopping spread spectrum, and is optimised for battery-constrained devices that need to remain active for months or years on a coin cell.
