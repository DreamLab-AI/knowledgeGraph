# rb 0018 inspection robot

An inspection robot is a mobile robotic system designed to autonomously or semi-autonomously navigate environments that are hazardous, inaccessible, or inefficient for human workers, in order to assess the structural, mechanical, or operational condition of infrastructure, equipment, or facilities. Equipped with sensor arrays (cameras, LiDAR, ultrasonic, thermal IR), inspection robots collect data for predictive maintenance, safety auditing, and non-destructive testing across industries such as oil and gas pipelines, power-grid infrastructure, bridges, and confined industrial spaces.
