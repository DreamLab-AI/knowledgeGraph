# Data Lifecycle

The sequence of stages that data passes through from initial creation or capture to eventual archival or destruction, typically comprising creation, storage, use, sharing, archiving, and disposal, with each stage carrying distinct security, quality, and regulatory obligations that organisations manage through lifecycle policies, retention schedules, and classification-driven controls.
