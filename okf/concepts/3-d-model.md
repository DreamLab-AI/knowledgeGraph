# 3D Model

A 3D Model is a digital representation of a three-dimensional object or environment constructed from vertices, edges, faces, and materials, encoded in standard formats such as glTF, OBJ, FBX, or USD, and used as a foundational asset in spatial computing, simulation, gaming, and metaverse applications.
