# Methane Abatement

Methane abatement is the set of techniques for capturing, destroying, or preventing the release of methane, a greenhouse gas with roughly 80 times the near-term warming potential of carbon dioxide. Approaches include flaring, combustion in engines or turbines, and conversion of otherwise-vented or flared gas into useful energy. In the Bitcoin context it underpins the use of stranded or waste methane to power mining as an emissions-reduction strategy.
