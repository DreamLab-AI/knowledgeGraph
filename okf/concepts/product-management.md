# Product Management

Product management is the organisational function responsible for guiding a product through its lifecycle by defining vision, strategy, and roadmap based on user needs, business goals, and market conditions. It coordinates design, engineering, and go-to-market work and prioritises features against constraints. AI tools increasingly assist with research synthesis, prioritisation, and roadmap visualisation.
