# Pharmaceutical Traceability

Blockchain-based pharmaceutical supply chain systems employing unit-level serialisation, immutable audit trails, and automated smart-contract verification to combat counterfeit medicines, enable DSCSA and EU FMD regulatory compliance, and deliver cold-chain monitoring and clinical-trial supply management across consortium networks such as MediLedger.
