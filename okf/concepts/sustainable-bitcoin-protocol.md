# Sustainable Bitcoin Protocol

The Sustainable Bitcoin Protocol (SBP) is a certification and tokenisation framework that attests the use of renewable or stranded energy in Bitcoin mining and issues on-chain Sustainable Bitcoin Certificates to verified miners. It enables green bitcoin premiums and provides institutional investors with auditable environmental provenance for Bitcoin holdings.
