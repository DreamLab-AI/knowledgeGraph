# Formal Language

A formal language is a set of strings of symbols drawn from a finite alphabet, defined precisely by formal rules such as a grammar or automaton rather than by usage or convention. The Chomsky hierarchy classifies formal languages by the generative power required to describe them, ranging from regular languages recognised by finite automata to recursively enumerable languages recognised by Turing machines. Formal languages provide the mathematical foundation for specifying syntax in programming languages, parsers, logic, and knowledge representation. They are studied in automata theory and underpin compilers and ontology languages.
