# Long-Horizon Planning

Long-horizon planning is the problem of generating and executing sequences of actions over extended time horizons — spanning hundreds to thousands of steps — to achieve high-level goals that cannot be accomplished through simple reactive policies. It requires maintaining coherent goals, managing intermediate subgoal states, and adapting plans in response to environmental changes and unexpected outcomes.
