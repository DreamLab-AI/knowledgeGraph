# Cross-Functional Collaboration

Cross-functional collaboration is coordinated work between people from different disciplines or organisational units — such as engineering, design, and operations — combining diverse expertise toward a shared goal. In distributed and remote settings it depends on digital workplace platforms and virtual collaboration tools to bridge the coordination gaps that would otherwise be closed by physical co-location. It is a recurring requirement for digital workplace platforms and virtual collaboration systems.
