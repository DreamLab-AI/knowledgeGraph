# Platform Engineering

Platform Engineering is the discipline of designing, building, and operating internal developer platforms (IDPs) that abstract infrastructure complexity and provide self-service toolchains, automated CI/CD pipelines, and standardised operational workflows for software engineering teams. It treats the IDP as a product, applying product management techniques to improve developer experience and reduce cognitive load. Platform Engineering sits at the intersection of DevOps, site reliability engineering, and cloud-native infrastructure.
