# Plant Model

A plant model is a mathematical representation of the dynamic system being controlled, mapping control inputs and disturbances to the system's state and outputs over time. Usually expressed as differential equations, transfer functions, or state-space form, it captures how the plant responds so a controller can be designed and tuned against it. Accurate plant models are central to control theory and to model-based control synthesis and simulation.
