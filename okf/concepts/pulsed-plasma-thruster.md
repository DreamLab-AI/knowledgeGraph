# Pulsed Plasma Thruster

A pulsed plasma thruster is an electric thruster that produces thrust by triggering an electric arc between a pair of electrodes, typically ablating a solid propellant such as polytetrafluoroethylene or ionising a gaseous propellant.[^1]
