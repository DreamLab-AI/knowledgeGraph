# Information Sharing

Information sharing is the coordinated exchange of data, findings or risk signals between organisations or stakeholders, used in AI governance to spread safety-relevant knowledge across otherwise competing or independent actors. Bodies such as the Frontier Model Forum and regulatory coordination groups rely on structured information-sharing arrangements to track emerging risks and align practice. It is a form of data sharing applied specifically to governance and oversight contexts.
