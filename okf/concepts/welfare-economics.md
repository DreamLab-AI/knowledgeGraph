# Welfare Economics

Welfare economics is the branch of economics that evaluates the allocation of resources and the distribution of outcomes in terms of aggregate social well-being. It formalises notions of efficiency, principally Pareto efficiency, alongside frameworks for comparing distributions through social welfare functions, and provides the normative basis for assessing market outcomes, market failures, and policy interventions. In tokenised and blockchain economies it informs mechanism design, public-goods funding, and the analysis of incentive structures that determine whether decentralised systems produce socially desirable equilibria.
