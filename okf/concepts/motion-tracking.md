# Motion Tracking

Motion tracking is a technology that captures and records the movement of objects, bodies, or body parts in physical space, translating this data into digital representations for use in VR/AR systems, animation, and metaverse applications. In XR contexts it is fundamental to embodied presence by mapping user movements to avatar animation and enabling natural interaction with virtual environments.
