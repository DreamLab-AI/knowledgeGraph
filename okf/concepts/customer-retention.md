# Customer Retention

Customer retention is the set of strategies and mechanisms a business uses to keep existing customers engaged and transacting over time, reducing churn. It commonly relies on loyalty and rewards schemes, personalised omnichannel experiences, and data-driven engagement to maximise customer lifetime value. As an economic mechanism it is typically cheaper than acquisition and central to sustainable revenue.
