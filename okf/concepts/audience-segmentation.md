# Audience Segmentation

Audience segmentation is the practice of dividing a population of users or customers into distinct groups that share attributes, behaviours, or needs. Segments are derived from demographic, behavioural, contextual, and inferred signals and are used to tailor messaging, targeting, and experiences. Segmentation makes personalisation, efficient targeting, and measurement possible across marketing and product functions.
