# Mesosphere

Directly above the stratosphere and below the thermosphere lies the mesosphere, within which temperature decreases as altitude increases.[^1][^2] The Australian Antarctic Division and the draft ISO text both tie its limits to this temperature behaviour, with the base at the stratopause and the top at the mesopause.[^2][^3]
