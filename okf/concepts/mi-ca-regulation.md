# MiCA Regulation

The Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) Regulation (EU 2023/1114) is the European Union's comprehensive legislative framework governing the issuance, offering, and trading of crypto-assets, including asset-referenced tokens, e-money tokens, and utility tokens. Adopted in June 2023 and phasing in through December 2024, MiCA establishes authorisation requirements for crypto-asset service providers (CASPs), disclosure obligations analogous to prospectus rules, and prudential standards for stablecoin issuers, creating the world's first complete statutory crypto-asset regime across a major economic bloc.
