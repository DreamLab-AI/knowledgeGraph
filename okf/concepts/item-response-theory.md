# Item Response Theory

Item Response Theory (IRT) is a family of psychometric models that relate the probability of a correct response to a test item to a latent trait of the respondent, such as ability, and to properties of the item, such as difficulty and discrimination. Unlike classical test theory, IRT places examinees and items on a common scale, enabling adaptive testing, equating across forms, and fine-grained measurement of ability. It is widely used in assessment, educational technology, and the adaptive components of intelligent learning systems.
