# Player Agency

Player agency is the degree to which a participant's choices meaningfully shape the state, narrative, and outcomes of an interactive virtual experience. High agency means decisions have consequential, non-trivial effects rather than cosmetic ones, giving players authorship over their path through the world. It is a core design value of open-world and non-linear interactive systems.
