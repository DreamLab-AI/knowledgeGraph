# Monopropellant Propulsion

Monopropellant propulsion stores and feeds one propellant formulation to the thruster. A catalyst, heat or another initiation mechanism triggers decomposition or reaction, producing hot gas that expands through a nozzle. `Monopropellant` describes the single stored feed: an ionic-liquid blend can contain both fuel and oxidising species within that formulation.[^1][^3]
