# NFT Marketplace

A digital platform enabling the creation, listing, buying, selling, and auctioning of non-fungible tokens (NFTs). NFT marketplaces provide discovery, escrow, royalty enforcement, and settlement infrastructure for unique digital assets, typically integrating smart contracts for trustless ownership transfer and on-chain provenance tracking.
