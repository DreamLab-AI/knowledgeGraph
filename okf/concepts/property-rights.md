# Property Rights

Property rights are the legally and institutionally recognised entitlements that determine who may use, transfer, exclude others from, or derive value from a resource — whether physical, digital, or intellectual. They form the foundational incentive structure of market economies, specifying the bundle of rights (usus, fructus, abusus) attached to an asset. In digital and blockchain contexts, property rights extend to on-chain ownership records, smart-contract-enforced exclusivity, NFT-based title deeds, and virtual land parcels, requiring mechanisms for interoperability, provenance tracking, and dispute resolution that operate across jurisdictions.
