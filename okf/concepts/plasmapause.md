# Plasmapause

As the outer boundary of the plasmasphere, the plasmapause marks the edge of the region of cold plasma around Earth.[^1][^2] Originally defined as a steep gradient in electron density that separates the relatively dense plasmasphere close to Earth from the depleted region beyond, called the plasmatrough, it is described by NASA as the outer boundary of the co-rotating ionospheric plasma region.[^2][^3]
