# rb 0089 risk assessment

Risk Assessment in robotics is the systematic process of identifying hazards associated with a robot system, estimating the severity and probability of potential harm, and determining whether risks are acceptable or require mitigation. It is mandated by ISO 10218-1/-2 for industrial robots and ISO 13482 for personal care robots, and forms the foundation for selecting appropriate safeguarding measures and collaborative operation modes.
