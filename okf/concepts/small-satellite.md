# Small Satellite

A small satellite is a spacecraft placed in a lower mass class than conventional large missions. The term is useful but not universal: agencies and market studies use different upper limits. NASA's small-spacecraft technology survey adopts 180 kilograms as a working boundary and subdivides the range into mini-, micro-, nano-, pico- and femtosatellites.[^1] That boundary belongs to NASA's scheme and should not be presented as an international legal definition.
