# Arbitrum DAO

Arbitrum DAO is the decentralised autonomous organisation that governs the Arbitrum One and Arbitrum Nova Layer 2 blockchain networks, operating through the ARB governance token launched by Offchain Labs in March 2023. Token holders propose and vote on protocol upgrades, treasury allocations, and parameter changes via on-chain governance contracts, making Arbitrum DAO one of the largest DAOs by treasury value in the Ethereum ecosystem. Governance decisions are executed through a Security Council with emergency intervention powers and a broader Arbitrum Improvement Proposal (AIP) process for standard changes.
