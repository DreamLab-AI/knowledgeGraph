# Cross-Border Transfer

A cross-border transfer is the movement of value or funds between parties located in different countries, settled across distinct currency and regulatory regimes. It encompasses bank wires, card networks, money-transfer operators, and increasingly digital-payment and cryptocurrency rails. Speed, cost, foreign-exchange handling, and compliance with multiple jurisdictions are its defining constraints.
