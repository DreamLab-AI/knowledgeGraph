# Sea Ice Lead

A sea ice lead is a linear opening in sea ice that forms where floes diverge or shear apart, exposing a surface layer of water.[^1][^2] SWEET describes it as a surface layer of a water body formed when surface ice fractures and moves apart, and NSIDC calls leads narrow, linear cracks.[^1][^2]
