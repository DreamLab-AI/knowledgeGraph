# Trust Building

Trust building is the deliberate process of establishing confidence among participants in a digital system through transparency, accountability, reliable behaviour, and clear communication. In online communities and AI-mediated platforms it is fostered by practices such as content provenance, disclosure of automated generation, and enforced community standards that make actors and outputs verifiable.
