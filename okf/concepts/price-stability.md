# Price Stability

Price stability is a macroeconomic condition in which the general level of prices for goods and services remains broadly constant over time, with inflation low, stable and predictable. It is the primary mandate of most central banks because it preserves the purchasing power of money, anchors expectations and supports sustainable economic growth. Central banks pursue price stability through monetary policy tools that influence interest rates, money supply and inflation expectations.
