# 3D Content Generation

The computational process of producing three-dimensional digital content through algorithmic and AI-driven methods, encompassing procedural generation, neural rendering, NeRF-based scene synthesis, and GAN-based 3D model creation. Applications span game development, virtual reality, digital twins, and automated CAD, enabling scalable production of photorealistic 3D assets from text or image inputs.
