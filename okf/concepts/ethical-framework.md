# Ethical Framework

A structured set of moral principles, values, and reasoning methods that guide the development, deployment, and use of AI systems to ensure they respect human dignity, promote well-being, and avoid harm. Ethical frameworks draw from consequentialism, deontology, virtue ethics, and care ethics to address AI-specific dilemmas around fairness, transparency, accountability, and privacy.
