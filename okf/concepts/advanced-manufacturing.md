# Advanced Manufacturing

Advanced manufacturing is the application of innovative technologies—including automation, robotics, additive manufacturing, digital twins, and data-driven process control—to improve the productivity, flexibility, and quality of producing goods. It integrates cyber-physical systems across the production lifecycle, blurring the boundary between digital design and physical fabrication. Advanced manufacturing is a central pillar of Industry 4.0 and modern industrial strategy.
