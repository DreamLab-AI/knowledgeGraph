# DHL Supply Chain

DHL Supply Chain is the contract logistics division of the DHL group. DHL's own United Kingdom site presents it under the title “DHL Supply Chain – The world’s leading contract logistics provider”, which is the company's description of itself, and lists it among the group's divisions with DHL Express, DHL Global Forwarding, DHL Freight and DHL eCommerce.[^1]
