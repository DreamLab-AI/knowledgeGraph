# AI Safety Institute

An AI Safety Institute (AISI) is a state-backed organisation established to evaluate, test, and research the capabilities and safety risks of advanced AI systems, particularly frontier models. Such bodies conduct pre-deployment and post-deployment evaluations, develop standardised benchmarks for dangerous capability elicitation, and advise governments on AI risk policy. The UK established the world's first AISI in November 2023 at the Bletchley Park AI Safety Summit; the US, Japan, Canada, and other nations subsequently established counterpart bodies. The UK later renamed its institute the AI Security Institute (DSIT) to reflect an expanded emphasis on national-security-relevant AI risks.
