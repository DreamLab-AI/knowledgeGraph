# Plutocratic Voting

Plutocratic voting is a governance model in which voting power is proportional to the quantity of tokens, shares, or stake an actor holds, so that wealthier participants exert correspondingly greater influence over collective decisions. It is the default in most token-based on-chain governance systems, where one token equals one vote. While simple and Sybil-resistant by construction, it concentrates control among large holders and can entrench incumbent interests.
