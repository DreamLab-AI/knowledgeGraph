# Data Catalog

A data catalog is a centralised, searchable inventory of an organisation's data assets enriched with metadata, descriptions, ownership and usage context. It enables discovery, governance and self-service analytics by indexing datasets, schemas and their relationships, often integrating glossaries and lineage. As a component of metadata management and data fabric architectures it is essential for finding and trusting data at scale.
