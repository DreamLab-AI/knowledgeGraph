# Joint Encoder

A joint encoder is a sensor mounted at a robot joint that measures angular or linear position, and often velocity, of that joint. It provides the proprioceptive feedback required for closed-loop position and motion control of articulated mechanisms. Encoders may be optical, magnetic or capacitive and are typically classed as incremental or absolute.
