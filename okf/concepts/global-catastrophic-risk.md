# Global Catastrophic Risk

Global Catastrophic Risk is a category of risk capable of inflicting severe, worldwide damage to human civilisation, potentially including mass casualties, societal collapse, or permanent loss of capability, without necessarily causing human extinction. It is distinguished from existential risk, which specifically threatens humanity's long-term potential or survival, though the two categories overlap substantially. Advanced AI, engineered pandemics, and nuclear conflict are commonly cited sources of global catastrophic risk.
