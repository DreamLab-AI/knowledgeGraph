# Credential Portability

Credential portability is the property that lets a holder carry verifiable credentials across providers, platforms, and jurisdictions without re-issuance or vendor lock-in. It relies on open standards such as W3C Verifiable Credentials and decentralised identifiers so that issuers, holders, and verifiers interoperate. It is a foundational capability for user-controlled digital identity and trust frameworks.
