# CMA CGM

CMA CGM is a container shipping and logistics group whose parent company is governed by French law. The European Commission described CMA CGM in a 2016 decision as the parent company of an international group of companies involved in the sectors of containerised liner shipping and port terminal management, offering a range of services including shipping, reefer transport, handling facilities in port as well as freight transport and logistics on land.[^1]
