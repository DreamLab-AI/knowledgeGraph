# Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive

The Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive, Directive (EU) 2024/1760, lays down rules requiring companies to address actual and potential human rights and environmental adverse impacts in their own operations, those of their subsidiaries and those of their business partners in their chains of activities.[^1]
