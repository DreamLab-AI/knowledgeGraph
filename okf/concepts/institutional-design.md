# Institutional Design

Institutional design is the deliberate construction of the rules, roles, and decision-making procedures that structure how a collective coordinates, allocates authority, and resolves disputes. Drawing on economics, political science, and mechanism design, it shapes incentives so that self-interested actors produce outcomes aligned with shared goals. It applies to states, firms, standards bodies, and on-chain organisations alike, where formal rules and enforcement determine legitimacy and durability.
