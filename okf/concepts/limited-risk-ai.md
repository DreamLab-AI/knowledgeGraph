# Limited Risk AI

Under the EU AI Act (Article 50), Limited Risk AI denotes systems subject only to transparency obligations—primarily chatbots and deepfake generators—where providers must inform users they are interacting with AI or label synthetic content as artificially generated. These systems face no conformity assessment, but non-compliance attracts fines of up to €7.5 million or 1.5% of global annual turnover.
