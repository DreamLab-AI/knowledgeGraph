- ### Definition
  - SA8000 is an auditable social-accountability certification standard for decent working conditions, used to standardise and implement [[Ethical Sourcing]] across supply chains.
- ### Content
  - Built on ILO conventions, the standard sets requirements across labour, safety, and management-system dimensions, verified by third-party audit. Buyers use SA8000 certification of suppliers as evidence of ethical, rights-respecting production.
