- ### Definition
  - National Ai Strategy is a key concept in the governance domain.
  - Related foundational concepts: [[AI Policy]] [[Public Policy]] [[AI Governance]] [[Regulation]] [[Economic Development]].
  - A national AI strategy is a government-led policy framework setting a country's vision, priorities and coordinated actions for developing, adopting and governing artificial intelligence. It typically addresses research and innovation funding, talent and skills, data and compute infrastructure, ethical and regulatory guardrails, public-sector adoption and economic competitiveness. Such strategies align ministries, industry and academia around shared objectives and measurable outcomes.
- ### Overview
  - Since the late 2010s most major economies have published national AI strategies to capture the technology's economic upside while managing its risks. Common pillars include sovereign compute and data, talent pipelines, sector-specific adoption, trustworthy-AI principles and international cooperation. Strategies increasingly link to regulation, standards and public-sector procurement.
- ### Key aspects
  - Vision and measurable national objectives for AI capability and adoption.
  - Investment in research, compute infrastructure and skills.
  - Trustworthy-AI principles, ethics frameworks and regulatory direction.
  - Public-sector adoption and procurement policy.
  - International cooperation and technological sovereignty considerations.
- ### Applications
  - Coordinating cross-ministry AI investment and regulation.
  - Directing sovereign compute and data infrastructure programmes.
  - Setting trustworthy-AI guardrails for industry.
  - Benchmarking national competitiveness against peer economies.
