- ### Definition
	- Trust Management is the systematic process of establishing, evaluating, and maintaining trust relationships between autonomous agents, services, or identities within a distributed system. It encompasses credential issuance, reputation scoring, revocation, and policy enforcement that let participants decide whether to rely on another party's claims or actions. In multi-agent and decentralised-identity systems it underpins authorisation decisions without requiring a central authority.
