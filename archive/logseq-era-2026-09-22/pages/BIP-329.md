- ### Definition
  - BIP-329 defines a portable wallet-label export format, letting metadata travel between wallets and supporting record-keeping for [[Taproot Assets]].
- ### Content
  - Each line of the JSONL export ties a label and optional fields to a referenced object such as a transaction or address. By standardising this previously proprietary data, BIP-329 enables users to migrate wallets without losing the contextual annotations needed for accounting and provenance.
