- ### Definition
	- Condition monitoring is the continuous or periodic measurement of physical parameters — vibration, temperature, pressure, or acoustic signature — from operating machinery to detect degradation before it causes failure. It is a core Industrial IoT application, relying on networked sensors to stream measurements to analytics systems that flag anomalies for predictive maintenance. It reduces unplanned downtime compared with fixed-interval preventive maintenance schedules.
