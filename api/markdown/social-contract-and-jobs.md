- ### Definition
  - The set of expectations and obligations between workers, employers and the state concerning employment, income security and welfare, considered in the context of automation and artificial intelligence reshaping the labour market.

- ### Content
  - The social contract describes the implicit agreement governing how labour, income and social protection are distributed across a society. As artificial intelligence and automation displace or transform tasks, the existing settlement between workers, firms and government comes under pressure, raising questions about retraining, income support and the allocation of productivity gains.
  - Debate centres on whether technological change will create as many jobs as it removes, how transition costs fall on different groups, and what policy instruments such as education, taxation and welfare reform are needed. The concept links technical questions about AI capability to political questions about distribution and legitimacy.
