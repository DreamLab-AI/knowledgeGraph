A spaceflight experiment is a scientific investigation designed to examine how spaceflight affects living systems or physical processes, flown on a platform such as the International Space Station or compared with ground-based work that mimics aspects of spaceflight.[^1]

NASA's Space Biology programme states its main objective as understanding how spaceflight affects living systems, in spacecraft or in ground-based experiments that mimic aspects of spaceflight, and preparing for future exploration missions.[^1] The programme covers animal, cell and molecular, microbial and plant biology.[^1]

NASA's publication on translational cell and animal research in space records, for each experiment, the investigator team, the research subjects, the ground-based controls and the flight hardware used. This shows that a flown experiment is usually reported together with a defined comparison arm and its hardware, not as a flight sample alone.[^2]

No single standard design for control groups is set out in these sources, so the details of ground comparison should be taken from the individual experiment record.
