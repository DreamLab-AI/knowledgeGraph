- ## Overview
	- Blockchain technology faces increasing scrutiny due to the high energy consumption of consensus mechanisms like Proof-of-Work (PoW), raising concerns about environmental sustainability. Sustainable consensus algorithms significantly reduce energy consumption while maintaining network security and decentralization.
- ## Technical Details
	- ### Proof-of-Stake (PoS)
		- Validators selected based on the quantity of cryptocurrency holdings staked as collateral
		- Eliminates need for energy-intensive computational competition
		- Ethereum's transition to PoS in September 2022 reduced yearly energy use by 99.98%
		- Estimated 11 million tons of CO2eq reduction from Ethereum's transition
	- ### Other Sustainable Mechanisms
		- **Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS)**: Token holders delegate voting power to elected validators
		- **Proof-of-Authority (PoA)**: Validators are pre-approved based on identity and reputation
		- **Robust Proof-of-Stake (RPoS)**: Limits coin age to prevent accumulation attacks and Nothing-at-Stake attacks
	- ### Energy Comparison
		- PoW: Approximately 80 TWh/year (Ethereum pre-transition)
		- PoS: Below 0.01 TWh/year (Ethereum post-transition)
		- Energy reduction equivalent to Belgium's yearly energy consumption
- ## Applications
	- Enterprise blockchain deployments requiring low carbon footprint
	- Environmentally-conscious cryptocurrency networks
	- Scalable blockchain platforms with faster transaction processing
	- Climate change mitigation in distributed ledger technology
