- ### Definition
  - Sustainable consensus mechanisms prioritise environmental efficiency and ecological impact reduction in [[BlockchainTechnology|blockchain]] systems, contrasting with energy-intensive [[ProofOfWork|Proof of Work]] approaches through alternatives like [[ProofOfStake|Proof of Stake]], [[DelegatedProofOfStake|Delegated Proof of Stake]], and [[ProofOfAuthority|Proof of Authority]]. These mechanisms achieve distributed agreement and security whilst consuming orders of magnitude less electricity, addressing climate concerns that have restricted cryptocurrency adoption and institutional investment. Major [[BlockchainPlatforms|blockchain platforms]] including [[Ethereum|Ethereum]], [[Cardano]], and [[Solana|Solana]] have transitioned to sustainable consensus models or launched with efficient designs, balancing decentralisation, security, and environmental responsibility.
