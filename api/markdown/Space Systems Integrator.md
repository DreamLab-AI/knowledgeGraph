A space systems integrator is an organisation that, in the sources used here, brings together elements from different suppliers into a working space system.[^1][^2]

ESA's Industry Space Days material refers to large system integrators as a group within the European space industry, next to start-up companies.[^1] In ESA flight-model development, elements from industry across Europe are received and integrated by the prime contractor, and electrical and software tests follow integration.[^2]

The UK Enterprise Space Category lists spacecraft manufacturing and integration as a category, which shows the activity is recognised in procurement even though the page does not define the role.[^3] A UK Government supply chain note says buyers have poor visibility of supply chain structure and have relied on primes to manage it, which is one reason integration responsibility matters to them.[^4]

None of the retained sources defines the term. Whether the integrator and the prime contractor are one organisation depends on the programme, so the two are treated as related roles and not as synonyms.[^2][^4]
