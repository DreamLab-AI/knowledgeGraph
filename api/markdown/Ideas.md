## Definition

An **Idea** is a cognitive construct representing a unit of thought that can be conceived, communicated, and evaluated. Ideas serve as mental surrogates for reasoning about possibilities and solutions, enabling knowledge workers to explore conceptual spaces without committing to implementation.

## Characteristics

### Essential Properties
- **Abstractness**: Independent of physical instantiation
- **Communicability**: Expressible through language or symbols
- **Evaluability**: Subject to assessment and refinement
- **Combinability**: Capable of synthesis with other ideas

### Ideation Framework Components
1. **Generation**: Creative processes producing novel ideas
2. **Capture**: Documentation and representation methods
3. **Evaluation**: Assessment against criteria and constraints
4. **Development**: Refinement and elaboration cycles
