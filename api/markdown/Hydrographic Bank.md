Hydrographic Bank is the sloping ground that borders a stream and confines the water in its natural channel at normal water level or flow.[^1]

That wording is the USGS glossary definition of bank.[^1] SWEET carries a class named bank as a subclass of hydrosphere feature, with no prose definition, which is why this page is bounded as a hydrosphere feature of the channel margin.[^2]

The USGS glossary also defines bank storage as the change in the amount of water stored in an aquifer adjacent to a surface-water body, resulting from a change in stage of that body.[^1] That entry shows the bank as an interface between surface water and groundwater, but it defines a storage process and not the landform.[^1]

Retained sources define bank for streams only. They say nothing about coastal or seabed banks, nor about how a bank line is delineated from satellite imagery.[^1][^2]
