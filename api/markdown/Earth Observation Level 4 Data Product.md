An Earth observation Level 4 data product is model output or the result of analyses of lower-level data, such as variables derived from multiple measurements.[^1][^2]

CEOS describes Level 4 variables as not directly measured by the instruments but derived from their measurements, possibly from several instruments.[^2] The Metrologia review notes that some applications require gridded data that are complete, and that because Level 2 and Level 3 products are not gap free, Level 4 production requires spatio-temporal interpolation, with in situ values possibly ingested.[^3]

NASA says that many Earth Observing System instruments have Level 4 products and that some interdisciplinary science investigations also generated them.[^1] Level 4 is therefore a mixed class, covering both assimilation or model-based analyses and other derived results.[^1][^3]

Retained sources do not establish a standard uncertainty treatment for Level 4 products, so users should check how each product combines its inputs.[^1][^3]
