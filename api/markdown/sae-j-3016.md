- ### Definition
  - A standard published by SAE International defining six levels of driving automation, from no automation to full automation, used as a common reference for vehicle autonomy.

- ### Content
  - SAE J3016 defines a taxonomy of driving automation across six levels, numbered zero to five, distinguishing the respective roles of the human driver and the automated system in performing the dynamic driving task. The levels clarify when a human must supervise and when the system is responsible.
  - The standard provides shared terminology used by manufacturers, regulators and researchers, and it is widely cited in discussions of advanced driver assistance and autonomous vehicle capabilities.
