Cislunar space radiation environment is the radiation environment of a cislunar space environment, as defined in NASA's Space Life Sciences Ontology.[^1]

Situated under space radiation environment, the class is marked deprecated and contains no dose values.[^1] Galactic cosmic rays, solar particle events and coronal mass ejections are hazards for crews beyond Earth's field and atmosphere, NASA states.[^2]

NASA briefings and workshop material place cislunar missions and deep space missions outside Earth's magnetosphere. Dose figures given depend on mission duration and solar minimum conditions.[^3] Crew monitoring with a worn dosimeter tracks exposure during operations, with separate environment alerting and trending systems.[^4] No sources define a regional dose for cislunar space.[^3][^4]
