- ### Definition
- The [[Russell Group]] is an association of leading UK research-intensive universities that advocates collectively on policy and funding, sitting within the wider domain of [[Education]]. It enables [[Collaboration]] between member institutions, engages with [[Governance]] of higher-education policy, and aligns with shared [[Standards]] of research quality.
- ### Overview
- The Russell Group represents a set of universities distinguished by the scale and intensity of their research activity.
- The association lobbies government and funding bodies, coordinates shared positions, and promotes the interests of research-led higher education.
- Membership is widely treated as a marker of research reputation, influencing rankings, recruitment, and partnerships.
- Member universities span the United Kingdom and maintain extensive international and industry collaborations.
- ### Key aspects
- Research intensity: members produce a large share of UK research output and doctoral training.

- Reputation signal: membership is used as shorthand for high research standing.
