- ### Definition
  - ISO 14064 is a three-part international standard series specifying principles and requirements for the quantification, monitoring, reporting, and independent verification of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and removals at organisational level (Part 1), at project level (Part 2), and for validation and verification bodies (Part 3). The standard provides a framework for organisations to account for their Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions in a manner that is auditable by third-party verification bodies, and it underpins voluntary carbon markets, corporate sustainability reporting obligations, and blockchain-based carbon credit tokenisation schemes.

- ### Content
  ISO 14064 was first published in 2006 and substantially revised in 2018 to align with the GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard and to reflect the expanded scope of Scope 3 (value-chain) emissions. The three parts form an integrated framework:

  Part 1 (Organisation level) specifies how to design, develop, manage, and report an inventory of GHG sources and sinks across operational boundaries. Organisations choose between equity-share and operational-control consolidation approaches to define their reporting boundary, and must apply consistent methodologies for activity data collection, emission factors, and uncertainty quantification.

  Part 2 (Project level) covers methodologies for quantifying the GHG reductions or removal enhancements achieved by specific mitigation projects—such as reforestation, methane capture, or industrial process efficiency—relative to a counterfactual baseline. This part underpins the issuance of Carbon Credits in voluntary and compliance markets. Third Party Auditor bodies accredited under ISO 17029 (Conformity Assessment for validation and verification) perform independent assurance, issuing verification statements that satisfy Carbon Accounting requirements.

  Part 3 specifies competence and process requirements for verification bodies. ISO 14064 sits within the broader ISO 14000 environmental management family and complements Environmental Standards such as ISO 14001 (EMS) and the TCFD framework for climate-related financial disclosures. ESG Reporting frameworks—including GRI, CDP, and the emerging ISSB standards—reference ISO 14064 as the preferred methodology for GHG inventory assurance, and blockchain-based carbon credit tokenisation projects increasingly require ISO 14064-compliant verification as a condition of token issuance.
