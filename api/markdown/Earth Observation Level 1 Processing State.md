Earth observation Level 1 is the processing state in which raw instrument data have been reconstructed and annotated or converted towards instrument or physical units, ahead of derived geophysical variables.[^1]

In the NASA scheme Level 1 covers Level 1A, Level 1B and Level 1C data. Level 1A is time-referenced, annotated instrument data, Level 1B is Level 1A data processed to instrument units, and Level 2 is where derived geophysical variables begin.[^1]

Mission usage varies. Sentinel-2 runs Level-1A and Level-1B stages, which generate top-of-atmosphere calibrated radiance, and then a Level-1C stage that applies geometric correction including orthorectification. Sentinel-1 Level-1 products are focused SAR data, with single look complex products in slant-range geometry and ground range detected products projected to ground range.[^2][^3] The SAR Handbook treats single look complex products as Level 1 and abbreviates this as L1.[^4]

This label alone does not fix the corrections applied.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
