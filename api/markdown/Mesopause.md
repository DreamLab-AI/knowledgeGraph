Within the layered atmosphere, the mesopause is the boundary between the mesosphere and the thermosphere, and it marks the coldest region of the atmosphere.[^1][^2] UCAR names the boundary between the mesosphere and the thermosphere as the mesopause, and NASA calls it the coldest part of the entire atmosphere.[^1][^3]

The Australian Antarctic Division adds that the altitude of the mesopause varies with season and latitude, and the draft ISO text gives an altitude of 80 km to 90 km.[^2][^4] SWEET classes the mesopause as a planetary boundary and sets it as the upper boundary of the mesosphere.[^5]

Retained sources disagree on the altitude and on the minimum temperature, so this page states no temperature, and they do not establish how the boundary is measured.[^2][^4]
