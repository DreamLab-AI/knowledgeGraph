A telethermometer is a class of thermometer in the SWEET instrument ontology.[^1]

SWEET gives the subclass relation to Thermometer and no further definition. Going by the name alone, one might expect a thermometer that is read from a distance, but the source opened for this page does not say so, so how a telethermometer senses or transmits temperature is not established here.[^1]

Satellite thermal sensing is outside this page. This ontology entry does not connect the term to the sensing of emitted radiation from space.[^1]

[^1]: ESIP Federation, SWEET ontology instrument module, *matrInstrument.ttl*. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ESIPFed/sweet/master/src/matrInstrument.ttl
