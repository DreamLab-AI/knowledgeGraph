### Technical Details
Key tax treatment aspects include:
- **Property Classification**: IRS treats cryptocurrency as property, not currency
- **Taxable Events**: Sales, exchanges, spending, receiving as payment
- **Non-Taxable**: Buying with fiat, wallet transfers, gifting (under limits)
- **Cost Basis Methods**: FIFO, LIFO, specific identification

### Capital Gains Rates

**Short-Term (held 1 year or less)**
Taxed at ordinary income rates: 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, or 37%

**Long-Term (held over 1 year)**
Preferential rates: 0%, 15%, or 20% based on taxable income

### Income Tax Situations
- Mining rewards: Ordinary income at fair market value when received
- Staking rewards: Taxable as income when received
- Airdrops: Taxable income when airdrop has value
- Payment for services: Ordinary income

### 2025 Reporting Changes
- **Form 1099-DA**: Brokers required to report gross proceeds starting 2025
- **Cost Basis Reporting**: Required from 2026
- **Digital Asset Question**: Mandatory checkbox on Form 1040

### Tax Loss Harvesting
Losses can offset gains and up to $3,000 of ordinary income per year. Unused losses roll forward indefinitely. Note: Wash sale rules may apply in future guidance.
