- ## Technical Details
	- **Key Regulations**:
		- **EU MiCA** (December 2024): Single ruleset for 27 EU countries, licensing requirements, stablecoin reserve backing, white paper disclosures
		- **US CLARITY Act**: CFTC authority over digital commodities
		- **US GENIUS Act**: Stablecoin reserve requirements, monthly audits, AML compliance, consumer priority in bankruptcy
		- **California Digital Financial Assets Law** (July 2026): Licensing requirements via DFPI
	- **Consumer Risks**:
		- FBI reported $9.3B lost to cryptocurrency scams in 2024 (US only)
		- No comprehensive federal authority over crypto spot markets
		- Pseudonymity enabling money laundering and illicit finance
	- **Global Landscape**:
		- Legal in 45 countries, partially banned in 20, generally banned in 10 (Atlantic Council study)
		- South Korea Virtual Asset Users Protection Act (2023)
		- Brazil Cryptoassets Act (June 2023): Central bank supervision
	- **Protection Mechanisms**: Tax policy, AML/CTF requirements, licensing, disclosure obligations
- ## Applications
	- Cryptocurrency exchange regulation
	- Stablecoin issuer oversight
	- ICO and token sale compliance
	- DeFi protocol consumer safeguards
	- Cross-border payment protection
