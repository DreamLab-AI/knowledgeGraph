An enforcement action is a formal measure taken by a regulatory or supervisory authority against an entity that has violated laws, rules, or licence conditions. It can include fines, cease-and-desist orders, licence revocation, restitution to affected parties, or referral for prosecution. Enforcement actions are the operative mechanism by which consumer-protection and market-conduct regulations are made binding.

### Content

- Actions range from monetary penalties to operating bans and are usually preceded by investigation and notice. Their deterrent and corrective effect underpins the credibility of consumer-protection and financial-conduct frameworks.
