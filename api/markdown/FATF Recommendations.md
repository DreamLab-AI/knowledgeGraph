## 2024 Updates

### Compliance Status
- 75% of assessed jurisdictions only partially compliant or non-compliant (June 2024)
- Jurisdictions with materially important VA sectors show better compliance
- 67 jurisdictions tracked covering 98% of global VASP activity

### Key Challenges
- Insufficient progress implementing Travel Rule
- Struggles with risk assessment and supervisory inspections
- Varying levels of VASP registration and licensing

### Progress Areas
- Increased jurisdictions registering/licensing VASPs
- Some improvements in core measure implementation
- Growing regulatory clarity in major markets

## Recommendation 15 Requirements

### VASP Obligations
- Customer due diligence (CDD)
- Record keeping
- Suspicious transaction reporting (STR)
- Same preventive measures as financial institutions

### Travel Rule
- Information sharing between VASPs
- Originator and beneficiary data transmission
- Transaction traceability requirements

## Emerging Risks (2024)

### Criminal Use Patterns
- Growing stablecoin use by criminal networks
- North Korea-linked networks (USD 1.5 billion Bybit hack)
- Terrorist financing activities
- Drug trafficking operations

### Fraud and Scams
- USD 50+ billion illicit on-chain activity attributed to fraud (2024)
- Sharp rise in fraud schemes
- Increasing sophistication of scams

## Jurisdictional Approaches

### Prohibition Approach
- China, Egypt, Saudi Arabia: Explicit VA/VASP prohibition
- Seychelles, Indonesia: Prohibition in progress

### Regulatory Approach
- Major jurisdictions implementing licensing regimes
- Risk-based supervision frameworks
- Ongoing compliance monitoring

## Implementation Guidance

### FATF Recommendations
- All countries must implement standards effectively
- Prevent criminals and terrorists exploiting weak supervision
- Regular jurisdictional reviews and updates

### Best Practices
- Comprehensive risk assessments
- Robust supervisory frameworks
- International cooperation mechanisms
- Technology-neutral regulations
