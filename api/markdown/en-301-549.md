- ### Definition
  - EN 301 549 is the European standard defining ICT [[Accessibility]] requirements across web, software, hardware, and documents. It implements and standardises accessibility obligations, harmonised with WCAG.
- ### Content
  - The standard sets functional accessibility criteria and is mandated for public-sector procurement under the EU Web Accessibility Directive and the European Accessibility Act. By aligning with WCAG success criteria it gives suppliers a single, testable conformance target across the European market.
