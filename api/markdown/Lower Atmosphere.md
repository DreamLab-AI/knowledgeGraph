In atmospheric usage, the lower atmosphere is the lowest layer of the atmosphere, usually identified with the troposphere, in which weather occurs.[^1][^2] NOAA describes the troposphere as the layer closest to the Earth, where temperature decreases with height, and states that this layer is known as the lower atmosphere.[^1]

The Met Office puts the top of the troposphere between about 9 km and 17 km altitude depending on latitude and weather conditions, and says nearly all weather takes place within it.[^2] SWEET types Lower Atmosphere as a subclass of its atmosphere layer class and gives it no definition.[^3]

None of the retained sources fixes an upper boundary for the term, and they use it for the troposphere rather than for any larger lower portion of the atmosphere.[^1][^2]
