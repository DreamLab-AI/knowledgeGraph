A category of artificial intelligence development and deployment strategies designed to augment human labor and create new tasks rather than primarily displacing existing jobs.

### Overview

- **[Industry analysis]** MIT professors Daron Acemoglu, David Autor, and Simon Johnson argue that the only unambiguously pro-worker category of technological change is 'new task creating technologies.' *(Source: Daron Acemoglu, David Autor, Simon Johnson, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
- **[Industry analysis]** The authors of 'Building Pro-Worker Artificial Intelligence' argue that the market is not capitalizing on pro-worker AI opportunities due to misaligned firm incentives, the 'AGI bet,' and a time horizon problem where automation solutions are already market-ready. *(Source: Daron Acemoglu, David Autor, Simon Johnson, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
