- ### Definition
  - Organisational and technological efforts reducing blockchain energy consumption through [[ProofOfStake]], [[Layer2Scaling]], renewable energy sourcing, and carbon offset programmes. Green initiatives address blockchain's environmental impact by transitioning from [[ProofOfWork]] consensus mechanisms consuming significant electricity to more efficient alternatives, deploying [[ZeroKnowledgeProof]] and other privacy techniques reducing computational overhead, and incentivising infrastructure operators to utilise renewable energy sources. Initiatives combine technical innovation with market-based mechanisms like [[CarbonCredits]] and sustainability certifications.
