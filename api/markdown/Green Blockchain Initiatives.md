## Energy Consumption Context

### Current Impact
- US cryptocurrency mining: 0.6-2.3% of national electricity
- Equivalent to 3-6 million homes
- Bitcoin alone: ~150 terawatt-hours annually
- Exceeds Argentina's total consumption (45M population)

### The Merge Impact
- Ethereum transition to PoS (September 2022)
- 99.95% energy reduction achieved
- Model for sustainable blockchain transformation
- Industry-wide influence

## Green Consensus Mechanisms

### Proof of Stake (PoS)
- Validator selection by stake holding
- Minimal computational requirements
- Cardano Ouroboros protocol
- Energy-efficient validation

### Alternative Mechanisms
- Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)
- Proof of History (PoH) - Solana
- Proof of Space and Time - Chia
- Hybrid consensus approaches
