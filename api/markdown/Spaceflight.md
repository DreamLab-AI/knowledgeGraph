Spaceflight is activity carried out by crewed or robotic vehicles outside the Earth’s atmosphere.[^1]

No universally accepted definition of where space begins exists in the ontology, so the edge of the activity depends on the definition used.[^1] Orbital flight, in which a spacecraft is placed on a trajectory fast enough to enter orbit around the Earth, differs from suborbital flight, in which a spacecraft reaches space but cannot achieve orbit according to the Federal Aviation Administration glossary.[^2]

These sources do not establish a single boundary altitude, and the FAA terms are given for its licensing context rather than as a universal classification.[^1][^2]

[^1]: Space Life Sciences Ontology, “space flight” (SLSO_0000055). http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/SLSO_0000055
[^2]: FAA, “Human Space Flight”. https://www.faa.gov/space/human_spaceflight
