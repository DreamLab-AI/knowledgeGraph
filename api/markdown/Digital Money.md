  - **Forms of Digital Money**:

		- CBDCs: Central bank issued digital currency
		- Cryptocurrencies: Decentralized, blockchain-based
		- Stablecoins: Value-pegged digital assets

		- $23 trillion trading volume in 2024 (90% increase)
