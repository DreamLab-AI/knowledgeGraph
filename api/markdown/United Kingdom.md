
The United Kingdom is a sovereign state in north-west Europe comprising England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, with a parliamentary system of government and a significant role in global finance, research, and technology policy. Its regulatory bodies and cities, including Manchester and Newcastle, participate in national initiatives spanning industrial strategy, financial services, and digital infrastructure. The UK's legal and regulatory framework governs data protection, financial services, and emerging technology standards within its jurisdiction.
