- ## Technical Details
	- **Key Regulations**:
		- **EU MiCA** (December 2024): Unified crypto framework across 27 EU countries
		- **EU Transfer of Funds Regulation**: Extends Travel Rule to VASPs, requires EDD on third-country counterparties
		- **FATF Recommendation 16**: Global Travel Rule standard
	- **Global Adoption**:
		- 65 of 94 jurisdictions passed Travel Rule legislation (April 2024)
		- 15 jurisdictions in progress
		- J5 coalition (Australia, Canada, Netherlands, UK, US) combating tax evasion
	- **Cross-Border Challenges**:
		- Stablecoin activities inherently cross-border via internet access
		- Jurisdictional ambiguity on applicable laws
		- Fragmented and insufficient international coordination
		- Stablecoins projected multi-trillion market by 2030 ($32T volume in 2024)
	- **International Bodies**: IOSCO (18 recommendations), FSB, FATF
- ## Applications
	- Cryptocurrency exchange licensing
	- Cross-border payment compliance
	- Travel Rule implementation
	- International stablecoin operations
	- Multi-jurisdictional VASP registration
