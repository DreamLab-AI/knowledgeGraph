### Technical Details
Key frameworks include:
- **FSB Framework**: Global standards for crypto-asset activities and stablecoin arrangements
- **G20 Roadmap**: Policy implementation, capacity building, coordination, data gaps
- **FATF Travel Rule**: 65 of 94 jurisdictions passed legislation as of April 2024
- **MiCA**: EU-wide harmonized framework effective December 2024

### International Coordination Bodies

**FSB (Financial Stability Board)**
Thematic reviews show progress but significant gaps and inconsistencies. Uneven implementation creates regulatory arbitrage opportunities.

**J5 Coalition**
Australia, Canada, Netherlands, UK, and US tax authorities collaborate on international tax evasion and money laundering cases involving crypto.

**FATF**
Sets global AML/CFT standards with Travel Rule threshold of $1,000/EUR for cross-border virtual asset transfers.

### Challenges
- Regulatory fragmentation across jurisdictions
- Inconsistent implementation timelines
- Stablecoin risks in emerging markets
- Cross-border enforcement coordination gaps

### FSB Recommendations
- Seamless information exchange between jurisdictions
- Collaborative enforcement for bad actors
- Standardized frameworks to prevent fragmentation
- Alignment with international standards

### Emerging Market Risks
FSB analysis shows EMDEs exposed to macro-financial risks from foreign currency-pegged stablecoins, potentially destabilizing financial flows and straining fiscal resources.
