OHB SE is a publicly traded aerospace and technology group headquartered in Bremen, which describes its activities as ranging from complete satellite systems and space missions to infrastructure for access to space and data-driven digital services.[^1]

OHB reports three business segments: Space Systems, Access to Space and Digital, and approximately 4,000 employees.[^1][^2] Access to Space provides infrastructure for European launch sites and is described as a major supplier to the European aerospace industry. Digital combines ground segment development and the operation of space systems with IT applications based on satellite data.[^2]

ESA awarded OHB a contract valued at over €280 million to build the two Harmony Earth Explorer satellites, with OHB System AG as prime contractor and Thales Alenia Space and ABB as subcontractors for the radar and thermal-infrared instruments.[^3] A 2010 release from Surrey Satellite Technology Ltd says OHB-System was to be prime contractor for the 14 Galileo satellites chosen by the European Commission, with responsibility for the platform and overall integration, while Surrey Satellite Technology built and integrated the navigation payloads.[^4]

The Harmony contract names OHB System AG, a group member, rather than OHB SE. The Galileo release is from 2010 and does not cover later contract extensions. Employee numbers and positioning are company statements as of the retrieval date.
