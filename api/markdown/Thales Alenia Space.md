Thales Alenia Space is a space systems company that describes itself as a joint venture between Thales and Leonardo, with shareholdings stated as 67% and 33%.[^1][^2]

According to the company, it forms the Space Alliance with Telespazio, a partnership covering satellite manufacturing through to services. It also describes itself as working with start-ups, SMEs and research centres across the space industry supply chain. These are company statements; the retained pages give no independent ranking of its market position.[^1][^3]

In June 2026 the company announced a contract with ESA for the development of two Sentinel-1 Next Generation satellites, described as the first tranche of a larger contract with a stated total of 700 million euros. The press release title calls the company prime contractor. The text retained here does not establish later tranches, delivery dates or the split of work with subcontractors.[^2]

Confusion can arise between this company name, the Space Alliance (which also includes Telespazio), and its parent companies. Ownership figures are as stated on the company pages at retrieval and may change.[^1][^3]
