- ## Technical Details
	- **Wyoming Framework** (Effective July 1, 2024):
		- **DAO LLC**: Governed by Wyoming Limited Liability Company Act, formed via Articles of Organization (WY Stat SS 17-31-106)
		- **DUNA**: Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association under SF0050 (Chapter 32)
	- **DUNA Key Features**:
		- Requires minimum 100 members
		- Provides legal existence, tax payment ability, limited liability
		- Members not personally liable for association or other members' actions
		- May convert to corporation, partnership, or LLC
		- Cannot distribute profits or dividends to members
	- **Adoption Examples**:
		- Syndicate Network Collective launched as first operating DUNA (August 2024)
		- Uniswap governance adopted DUNA (September 2025)
		- a16z directing associated DAOs toward DUNA status
	- **Alternatives**: Offshore foundations, traditional LLCs for for-profit token projects
- ## Applications
	- DAO incorporation and legal recognition
	- Member liability protection
	- Third-party contract execution
	- Tax compliance structuring
	- Intellectual property ownership
