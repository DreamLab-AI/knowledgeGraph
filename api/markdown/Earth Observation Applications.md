Earth observation applications are uses of Earth observation data to inform decisions and evidence needs in fields such as agriculture, disasters, water, wildfires, conservation and health.[^1]

NASA's Earth Action programme provides funding, technical expertise and partnerships to help decision-makers apply Earth data to challenges in those fields and others.[^1] The list on its page is illustrative and does not define the full range of applications.

Coordination matters at an international level. The Committee on Earth Observation Satellites has over 60 members and associates who exchange information on Earth observation activities, with outputs aimed at stakeholder needs.[^2] It coordinates activity and is not itself a data provider.

National bodies also use the data for policy evidence. The UK Joint Nature Conservation Committee advises on projects spanning rural payments, water quality monitoring, forestry and habitat assessments.[^3] These pages describe programme practice in the United States and the United Kingdom and do not establish the accuracy or maturity of any individual application.
