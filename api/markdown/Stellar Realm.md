Stellar realm is a grouping class in the SWEET astronomy ontology for the regions and structures that belong to a star, such as its interior, its atmosphere and its active regions.[^1]

In that ontology the stellar realm is itself a subclass of a general astronomical realm. The stellar interior, the stellar atmosphere and the active region are classed beneath it, and the corona and the stellar core are placed further down the same hierarchy.[^1] The label is a structural device of the ontology rather than a term with its own observational definition in the retained sources.

For the Sun, a concrete picture of the layering emerges: NASA lists its interior regions as the core, the radiative zone and the convection zone, and its atmosphere as the photosphere, chromosphere, transition zone and corona.[^2]

Neither the retained sources nor the ontology file make claims about every star having all these parts: measurements are absent from the ontology records, so no statements about stars other than the Sun are made here.
