Planetary science is the study of the objects of the solar system, which NASA describes as aimed at understanding the history of the solar system and the distribution of life within it.[^1]

NASA states that its robotic explorers gather data on how planets formed, what led to different evolutionary paths among them, and which processes have occurred and remain active.[^1] The work is organised around individual bodies, and NASA publishes separate descriptions of the Moon, Mars, Jupiter’s moon Io and Saturn.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

These sources are programme and explanatory pages. They do not give a formal disciplinary definition or a list of sub-fields, so the scope described here is NASA’s framing rather than a settled boundary with astronomy or Earth science.
