### Technical Details
Key legal structure options include:
- **Wyoming DUNA**: Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association providing legal recognition for DAOs
- **Foundation Model**: Non-profit foundations (Cayman, Swiss, or Delaware) holding DAO assets
- **LLC Wrapper**: Limited Liability Company providing liability protection for members
- **Hybrid Structures**: Combinations of on-chain governance with off-chain legal entities

### Jurisdiction Considerations
- **Wyoming (USA)**: First state to recognize DAOs as legal entities through DAO LLC legislation
- **Marshall Islands**: DAO Act 2022 recognizing DAOs as domestic entities
- **Switzerland**: Foundation structures supporting decentralized governance
- **Cayman Islands**: Foundation companies used by major DeFi protocols

### Benefits of Legal Wrappers
- **Limited Liability**: Protects individual members from personal liability
- **Contract Capability**: Enables DAOs to enter binding legal agreements
- **Bank Accounts**: Access to traditional financial services
- **Regulatory Interface**: Clear entity for regulatory engagement

### Challenges
- **Jurisdictional Conflicts**: DAOs operate globally but legal entities are jurisdiction-specific
- **Governance Mapping**: Translating token voting to legally recognized decision-making
- **Tax Complexity**: Unclear tax treatment across jurisdictions
- **Ongoing Compliance**: Maintaining legal entity requirements alongside decentralized operations
