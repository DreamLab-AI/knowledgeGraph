### Technical Details
Key implementation approaches include:
- **Digital Product Identities**: Unique tokens or digital twins assigned to products or batches
- **Event Recording**: Each transformation, shipment, or inspection creates new blockchain transactions
- **Supply Chain Actor Connections**: Digital certificates linking manufacturers, distributors, retailers
- **Chain of Custody**: Continuous auditable history from source to consumer

### Industry Applications
- **Food Safety**: Walmart tracks fresh produce from farm to store using blockchain
- **Pharmaceuticals**: MediLedger Project reduces fraud and counterfeiting in drug supply chains
- **Jewelry**: Everledger creates digital identities for diamonds verifying origin and authenticity
- **Apparel**: Tracking cotton, leather, and materials for sustainability verification

### Market Growth
The global blockchain market was estimated at $20.16 billion in 2024, forecasted to reach $393.42 billion by 2032 at 43.65% CAGR. Demand for tracking inventory, asset provenance, and end-to-end traceability drives specialized blockchain adoption.

### Government Initiatives
NIST NCCoE is developing reference implementations for manufacturing supply chain traceability. These Minimal Viable Products support experimental supply chain ecosystems to explore practical mechanics of sharing traceability information across industries.
