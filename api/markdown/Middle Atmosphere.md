Collectively, the stratosphere and the mesosphere taken together are termed the middle atmosphere.[^1][^2] UCAR says the two layers together are sometimes referred to by this name, and the draft ISO text says they span heights from approximately 10 km to 100 km.[^1][^2]

SWEET declares the class equivalent to its ozone layer class, but the Met Office describes the ozone layer as a zone within the stratosphere, so the ozone layer covers only part of the middle atmosphere.[^3][^4] The page follows the atmospheric science sources and treats the SWEET equivalence as a modelling shortcut that the retained sources do not support.[^3][^4]

Whether the thermosphere belongs to the term is not established by the retained sources, which give differing altitudes for its edges.[^1][^2]
