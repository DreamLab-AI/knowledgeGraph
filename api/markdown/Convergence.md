## Overview

Technology convergence in 2025 is no longer a distant vision but a present reality reshaping industries, economies, and human experiences. The integration of AI, blockchain, IoT, metaverse technologies, and digital twins creates new paradigms for manufacturing, healthcare, energy, and smart city infrastructure.

## Key Convergence Areas

### Industrial Metaverse
	- Convergence of physical industrial operations with immersive digital technologies
	- Market projected to exceed $150 billion by 2035
	- Technology stack: VR/AR, IoT sensors, AI, cloud computing, 5G connectivity
	- Major implementations by Siemens, GE, and Boeing achieving 30% design time reduction

### AI and Blockchain
	- Synergistic combination enhancing ICT system performance and efficiency
	- Applications in cybersecurity, energy, smart cities, finance, healthcare
	- Blockchain business value projected to surpass $176 billion by 2025, $3.1 trillion by 2030

### Metaverse and Blockchain
	- Blockchain as key component connecting real and virtual worlds seamlessly
	- Decentralised ownership and authentication of digital assets
	- Secure, transparent transactions in virtual environments

## Cross-Domain Applications
	- **Healthcare**: IoT data collection, AI diagnostics, blockchain-secured medical records
	- **Energy**: IoT-enabled smart grids with blockchain-managed energy transactions
	- **Smart Cities**: AI processing of IoT sensor data with blockchain-secured platform sharing
	- **Manufacturing**: Digital twins with XR interfaces and AI-driven optimisation

## Challenges
	- Data privacy and regulatory compliance across jurisdictions
	- Cybersecurity across interconnected systems
	- Ethical AI usage and accountability
	- Environmental impact of combined technology infrastructure
	- Scalability and trustworthiness of integrated communications
