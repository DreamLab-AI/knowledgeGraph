- ### Definition
  - IEEE VR is the flagship [[Academic Conference]] for [[Virtual Reality]] and 3D user interfaces, sponsored by the [[IEEE]]. It advances [[Immersive Technology]] research and intersects strongly with [[Human-Computer Interaction]].
- ### Overview
  - IEEE VR brings together researchers and practitioners working on virtual, augmented and mixed reality, covering displays, tracking, rendering, interaction and human factors. It is widely regarded as the most influential annual venue for the field.
  - The conference shapes the research direction of spatial computing by curating peer-reviewed papers, journal tracks, workshops and demonstrations, and by recognising seminal contributions through awards.
  - Although it is a conference rather than a formal standards organisation, its association with the IEEE links it to the broader technical-community processes that influence terminology and best practice.
- ### Key aspects
  - Peer review: rigorous selection of full papers and journal-track articles.
  - Breadth: covers perception, displays, locomotion, avatars, haptics and applications.
  - Community: anchors a research community of VR/AR academics and industry labs.
  - Longevity: an established annual series with a long publication record.
  - Cross-disciplinary: bridges graphics, HCI, perception and systems.
- ### Mechanisms
  - Annual call for papers with technical programme committees.
  - Journal-integrated publication through IEEE transactions.
  - Workshops, tutorials and doctoral consortia for community building.
- ### Applications
  - Disseminating advances in head-mounted display and tracking technology.
  - Establishing shared evaluation methods for immersive interaction.
  - Connecting academic research with industry VR/AR development.
