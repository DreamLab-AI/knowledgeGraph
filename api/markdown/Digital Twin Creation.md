The process of constructing virtual replicas of physical entities that dynamically reflect real-time conditions through continuous bidirectional data linkage, enabling simulation, monitoring, and optimisation across an asset's entire lifecycle from design through decommissioning.

## Technical Details

- **Market Growth**:
  - Global market: ~60% annual growth over next five years
  - Projected $73.5B by 2027 (MarketsandMarkets, 2022 forecast)
  - Current market: $3.1B (2020) to $48.2B by 2026
- **Creation Process**:
  - 3D scanning of existing assets (LiDAR, structured-light)
  - Meticulous data collection and analysis
  - Sensor integration for real-time updates
  - AI/ML model training for behavior prediction
- **Digital Twin vs Simulation**:
  - Simulations: Static, predefined scenarios, no real-world connection
  - Digital Twins: Dynamic, real-time conditions, bidirectional data flow
- **Required Technologies**: CAD modeling, cloud computing, IIoT, AR/VR, AI/ML, systems integration

## Applications

- Manufacturing predictive maintenance
- Healthcare surgical planning
- Automotive EV battery simulation
- Smart city traffic optimization
- Product lifecycle management

### Current Landscape (2026)

- OpenUSD has consolidated as the de facto interchange substrate for twin creation: the Alliance for OpenUSD (AOUSD) ratified Core Specification 1.0 in 2025 and shipped four releases through USD 25.08, while standing up an Industrial and Engineering Digital Twin (IEDT) Interest Group to define twin terminology and requirements.
- Generative and neural methods have shifted twin authoring from manual modelling towards synthesis: NVIDIA's Cosmos world foundation models (unveiled at CES, January 2025) generate physically grounded world simulations, and Isaac Sim 5.0 with NuRec RTX-accelerated 3D Gaussian splatting (SIGGRAPH, August 2025) lets developers reconstruct real scenes from sensor data to narrow the sim-to-real gap.
- NVIDIA introduced the Omniverse DSX Blueprint at GTC Washington in October 2025 and made it generally available in March 2026 alongside the Vera Rubin reference design, standardising gigawatt-scale AI-factory digital twins that unify power, cooling and networking simulation on OpenUSD and SimReady assets.
- Siemens launched Digital Twin Composer at CES 2026 (early access), building industrial-metaverse twins on NVIDIA Omniverse libraries and knitting them into a Xcelerator digital thread spanning design, engineering and operations.
- Ecosystem consolidation accelerated: Schneider Electric, AVEVA and ETAP joined AOUSD in November 2025 and Aras in April 2026, alongside Accenture, Esri, PTC, Rockwell Automation and Renault, with AOUSD passing 50 general and 88 contributor members by year-end 2025.
- Twin outputs are increasingly consumed in XR — Omniverse spatial streaming (announced CES 2025) delivers high-fidelity OpenUSD twins to Apple Vision Pro — while cloud availability expanded via Omniverse images on AWS Marketplace (L40S) and Azure Marketplace.
- Market momentum is strong: analysts valued the AI-powered digital twin market at roughly USD 31.5bn in 2025, projected to USD 225.4bn by 2032 (CAGR ~32.6%), underpinned by ISO 23247 (manufacturing) and IEC 62832 (industrial process) standards reducing integration risk.
- Open challenges as of 2026 remain the labour-intensive CAD-to-OpenUSD conversion pipeline, closing the simulation-to-reality gap for physical AI, lifecycle-connected digital threads across siloed PLM/IT/OT systems, and secure real-time IT/OT data fabrics (addressed in part by DSX Exchange).
