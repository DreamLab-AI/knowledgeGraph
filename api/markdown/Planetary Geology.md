Planetary geology is the study of the surfaces and rocky structure of planets, moons and other solid bodies and the processes that shaped them.[^1][^2]

NASA reports that its spacecraft have visited every planet and a variety of small bodies, and that missions will bring back samples that allow detailed study back on Earth.[^1] Such data connect surface geology to the history of the solar system.

Mars gives concrete examples. NASA lists Olympus Mons as the largest volcano in the solar system and describes a rocky mantle and crust beneath the surface features, so surface geology and interior structure are treated together.[^2]

Sources do not give a formal definition of the field or describe geological mapping methods. The lead above is a scoped description drawn from the NASA programme and fact pages.[^1][^2]
