### Technical Details
Key regulatory requirements include:
- **Reserve Backing**: 1:1 asset backing for e-money tokens
- **Redemption Rights**: At-par redemption guarantees for holders
- **Capital Requirements**: Minimum capital for stablecoin issuers
- **Segregation**: Reserve assets held separately from issuer assets

### EU MiCA Framework
- **E-Money Tokens (EMTs)**: Pegged to single fiat currency
- **Asset-Referenced Tokens (ARTs)**: Pegged to multiple assets or commodities
- **Significant Tokens**: Additional requirements for systemically important stablecoins
- **Effective Date**: June 30, 2024 for stablecoin provisions

### Market Impact
Major exchanges delisting non-compliant stablecoins in EU jurisdictions. Binance, Kraken, OKX, and others phasing out tokens not meeting MiCA requirements. USDC obtained EU authorization while USDT faced challenges.

### US Regulatory Landscape
- **Multiple Regulators**: OCC, FDIC, SEC, CFTC all potentially involved
- **State Licensing**: Money transmitter licenses required in many states
- **Proposed Legislation**: Multiple stablecoin bills pending in Congress
- **SEC Scrutiny**: Algorithmic stablecoins facing securities classification questions
