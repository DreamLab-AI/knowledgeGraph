### Technical Details
Key regulatory agencies include:
- **SEC**: Securities classification via Howey Test, registration requirements, enforcement actions
- **CFTC**: Commodities jurisdiction, derivatives oversight, market manipulation enforcement
- **FinCEN**: BSA compliance, money transmitter registration, AML/CFT requirements
- **OCC**: Banking guidance on custody and stablecoin activities

### Legislative Developments (2024)
- **FIT21**: Financial Innovation and Technology Act passed House with bipartisan 279-136 vote
- **Market Structure**: Framework dividing jurisdiction between SEC and CFTC
- **Stablecoin Bills**: Multiple proposals for federal stablecoin oversight
- **SEC Task Force**: Internal crypto task force for workable registration paths

### Enforcement Landscape
The SEC maintains an enforcement-driven approach, with major cases against Coinbase, Ripple, and other platforms. Court rulings continue to shape the definition of securities in the digital asset context, while the CFTC focuses on derivatives markets and fraud prosecution.

### State-Level Requirements
- **New York BitLicense**: Comprehensive virtual currency license since 2015
- **Money Transmitter Licenses**: Required in most states for crypto exchanges
- **State Banking Charters**: Trust company charters for custody operations
- **Varying Standards**: Patchwork of requirements across 50 states
