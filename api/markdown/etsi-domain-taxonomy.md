- ### Definition
  - The ETSI Domain Taxonomy is a hierarchical classification framework produced by the European Telecommunications Standards Institute for systematically organising AI-related technical specifications, standards, and work items across application domains such as transportation, healthcare, telecommunications, and public safety. It provides a controlled vocabulary and structural ontology that enables consistent referencing of AI standards across ETSI technical committees and supports cross-domain applicability analysis. The taxonomy underpins ETSI's AI standardisation roadmap and facilitates alignment with CEN-CENELEC and ISO/IEC standardisation activities.

- ### Content
  - The ETSI Domain Taxonomy emerged from ETSI's effort to provide a coherent map of AI application areas to guide its technical committees in identifying gaps and overlaps in existing standardisation coverage. The taxonomy organises domains hierarchically—from broad sectors such as healthcare and transport down to specific application categories such as medical imaging diagnostics or autonomous vehicle perception—allowing standards to be tagged with multiple domain affiliations.
  - Each domain entry in the taxonomy is linked to relevant ETSI technical standards, ETSI Group Reports, and external standards from bodies such as ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, enabling users to navigate from a domain of interest to the applicable standardisation landscape. This cross-referencing function is particularly useful for manufacturers seeking to identify which harmonised standards apply to their AI system under the EU AI Act.
  - The taxonomy is a living document updated as new AI application domains mature and new ETSI working groups are established. It interfaces with knowledge representation tools including controlled vocabularies and structured ontologies, reflecting ETSI's increasing engagement with semantic web technologies to make its standardisation artefacts machine-readable and interoperable with regulatory information systems. Alignment with the CEN-CENELEC AI standardisation work programme ensures coherence across the European standards ecosystem.
