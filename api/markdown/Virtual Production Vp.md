- ### Definition
  - A modern filmmaking methodology that combines traditional production techniques with real-time computer graphics, LED volumes, game engines, and motion capture technology to create in-camera visual effects, replacing traditional green screen compositing with immersive digital environments displayed on large LED walls during live production.

- ### Content

  - ## Overview
  - Virtual production merges real-time computer graphics, live-action footage, and performance capture to produce scenes where actors and directors interact with virtual environments on set. Unlike traditional green screen setups, VP environments are displayed in real time on LED volumes, creating immersive in-camera visual effects. The VP industry is projected to reach over $11 billion by 2034. In July 2025, the world's largest LED virtual production volume was unveiled in China, spanning 50 meters in diameter with over 1,700 square meters of LED display.
  - ## Technical Details
  - ### Core Components
		- **LED Wall/Volume**: Wraparound LED display projecting digital environments behind actors
		- **Game Engine**: Typically Unreal Engine for real-time rendering synced with camera movement
		- **Camera Tracking**: Optical markers, inertial sensors, or inside-out tracking for 3D positioning
		- **Performance Capture**: Motion capture integration for character animation
  - ### Key Benefits
		- **In-Camera VFX**: Reduces post-production compositing requirements
		- **Natural Lighting**: LED backgrounds provide realistic illumination on actors
		- **Real-Time Iteration**: Instant background changes and creative decisions during filming
		- **Cost Reduction**: Eliminates location travel and unpredictable conditions
  - ### Technical Challenges
		- Maximum 240 fps for slow-motion shooting limitations
		- HDR color space rendering challenges on LED panels
		- Moire effect in wide shots
		- High initial setup costs prohibitive for smaller budgets
  - ## Applications
  - Feature film and episodic television production
  - Commercial and advertising content creation
  - Live broadcast graphics integration
  - Music video production
  - Virtual location scouting and pre-visualization
