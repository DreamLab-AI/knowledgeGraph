The organizational governance structure that assigns specific responsibility and oversight for AI strategy, risk, and value realization to C-suite leadership, such as the CEO or CTO.

### Overview

- KPMG's quarterly pulse survey found that organizations where CEOs were accountable for AI were more than twice as likely to report meaningful business value from AI usage compared to those where CEOs were not accountable. *(Source: Host (AI Daily Brief), via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
