### Technical Details
Key jurisdictions include:
- **Singapore (MAS)**: Stringent DPT regulations since April 2024 with mandatory custody segregation, external audits, and slower licensing; robust stablecoin framework since August 2023
- **Hong Kong (SFC/HKMA)**: Crypto licenses since June 2023, stablecoin issuer sandbox, spot crypto ETF approvals, and financial transparency requirements per Mantra Dao ruling (2024)
- **Japan (FSA)**: Mature framework with strict licensing, segregation, and onshore custody; expanding to institutional staking and crypto ETF pathways

### Regulatory Approaches
70% of 24 reviewed jurisdictions made progress implementing regulations in 2024. Countries extend existing financial regulations to encompass crypto-assets, aligning oversight with traditional frameworks.

### 2024-2025 Developments
Regional policy momentum accelerated driven by market growth, competitive dynamics, and spillovers from Western jurisdictions. Singapore, Hong Kong, and Japan building comprehensive frameworks integrating blockchain into the real economy.
