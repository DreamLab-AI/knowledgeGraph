- ### Definition
  - An architecture decision record numbered 008, documenting a specific architectural choice within a project together with its context, options considered, and consequences. ADRs form a chronological log of design decisions.

- ### Content
  - Architecture decision records are short documents that capture a single significant decision, its rationale, and the alternatives weighed. Numbering them sequentially produces an auditable history of how a system's design evolved.
  - A record such as ADR-008 references the eighth decision in such a series. The format helps teams understand why earlier choices were made and supports revisiting decisions when circumstances change.
