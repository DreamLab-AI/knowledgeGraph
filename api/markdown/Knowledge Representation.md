### Definition
Knowledge representation is the field of artificial intelligence concerned with representing information about the world in a form that computer systems can utilize to solve complex tasks. It involves structuring and encoding knowledge using formal languages, semantic networks, frames, rules, or logic-based systems to enable machines to reason, infer, and make decisions based on stored information.
