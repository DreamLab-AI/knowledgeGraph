Parameterize is the act of representing a process that a model cannot treat explicitly by a small set of parameters, as the meteorological and climate-modelling literature uses the term.[^1][^2]

The American Meteorological Society describes parameterization as the representation, in a dynamic model, of physical effects in terms of admittedly oversimplified parameters, rather than requiring such effects to emerge from the dynamics of the system.[^1] The IPCC glossary narrows this to processes that cannot be explicitly resolved at the spatial or temporal resolution of the model, which are replaced by relationships with model-resolved larger-scale variables.[^2]

NASA describes the higher processing levels as converting data into more useful parameters and formats, and places derived geophysical variables at Level 2.[^3] That statement concerns derivation of variables from measurements and does not itself say that a retrieval is a parameterization.

Retained sources define the term for dynamic and climate models only. They do not give a definition specific to Earth observation processing, and they give no figures, so the boundary between parameterizing a process and deriving a variable is left to the processing chain in use.[^1][^2][^3]
