- ## Technical Details
	- **Three Principles**:
		- Eliminate waste and pollution
		- Circulate products and materials at highest value
		- Regenerate nature
	- **Blockchain Benefits**:
		- Transparency and accountability in supply chains
		- Digital product passports for lifecycle tracking
		- Authentication of recycled materials
		- Reward systems for waste collection (e.g., Coca-Cola in Africa)
	- **Enabling Technologies**: IoT for tracking, blockchain for transparency, AI for optimization
	- **EU Digital Product Passport**: Legislation requiring product information for reuse and recycling
	- **2024-2025 Outlook**: Startups scaling circular solutions; more efficient recycling facilities expected
- ## Applications
	- Recycled material traceability
	- Product lifecycle documentation
	- Waste management optimization
	- Construction material reuse
	- Consumer goods circular programs
