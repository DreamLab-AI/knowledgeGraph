### Learning Paradigms
- **Supervised Learning**: Learning from labeled training data for classification and regression tasks (spam detection, predictive analytics)
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Discovering patterns without labels (clustering, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction)
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Learning optimal actions through environmental rewards and penalties
- **Semi-Supervised Learning**: Combining small amounts of labeled data with large unlabeled datasets
