### Technical Details
Key monitoring components include:
- **Transaction Screening**: Real-time analysis of incoming/outgoing transactions
- **Wallet Clustering**: Linking addresses to known entities
- **Risk Scoring**: Automated risk assessment based on behavioral patterns
- **Sanctions Screening**: Checking against OFAC, EU, UN sanctions lists

### Major Analytics Platforms

**Chainalysis**
Market leader with government and institutional clients. Provides Know Your Transaction (KYT) and reactor investigation tools.

**Elliptic**
Over 100 billion data points linking crypto addresses to verified entities. 99% market coverage with cross-chain tracing.

**TRM Labs**
Supports 200+ million assets across 100+ blockchains including DeFi and NFT coverage.

**Crystal Intelligence**
Comprehensive alert systems and exposure monitoring for sanctioned entities.

**Merkle Science**
Behavioral rule engine for proactive suspicious address detection.

### Core Monitoring Functions
- **Address Screening**: Pre-transaction verification of counterparty addresses
- **Pattern Detection**: Identifying structuring, layering, and mixing activity
- **Entity Resolution**: Connecting pseudonymous addresses to real-world identities
- **Cross-Chain Tracing**: Following funds across multiple blockchains

### Regulatory Expectations
Financial institutions are expected to implement risk-based monitoring programs proportionate to their exposure, with documented policies, trained staff, and regular independent testing.
