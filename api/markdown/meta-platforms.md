- ### Definition
  - **Meta Platforms Inc** (NASDAQ: META, formerly Facebook Inc 2004-2021) is a Menlo Park-headquartered American multinational technology conglomerate founded 4 February 2004 in a Harvard University dormitory by Mark Zuckerberg with co-founders Eduardo Saverin, Andrew McCollum, Dustin Moskovitz and Chris Hughes as TheFacebook.com — initially a Harvard-only student social directory expanding to Ivy League campuses then US universities (2005), the public internet (September 2006), mobile platforms (Facebook for iPhone 2008, native Android 2010), and ultimately a global communications stack — rebranded to **Meta Platforms** on 28 October 2021 (Facebook Connect keynote, Zuckerberg "Founder's Letter") to signal a strategic pivot toward the **metaverse** (immersive 3D social computing) and decouple the corporate identity from the increasingly contested Facebook product brand following the **Frances Haugen whistleblower disclosures** (September-October 2021 Wall Street Journal "Facebook Files" series and US Senate testimony exposing internal research on Instagram teen mental health, XCheck VIP moderation tier, Civic Integrity team dissolution, algorithmic amplification of divisive content), operating a four-segment corporate portfolio comprising (i) **Family of Apps** (Facebook 3.07B monthly active users 2024, Instagram 2B+ MAU, WhatsApp 2.5B+ MAU, Messenger 1B+ MAU, Threads launched 5 July 2023 reaching 200M+ MAU by Q1 2024 as a federated ActivityPub-compatible text-based Twitter/X competitor) generating ~98% of consolidated revenue (~$135B FY2023, ~$160B+ FY2024) almost entirely through advertising sold via the Meta Audience Network, Meta Business Suite, and the Advantage+ AI-driven ad optimisation platform serving 10M+ active advertisers, (ii) **Reality Labs** (RL, formed by absorbing Facebook Reality Labs 2020 around the 2014 **Oculus VR** acquisition for $2B and the 2019 CTRL-labs neural-interface acquisition for ~$500M-1B), producing the **Meta Quest** VR headset line (Quest 1 2019, Quest 2 2020 ~20M units, Quest Pro 2022 codenamed Project Cambria with eye/face tracking and pancake lenses at $1,499 later cut to $999, Quest 3 October 2023 at $499 with colour passthrough mixed reality, Quest 3S October 2024 at $299 entry-level, Quest 4 anticipated 2025-2026 from leaked roadmaps), the **Ray-Ban Meta** smart glasses partnership with EssilorLuxottica (Ray-Ban Stories 2021 first gen, Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer 2023 with Meta AI integration and 12MP camera, Ray-Ban Meta Skyler/Headliner 2024 generation expanding form factors, ~1M+ units shipped 2024), the **Horizon Worlds** social VR platform (200K+ MAU 2024 after early growing pains), the **Project Orion** prototype AR holographic glasses unveiled at Meta Connect 2024 (September 2024) featuring silicon-carbide waveguides, custom uplink/downlink compute pucks and a sub-100g form factor representing the most advanced full-AR consumer prototype publicly demonstrated, and Reality Labs absorbing cumulative operating losses exceeding **$60B** 2019-2024 (~$18B 2023 alone, ~$17B 2024) reflecting the most expensive corporate moonshot in technology history outside Saudi Aramco infrastructure,   - ## About Meta Platforms
  - **Meta Platforms Inc** is the corporate parent of Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger and Threads, of the Meta Quest virtual-reality headset line and Ray-Ban Meta smart-glasses partnership, of the Reality Labs immersive-computing division building toward true augmented-reality hardware, and of FAIR (Fundamental AI Research) — the open-weight foundation-model lab whose **Llama** family has redefined the economics of large language models since 2023. Few companies in the modern technology sector have undergone as comprehensive a re-identification: from a Harvard dormitory side-project (TheFacebook.com, February 2004) to the second-largest social network of the 2010s, to a global advertising duopoly partner of Google through the 2010s mobile boom, to the controversial subject of the **Cambridge Analytica** scandal (2018) and the **Frances Haugen** whistleblower disclosures (2021), to the metaverse-renamed Meta of late 2021, to — by 2024 — the most prolific open-weight AI publisher on Earth.
  - The corporate strategy as of 2026 is unambiguously **AI-first** in revenue protection and **immersive-computing-first** in long-horizon optionality. The ~$160B 2024 advertising business funds approximately $60-80B annual capital expenditure split between AI training compute (~$40B+) and Reality Labs hardware/software R&D (~$18-20B), an allocation strategy CFO Susan Li has repeatedly defended to analysts as "investing through the cycle in two generational platform shifts simultaneously". The dual bet is unusual in scale: most hyperscalers concentrate moonshots on a single platform thesis, but Meta is committing tens of billions annually to both Generative AI (where it competes with OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic, xAI) and to wearable computing (where it competes with Apple Vision Pro, Snap Spectacles, the diffuse XR ecosystem).
  - Meta is also the **largest open-weight AI publisher in commercial history**. Whilst OpenAI, Google and Anthropic have largely retained model weights for proprietary API monetisation, Zuckerberg has positioned open weights as both a developer-community lock-in mechanism and a hedge against Apple/Google platform-tax exposure. The Llama family has accumulated **~650M cumulative downloads** across Hugging Face, Kaggle, Ollama and direct distribution by mid-2025, with **60,000+ community-fine-tuned variants** registered on Hugging Face Hub. Meta's open-weight strategy has effectively re-anchored the AI research community after the closure of GPT-3 weights, becoming the dominant base model for academic research, enterprise customisation and sovereign-AI initiatives across the EU, India, the UAE and Latin America.

  - ### Corporate History and Identity

	  #### Founding and Early Growth (2004-2012)

	  **TheFacebook.com** launched 4 February 2004 from Mark Zuckerberg's Kirkland House dormitory at Harvard University, with co-founders **Eduardo Saverin** (CFO/business operations, early funder), **Andrew McCollum** (graphic artist), **Dustin Moskovitz** (lead engineer, now Asana co-founder) and **Chris Hughes** (communications, later The New Republic owner). Within two months the site claimed 50% of Harvard undergraduates; expansion to Stanford, Columbia and Yale followed within months, then the broader Ivy League, then US universities (May 2005), high schools (September 2005), and finally the general public (26 September 2006).

	  Pre-IPO funding history: Peter Thiel **$500K angel investment June 2004** at $4.9M valuation; Accel Partners **$12.7M Series A May 2005**; Greylock $25M Series B 2006; Microsoft **$240M for 1.6% October 2007** valuing Facebook at $15B; Digital Sky Technologies $200M 2009 at $10B valuation. The company filed its S-1 February 2012 and IPO'd **18 May 2012** on NASDAQ at $38/share (ticker **FB**, later renamed **META** 9 June 2022) raising $16B — the largest internet IPO ever at the time. The IPO was widely considered botched due to NASDAQ trading-system issues and aggressive insider selling, with shares falling below $20 within months before recovering by mid-2013.

	  #### Key Acquisitions

	  - **Instagram** (9 April 2012, **$1B cash + stock**): Photo-sharing app founded 2010 by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger; 30M users at acquisition; grew to 2B+ MAU by 2024. The acquisition is widely cited as one of the most prescient in technology M&A history, with current Instagram revenue estimated at $50-65B annually (~40% of Meta's ad revenue).
	  - **WhatsApp** (19 February 2014, **$19B cash + stock**): Founded 2009 by Jan Koum and Brian Acton; ~450M MAU at acquisition; grew to 2.5B+ MAU. End-to-end encryption (Signal Protocol) implemented 2016 over Koum's insistence; both founders subsequently departed in 2017-2018 disagreements over advertising and monetisation. Now monetised primarily through WhatsApp Business API and Click-to-WhatsApp ads.
	  - **Oculus VR** (21 March 2014, **$2B cash + stock**): Founded 2012 by Palmer Luckey; Oculus Rift Kickstarter darling 2012; rebranded under Meta to **Meta Quest** in 2022 dropping the Facebook account requirement. Luckey departed 2017; subsequently founded defence-tech Anduril. Oculus formed the nucleus of what became Reality Labs.
	  - **CTRL-labs** (September 2019, $500M-1B undisclosed): EMG neural-interface wristband technology; basis for the in-development Meta neural-interface wristband companion to AR glasses.
	  - **Within** (2022, $400M after FTC challenge): VR fitness app maker, contested by FTC competition case; ultimately completed.
	  - **Onavo** (2013): VPN/data analytics; later controversial for tracking competitor app usage to inform acquisition decisions; shut down 2019 amid privacy criticism.
	  - **LiveRail** (2014, $400-500M): Video ad tech; integrated into Meta Audience Network.

	  #### Rebrand to Meta (October 2021)

	  At Facebook Connect on **28 October 2021**, Zuckerberg announced the corporate parent's rename to **Meta Platforms Inc**, citing the company's metaverse ambitions and the need to disentangle corporate identity from the increasingly contested Facebook product brand. The rebrand followed months of damaging revelations:

	  - **The Facebook Files** (Wall Street Journal series, September-October 2021): Based on documents leaked by product manager **Frances Haugen**, the series exposed internal research on Instagram's impact on teen mental health, the "XCheck" VIP moderation tier exempting celebrities from normal content rules, internal awareness of cartel violence on WhatsApp and human trafficking on Facebook in developing markets, and the post-2018 News Feed algorithm change weighting "Meaningful Social Interactions" that had inadvertently amplified divisive content.
	  - **Haugen Senate Testimony** (5 October 2021): Haugen testified to the US Senate Subcommittee on Consumer Protection that Meta consistently prioritised growth and engagement over safety; appeared before the UK Joint Committee on the Online Safety Bill 25 October 2021 contributing to the eventual Online Safety Act 2023.
	  - **NASDAQ ticker change**: 9 June 2022, the listed ticker symbol changed from **FB** to **META**.

	  Reality Labs operating losses ballooned through the rebrand period: $10.2B 2021 → $13.7B 2022 → $16.1B 2023 → ~$17B 2024, with cumulative 2019-2024 losses exceeding **$60B**. The 2022 share-price drawdown (peak ~$384 September 2021 to trough ~$88 November 2022, **-77%**) prompted the **"Year of Efficiency"** announced February 2023 cutting headcount by ~25% (~21K layoffs across two rounds Nov 2022 / March 2023). Shares recovered to all-time highs above $600 by late 2024 on AI optimism.

  - ### Components and Architecture

	  #### Family of Apps (FoA)

	  - **Facebook** (the core product, 3.07B MAU Q4 2024): Newsfeed, Marketplace, Groups, Pages, Events, Stories, Reels, Live, Gaming, Watch. The original product remains the largest social network globally though declining in younger demographics. Revenue: ~$70-80B 2024 estimated.
	  - **Instagram** (2B+ MAU): Photo/video sharing, Reels (TikTok competitor launched 2020), Stories, IGTV (discontinued), Shopping, DMs. ~$50-65B revenue 2024. Adam Mosseri as Head of Instagram since 2018.
	  - **WhatsApp** (2.5B+ MAU): End-to-end encrypted messaging via Signal Protocol; WhatsApp Business, WhatsApp Pay (India/Brazil), Communities (large-group hierarchies), WhatsApp Channels (broadcast). Monetisation primarily through Click-to-WhatsApp ads ($10B+ run rate 2024) and WhatsApp Business API enterprise pricing.
	  - **Messenger** (1B+ MAU): Originally part of Facebook, split out as standalone 2014; re-integrated cross-platform messaging with Instagram DMs 2020 (DMA-compliant interoperability planned 2025-2026).
	  - **Threads** (launched 5 July 2023): Text-based microblogging Twitter/X competitor. 100M sign-ups in first 5 days (fastest-growing consumer app ever). Reached **200M+ MAU Q1 2024**. ActivityPub federation began limited rollout 2024 enabling Mastodon/Fediverse interoperability. Led by Adam Mosseri.

	  #### Reality Labs (RL)

	  - **Meta Quest VR Line**: Quest 1 (2019, $399, Snapdragon 835, 3DoF controllers), Quest 2 (2020, $299-399, ~20M units sold making it the best-selling VR headset ever), Quest Pro / Project Cambria (2022, $1,499 later $999, eye/face tracking, pancake lenses), Quest 3 (Oct 2023, $499, Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2, full-colour passthrough mixed reality), Quest 3S (Oct 2024, $299, entry-level MR), Quest 4 anticipated late 2025-2026 from leaked roadmaps.
	  - **Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses** (partnership with **EssilorLuxottica** since 2019): Ray-Ban Stories first generation (Sept 2021, photo/video, limited functionality, ~300K units); Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer (Oct 2023, $299, 12MP camera, Meta AI integration, live-streaming); Ray-Ban Meta Skyler/Headliner expansions 2024 covering additional frame styles. **~1M+ units shipped 2024** making them the fastest-growing smart-glass product category since Google Glass.
	  - **Project Orion** (unveiled Meta Connect 25 September 2024): Holographic AR glasses prototype with silicon-carbide waveguides delivering 70° field of view, custom uplink/downlink compute pucks (wireless co-processor), Micro-LED display, sub-100g form factor. Not a commercial product — internal prototype demonstrating the engineering envelope; **Orion is widely considered the most advanced full-AR consumer prototype publicly demonstrated** as of 2024, with consumer derivatives anticipated late this decade.
	  - **Horizon Worlds** (social VR platform, 2021): User-generated 3D social spaces; growing pains 2022-2023 with user retention; ~200K MAU 2024; underwent significant overhaul including teen-account expansion and creator monetisation.
	  - **Horizon OS** (2024): Meta opened the Quest operating system to third-party hardware manufacturers including Lenovo, ASUS Republic of Gamers, Xbox cloud-streaming partnership.

	  #### FAIR and AI Research

	  **FAIR (Fundamental AI Research)** was founded **December 2013** under Yann LeCun (then NYU Center for Data Science chair, recipient of the **2018 Turing Award** with Bengio and Hinton). FAIR laboratories operate in: Menlo Park (HQ), New York, Paris (where LeCun maintains a major presence), London, Tel Aviv, Montreal, Pittsburgh and Seattle. FAIR's research culture emphasises open publication and weight release — a deliberate counterpoint to OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind's increasingly closed-model trajectory post-2022.

	  **Generative AI organisation (GenAI)** was reorganised under product VP Ahmad Al-Dahle from early 2023 to drive consumer-facing AI products (Meta AI assistant, image generation, business chat) in tighter coordination with FAIR foundation-model research.

	  #### Llama Foundation Model Family

	  - **Llama 1** (24 February 2023, research-only licence): 7B/13B/33B/65B parameters; weights leaked to BitTorrent within a week, catalysing the open-source LLM revolution (Stanford Alpaca, Vicuna, OpenAssistant all built on leaked Llama 1 weights).
	  - **Llama 2** (18 July 2023, commercially-licensed): 7B/13B/70B; first major commercially-permissive open-weight LLM; partnership announcement with Microsoft Azure for Llama 2 hosting.
	  - **Code Llama** (24 August 2023): Specialised code generation model 7B/13B/34B/70B; basis for many commercial coding-assistant tools.
	  - **Llama 3** (18 April 2024): 8B/70B; introduced Llama Guard 2 safety classifier, Code Shield input/output filtering.
	  - **Llama 3.1** (23 July 2024): Refreshed 8B/70B plus flagship **405B-parameter model** — **the first openly-released GPT-4-class model**, trained on 15.6T tokens across 16K H100 GPUs over ~30M GPU-hours. License permits training other models on Llama 3.1 outputs (a notable departure from Llama 2's restriction).
	  - **Llama 3.2** (25 September 2024): Multimodal vision-language 11B/90B plus mobile-edge 1B/3B text-only models optimised for on-device inference on smartphones. First mobile/edge Llama deployment.
	  - **Llama 3.3** (December 2024): 70B distilled model approaching Llama 3.1 405B reasoning quality at fraction of inference cost.
	  - **Llama 4** (April 2025): Mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture introduction — **Llama 4 Scout** 17B active / 16 experts (109B total), **Llama 4 Maverick** 17B active / 128 experts (400B total), and **Llama 4 Behemoth** (~2T total parameters) in continued training as the teacher model. Multimodal native (image + text input). Maverick achieves benchmark parity with GPT-4o and Gemini 2.0 Flash at substantially lower inference cost. Excluded from EU markets pending EU AI Act GPAI Code of Practice clarifications.

	  **Cumulative downloads**: ~650M across Hugging Face / Kaggle / Ollama / direct mid-2025. **Derived variants**: 60,000+ on Hugging Face Hub.

	  #### Other Frontier Models

	  - **Segment Anything Model (SAM)** April 2023, **SAM 2** July 2024, **SAM 2.1** late 2024: Promptable image and video segmentation; 10M+ downloads; basis for medical-imaging, robotics, AR pipelines, content moderation.
	  - **DINOv2** April 2023: Self-supervised vision foundation model; widely used as ViT backbone in downstream tasks.
	  - **ImageBind** May 2023: Six-modality joint embedding (image, text, audio, depth, thermal, IMU) demonstrating emergent zero-shot cross-modal retrieval.
	  - **Sapiens** 2024: Human-centric vision models (2D pose, depth, normal, segmentation) for AR avatar generation.
	  - **SeamlessM4T** August 2023 / **SeamlessExpressive** January 2024: 100-language speech-to-speech translation with expressive prosody preservation.
	  - **Voicebox** June 2023: Non-autoregressive in-context speech generation.
	  - **AudioGen / MusicGen** (2023): Text-to-audio and text-to-music generation; basis for Audiobox 2023.
	  - **CICERO** November 2022 *Science*: First AI system to achieve human-level performance in Diplomacy (negotiation + strategy game).
	  - **NLLB-200** July 2022: 200-language "No Language Left Behind" machine translation.
	  - **Meta MovieGen** September 2024: Text-to-video 16-second 1080p output with synchronised audio generation; competitive with OpenAI Sora.
	  - **Meta AI Assistant** (consumer-facing): Deployed across WhatsApp/Instagram/Messenger/Facebook and Ray-Ban Meta glasses; ~600M MAU late 2024; powered by Llama 3.1/3.2/3.3 with Imagine image generation and live-video reasoning (Ray-Ban Meta "Hey Meta" voice queries).

  - ### Academic Context: Research Foundations and Institutional Position

	  Meta's research trajectory is anchored by Yann LeCun's **2018 Turing Award**-recognised convolutional-network legacy (LeNet 1989, demonstrating gradient-based learning for OCR at AT&T Bell Labs) and his consistent intellectual public-facing advocacy for **self-supervised learning**, **world models**, and **objective-driven AI** as alternatives to autoregressive LLM scaling — positions articulated in his 2022 position paper "A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence" and amplified across his media commentary criticising what he characterises as LLM hype cycles. LeCun's **JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture)** research programme — I-JEPA 2023, V-JEPA 2024 — represents FAIR's foundational alternative path to AGI through self-supervised world-model learning rather than next-token prediction at scale.

	  FAIR has produced foundational contributions across machine learning:

	  - **PyTorch** (2016, open-sourced 2017): Originally Soumith Chintala et al. at FAIR; now governed by the Linux Foundation's PyTorch Foundation (transitioned 2022) but with continuing major Meta investment. Powers ~80% of academic ML research and competes effectively with Google's TensorFlow for production deployment.
	  - **FAISS** (Facebook AI Similarity Search): Vector similarity library underlying RAG and retrieval systems globally; 30K+ GitHub stars.
	  - **Detectron / Detectron2**: Object detection frameworks; basis for Mask R-CNN (He et al. 2017 ICCV Best Paper).
	  - **fastText** (Joulin et al. 2016): Efficient word-embedding library, multilingual support, widely deployed.
	  - **RoBERTa** (Liu et al. 2019): Robustly Optimised BERT Pretraining Approach; for several years the dominant transformer pre-training recipe.
	  - **BART** (Lewis et al. 2020): Bidirectional autoregressive transformer for sequence-to-sequence tasks.
	  - **OPT-175B** (May 2022): Open Pre-trained Transformers; first major open release of GPT-3-scale model weights (research-only), precursor to Llama.
	  - **Galactica** (November 2022): Scientific LLM; controversial public withdrawal after 3 days due to hallucinated citation generation — an early lesson in production LLM safety that shaped subsequent Llama release protocols.
	  - **Mask R-CNN, DETR, ConvNeXt, ViT exploration, SAM**: Vision research lineage spanning a decade.

	  **Citation impact**: FAIR researchers collectively rank amongst the most-cited ML institutions globally (alongside Google Brain/DeepMind and Stanford/Berkeley); the original AlexNet/LeNet/CNN lineage traces directly to LeCun's pre-FAIR work; PyTorch papers have accumulated >100K citations.

  - ### Future Directions (2026-2030)

	  - **Llama 5 anticipated 2025-2026**: Expected to scale MoE further, deepen multimodality (native video, native audio), and integrate longer-horizon reasoning derived from o1-style inference-time compute techniques.

	  - **Inference cost compression**: MTIA v3 and v4 silicon iterations targeting 5-10x inference cost reduction versus H100 for ranking and Llama inference workloads.
	  - **Meta AI agentic capabilities**: Multi-step task execution, calendar/booking integration, e-commerce checkout via Meta AI assistant; competing directly with OpenAI Operator, Anthropic Computer Use, Google Gemini-with-Agents.
	  - **Sovereign Llama**: Llama-derived sovereign foundation models in partnership with India (Sarvam AI, Krutrim), UAE (G42, MBZUAI), Saudi Arabia, Brazil and Indonesia leveraging open-weight licensing for nation-state AI strategies.

	  - **Orion consumer derivative** (~2027-2030): The first consumer derivative of Project Orion AR holographic glasses; targeted sub-$1000, sub-100g, all-day-wearable form factor with substantial Llama-class on-device AI.
