QinetiQ is a UK-based technology and engineering company that describes a space offer of engineering, technology and assurance services for defence and security customers.[^1][^2][^3]

Its Trusted Partner Space page lists capability themes that include secure communications, space domain awareness, navigation and timing, and space control, and says these are delivered through systems engineering and technical support and related services such as operational analysis.[^1] A second page lists specific technology areas, among them a positioning, navigation and timing receiver range, optical communications and sensing, and a space weather modelling tool.[^2]

A third page describes test, evaluation and assurance work intended to give customers confidence that dual-use commercial and civil space technologies are suitable for defence and security use.[^3] All three are company statements. They do not show that QinetiQ acts as a spacecraft prime, and independent evidence of contracts, performance or market position was not gathered here.

QinetiQ's catalogue relation to the UK space prime group reflects its place in the supply base rather than a stated prime role. The UK Government supply chain note refers to Tier 1 suppliers and primes in general terms and does not name the company.[^4]
