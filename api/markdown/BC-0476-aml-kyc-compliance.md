### Technical Details
Key compliance components include:
- **Customer Due Diligence (CDD)**: Identity verification at onboarding
- **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**: Additional scrutiny for high-risk customers
- **Transaction Monitoring**: Automated detection of suspicious patterns
- **Sanctions Screening**: Checking against OFAC, EU, UN sanctions lists

### Regulatory Requirements
- **FATF Standards**: Global AML/CFT framework for virtual assets
- **EU AMLD5/6**: Fifth and Sixth Anti-Money Laundering Directives
- **US FinCEN**: Registration and reporting for money services businesses
- **MiCA**: EU requirements for crypto-asset service providers

### Blockchain-Specific Challenges
- Pseudonymous transactions requiring additional analytics
- Cross-border operations spanning multiple jurisdictions
- DeFi protocols with no centralized operator
- Privacy coins and mixing services

### Compliance Solutions
- **Chainalysis**: Blockchain analytics and transaction monitoring
- **Elliptic**: Crypto compliance and risk management
- **CipherTrace**: AML solutions for cryptocurrency
- **On-Chain KYC**: Reusable verified identity credentials
