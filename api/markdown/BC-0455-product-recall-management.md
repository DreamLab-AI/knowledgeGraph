### Technical Details
Key capabilities include:
- **Immutable Supply Chain Records**: Complete product journey from origin to retail recorded on blockchain
- **Rapid Traceability**: Trace contaminated products from days to seconds
- **Smart Contract Automation**: Automated alerts and coordination across supply chain partners
- **Targeted Response**: Precise identification of affected batches minimizing recall scope

### Industry Applications
- **Food Industry**: Walmart requires leafy-green suppliers to input data into blockchain platform, tracing contaminated produce in seconds; Tyson Foods tracks farm-to-production supply chain
- **Pharmaceuticals**: IBM Blockchain partnered with FDA, KPMG, Merck, and Walmart demonstrating recall time reduction from days to seconds under Drug Supply Chain Security Act
- **Automotive**: Blockchain-based recall management using Ethereum and IPFS providing transparency, traceability, reliability, and trust features

### Research Findings
A 2024 study demonstrates companies with blockchain adoption have significantly shorter recall times. Research addresses how effective traceability mechanisms help track defective products that cause recall incidents.

### Benefits
Blockchain dramatically reduces verification burden, improves consumer safety, minimizes economic impact of recalls through precision targeting, and builds confidence in supply chain reliability.
