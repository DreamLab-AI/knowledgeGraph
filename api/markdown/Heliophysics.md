Heliophysics is the study of the Sun and of how it influences space, the planets and technology, in the words of NASA's Heliophysics Division.[^1]

NASA says that space is not completely empty and describes the Earth as sitting in the extended atmosphere of an active star. A practical branch is space weather, which the Met Office describes as conditions in space that can have an effect on Earth. It lists possible impacts that include disruption to radio communications and GPS, disruption of power grids and damage to spacecraft.[^1][^2]

The UK Space Agency annual report records support for a UK-led space weather mission called Vigil and describes it as a priority severe space weather mission. The report does not describe the mission's instruments here.[^3]

These sources come from space agencies and a weather service, so the scope given reflects their programmes. They do not establish the frequency or severity of any particular event.[^1][^2][^3]
