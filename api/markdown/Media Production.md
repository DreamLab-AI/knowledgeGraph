
Media production is the end-to-end process of creating audiovisual content, spanning pre-production planning, capture, editing, and post-production finishing. It increasingly integrates generative AI, virtual production, and automated voice and speech tools to accelerate and personalise output. As a workflow it coordinates creative, technical, and asset-management activities across a content pipeline.

- ### Content
  - Modern production blends traditional capture with virtual sets, real-time rendering, and AI-assisted editing, asset generation, and localisation. The shift compresses timelines and lowers cost barriers, while raising provenance, rights, and quality-control concerns around synthetic media.
