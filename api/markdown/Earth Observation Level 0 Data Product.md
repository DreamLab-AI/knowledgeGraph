An Earth observation Level 0 data product is the lowest processing level of satellite data, consisting of raw instrument and payload data before calibration or geolocation.[^1][^2]

NASA defines Level 0 as reconstructed, unprocessed instrument and payload data at full resolution, with communications artefacts such as synchronisation frames, headers and duplicate data removed. Its processing-level page summarises Level 0 products as raw data at full instrument resolution.[^1]

Its form depends on the mission. Sentinel-2 Level-0 is raw image data in on-board compressed Instrument Source Packet format, and Sentinel-1 Level-0 contains raw SAR data from unprocessed Instrument Source Packets, divided into several product classes.[^3][^4] For SAR the handbook notes that raw data do not give a useful picture of the scene until aperture synthesis processing, and that some operators do not release them.[^2]

Level 0 is the input to higher levels, and a Level 0 product should not be treated as an image or as physical measurements without further processing. Which Level 0 products are public varies by sensor and operator, and the sources here do not give a general rule.[^4][^2]
