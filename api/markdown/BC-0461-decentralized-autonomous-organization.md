### Technical Details
Key DAO components include:
- **Governance Tokens**: Grant voting power proportional to holdings for protocol decisions
- **Smart Contracts**: Automate rule enforcement, fund management, and proposal execution
- **Treasury Management**: Community-controlled funds with transparent on-chain accounting
- **Proposal Systems**: Structured processes for submitting, discussing, and voting on changes

### Market Statistics (2024)
As of 2024, over 13,000 DAOs exist with total treasury holdings of $24.5 billion and 11.1 million governance token holders. Average DAO treasury size is approximately $1.2 million. Native tokens comprise 67.3% of treasury holdings, stablecoins 18.2%.

### Governance Challenges
- **Voter Participation**: Average participation around 17% across DAOs in 2025
- **Whale Concentration**: Large token holders can disproportionately influence decisions
- **Security Threats**: Over $2.2 billion stolen across 303 security incidents in 2024
- **Legal Uncertainty**: Regulatory frameworks for DAO structures still evolving

### Platform Examples
- **Aragon**: No-code DAO launcher with ERC20Votes standard token governance
- **DAOHaus**: Multi-chain DAO platform with Safe multisig integration
- **Tally**: Uses OpenZeppelin Governor contracts for on-chain voting
- **Snapshot**: Off-chain voting moving toward on-chain with Snapshot X on Starknet
