User trust is the confidence individuals place in a system to handle their data, decisions and interactions reliably, securely and in their interests. It is built through transparency, predictable behaviour, privacy safeguards and demonstrable accountability, and is a key outcome of privacy and data-protection practices. Eroded trust undermines adoption, while sustained trust is a competitive and ethical asset.

### Content

- Trust is earned through transparent data practices, robust security, clear consent, and consistent, explainable behaviour. It functions as a leading indicator of adoption and retention, and its breach through misuse or breaches can cause lasting reputational harm.
