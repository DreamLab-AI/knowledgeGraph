- ## Overview
	- Virtual Reality creates fully immersive digital environments through head-mounted displays. Global AR/VR headset shipments increased approximately 10% in 2024 to 7.5 million units, with the market projected to reach $237 billion by 2032. The Apple Vision Pro launched in 2024 with high-resolution displays and user-centric design. Standalone headsets like Meta Quest 3 and Pico 5 have eliminated the need for external PCs. Google announced Android XR in 2024 for extended reality devices.
- ## Technical Details
	- ### Display Technologies
		- **High-Resolution Panels**: LCD, OLED, and micro-OLED displays
		- **Foveated Rendering**: Eye-tracking optimized graphics processing
		- **Wide Field of View**: Peripheral vision coverage for immersion
		- **Pass-Through Cameras**: Mixed reality capabilities
	- ### Tracking Systems
		- **Inside-Out Tracking**: Camera-based position detection without external sensors
		- **Hand Tracking**: Controller-free gesture recognition
		- **Eye Tracking**: Gaze direction detection for foveated rendering
		- **Body Tracking**: Full-body movement capture
	- ### Interaction Methods
		- Motion controllers with haptic feedback
		- Hand and gesture recognition
		- Voice commands and spatial audio
		- Eye-gaze interaction
	- ### Platform Developments
		- Apple Vision Pro spatial computing platform
		- Meta Quest standalone ecosystem
		- Google Android XR operating system
		- PlayStation VR console integration
- ## Applications
	- Gaming and entertainment experiences
	- Healthcare pain management and therapy
	- Surgical training and medical simulation
	- Educational virtual classrooms and field trips
	- Enterprise training and collaboration
	- FAA-approved flight simulation training
