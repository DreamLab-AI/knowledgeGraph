Heliosphere refers to the bubble around the Sun and its planets created by the solar wind and bounded where the interstellar medium impedes it.[^1]

NASA states that the solar wind ultimately travels past all the planets to some three times the distance to Pluto before being impeded by the interstellar medium. Another NASA page describes the same structure as a bubble that envelopes the planets of the solar system and is filled predominantly with plasma flowing out from the Sun.[^1][^2]

According to the Voyager mission page, the heliosphere is the protective bubble of particles and magnetic fields generated by the Sun, and Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 are the only spacecraft to have operated outside it. The outer boundary where the solar wind meets the interstellar medium is called the heliopause.[^3][^2]

Extent given by NASA is expressed relative to the distance to Pluto and is not a precise measurement. Sources do not describe the shape of the heliosphere or how its size changes with solar activity, and they should not be read as doing so.[^1]
