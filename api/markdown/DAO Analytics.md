- ## Technical Details
	- **Market Overview**: Over 13,000 active DAOs managing $24.5B in collective treasury with 11.1M governance token holders (2024)
	- **Key Analytics Platforms**:
		- **DeepDAO**: Rankings, financial and governance analysis across all DAOs
		- **Dune Analytics**: 700,000+ dashboards covering DeFi, NFT, and blockchain metrics; SQL-queryable data
		- **Etherscan/Block Explorers**: Raw on-chain data for token balances and transactions
	- **Metrics Tracked**:
		- Proposal trends, success rates, and common themes
		- Contribution data and reward distribution
		- Social metrics (Discord, forum engagement, sentiment)
		- Voting participation rates and power concentration
	- **Research Findings**: Top 10% of token holders control 76.2% of voting power; DAOs without weighted decision-making may be more viable long-term
- ## Applications
	- Treasury management optimization
	- Governance participation analysis
	- Proposal performance tracking
	- Token distribution monitoring
	- DAO health assessment
