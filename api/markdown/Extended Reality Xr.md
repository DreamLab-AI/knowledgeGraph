## Core Technologies

### Virtual Reality (VR)
- Completely immerses users in virtual environments
- Disconnects users from physical world
- Headsets: Meta Quest, HTC Vive, PlayStation VR
- Applications: gaming, design reviews, training, virtual tours

### Augmented Reality (AR)
- Overlays digital content onto real world
- Enhances perception of physical surroundings
- Devices: smartphones, tablets, AR glasses
- Applications: navigation, retail, industrial maintenance

### Mixed Reality (MR)
- Convergence of physical and virtual worlds
- Digital objects interact with physical environment
- Spatial mapping and sensor integration
- Applications: industrial design, training, collaboration
