- ### Definition
  - Renewable energy integration powers compute and mining with low-carbon generation, a key response to [[Bitcoin Environmental Issues]] and a building block of any [[Carbon Neutral Blockchain]].
- ### Content
  - Flexible, interruptible mining loads can act as a buyer of last resort for curtailed wind and solar, improving project economics while lowering the carbon intensity of hashing. Realising the benefit depends on genuine additionality, transparent accounting, and avoiding displacement of cleaner uses of that energy.
