  - ## About Hunyuan Video
  - **Hunyuan Video (HunyuanVideo)** is the flagship open-weights text-to-video foundation model published by **Tencent's Hunyuan Foundation Model team** on **3 December 2024**, accompanied by the 41-page technical report "HunyuanVideo: A Systematic Framework For Large Video Generative Models" (Kong et al. 2024, arXiv:2412.03603). At 13 billion parameters it is the largest open-source text-to-video model released to date, and its weights, code, inference pipeline, and prompt-rewriting helper LLM are all distributed under a custom community licence permitting research, individual creator, and small-commercial use whilst restricting deployment by entities exceeding 100 million monthly active users and requiring attribution. Within months of release it became the de facto open-source baseline for academic video-diffusion research and the centre of gravity for the ComfyUI/Kijai workflow community.
  - The model occupies a strategically significant position in the **2024-2026 generative video landscape**: it is the first open-source release whose human-preference quality is benchmarked competitively with closed commercial systems (Kling 1.5, MiniMax Hailuo, Luma Dream Machine), narrowing what had been a step-change gap between proprietary and open video generation that persisted through Mochi 1 (Genmo, October 2024), CogVideoX-5B (THUDM, August 2024), Open-Sora 1.x (HPC-AI Tech, March 2024), and Stable Video Diffusion (Stability AI, November 2023). Tencent's release was widely interpreted as an industrial-policy move asserting Chinese open-source leadership in generative AI to parallel Alibaba's subsequent Wan 2.1 release (February 2025).
  - The architecture combines three mature components: (1) a **Causal 3D Variational Autoencoder** compressing 4 frames × 8×8 spatial blocks into single latent tokens with 16-channel latents and a temporal-causal mask preserving the autoregressive structure needed for arbitrary-length generation; (2) a **dual-stream then single-stream Multimodal Diffusion Transformer** (MMDiT-style, inspired by Stable Diffusion 3 and FLUX.1) in which text and video tokens are processed through separate but symmetric attention streams in early layers (preserving modality-specific information) before being concatenated and processed jointly in later layers (enabling deep cross-modal alignment); and (3) a **Flow Matching** training objective (Lipman et al. 2023) replacing the older ε-prediction DDPM noise schedule with a velocity-field formulation that yields straighter trajectories in latent space and consequently more stable training and faster sampling. The model accepts bilingual Chinese/English prompts via a frozen T5/MLLM-style encoder.

  - ### Core Technical Framework

	  HunyuanVideo's design synthesises several lines of research that had previously been demonstrated only in isolation.

	  **Flow Matching Training Objective**: The model is trained via **Conditional Flow Matching** (Lipman et al. 2023, Liu et al. 2023 Rectified Flow), in which the network learns a velocity field v_θ(x_t, t) parameterising the continuous-time ODE dx/dt = v_θ(x_t, t) that transports samples from the prior p_0 = N(0, I) to the data distribution p_1 = p_data along straight conditional paths x_t = (1−t)·x_0 + t·x_1 with t ∈ [0,1]. The training loss is the simple regression:

	  L_CFM = E_{t, x_0, x_1}[‖v_θ((1−t)x_0 + t·x_1, t) − (x_1 − x_0)‖²]

	  Compared to ε-prediction DDPM the Flow Matching formulation yields (a) more stable training at scale, (b) fewer denoising steps for comparable quality (HunyuanVideo achieves strong results at 30-50 sampling steps versus 100-1000 for older DDPMs), and (c) interpretable per-step velocity outputs amenable to distillation (Adversarial Diffusion Distillation, Latent Consistency Models).

	  **Causal 3D VAE**: The video encoder compresses raw RGB video x ∈ ℝ^{T×H×W×3} into a latent tensor z ∈ ℝ^{T'×H'×W'×16} with compression factor 4×8×8 (temporal × spatial). The temporal compression is **causal**: the encoder for frame t depends only on frames ≤ t, allowing the decoder to be unrolled at arbitrary length and enabling long-form generation despite training on fixed-length clips. The first frame is preserved unchanged (no temporal compression) which simplifies image conditioning for the image-to-video extension.

	  **Dual-Stream then Single-Stream MMDiT**: The 13B-parameter transformer backbone follows the MMDiT design pattern popularised by Stable Diffusion 3 and FLUX.1. Text tokens (from the prompt encoder) and video tokens (from the Causal 3D VAE latent) are first processed through **N₁ ≈ 20 dual-stream blocks** in which each modality has separate Q/K/V projections, separate MLPs, but shared cross-attention—this preserves the high-dimensional semantic structure of each modality whilst enabling early cross-modal information flow. They are then concatenated and processed through **N₂ ≈ 40 single-stream blocks** with shared parameters operating on the joint token sequence, enabling deep multimodal fusion. **Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE, Su et al. 2021)** encode 3D spatiotemporal positions (frame, height, width) directly into the attention queries and keys, generalising the 1D RoPE used in LLMs to the 3D case via per-axis frequency assignment.

	  **Prompt Encoder**: Unlike older text-to-video systems that used CLIP, HunyuanVideo uses a **frozen MLLM-style encoder** (a fine-tuned multimodal large language model derived from Hunyuan's main LLM family) producing dense token embeddings with substantially richer semantics than CLIP's 77-token pooled vector. This enables long, detailed prompts (up to several hundred tokens) and bilingual Chinese/English conditioning—reflecting Tencent's primary user base. A separate prompt-rewriting helper LLM is distributed alongside the model to expand terse user prompts into the verbose, descriptive style preferred by the diffusion backbone.

  - ### Architectural Components

	  #### Causal 3D Variational Autoencoder

	  The video tokeniser is the most resource-critical component of the pipeline. It operates on FP16 video frames at the target resolution (540p or 720p) and produces 16-channel latents with 4×8×8 compression. For a 5-second 720p video at 24fps (129 frames × 720 × 1280 × 3 = ~356M values in pixel space), the latent representation is ~33 frames × 90 × 160 × 16 = ~7.6M values, a 47× compression ratio. The encoder/decoder pair adds ~300M parameters and requires ~10-15GB VRAM during full-precision decoding—often the bottleneck on consumer hardware, leading the community to develop **tile-decoding** strategies that decode the latent in spatial tiles with overlapping margins and stitch the results.

	  The **causal mask** in the temporal dimension is the defining feature: the encoder for frame t attends only to frames ≤ t (in latent space, latent slice t' attends only to t″ ≤ t'). This means a generated latent can be decoded incrementally as new frames arrive, in principle enabling streaming or arbitrary-length generation. In practice the transformer backbone is trained on fixed 129-frame clips so generation beyond ~5 seconds requires either community extensions (sliding-window inference, RIFLEx for context length scaling) or fine-tuning.

	  #### Multimodal Diffusion Transformer Backbone

	  The 13B transformer comprises approximately 60 total blocks split between dual-stream and single-stream phases. Key configuration:
	  - **Hidden dimension**: 3072
	  - **Attention heads**: 24
	  - **MLP ratio**: 4×
	  - **Dual-stream blocks (N₁)**: ~20
	  - **Single-stream blocks (N₂)**: ~40
	  - **RoPE axes**: 3 (temporal, height, width) with per-axis frequencies
	  - **Activation**: SwiGLU
	  - **Normalisation**: RMSNorm with QK-Norm in attention

	  This configuration is broadly comparable to FLUX.1-dev (12B parameters, all single-stream MMDiT) but adds the dual-stream early phase which Tencent's ablations show improves prompt-following on long descriptive prompts. The single-stream phase is where the bulk of the parameter count resides and where consumer-hardware optimisation focuses (quantising single-stream weights to FP8 or INT4 has minimal quality impact, whilst quantising dual-stream weights tends to degrade semantic alignment).

	  #### Prompt Encoder and Prompt-Rewriter

	  Two text models are involved at inference:

	  1. **Prompt rewriter** (a Hunyuan-Lite or similar small LLM, ~7B parameters): Expands user input from terse phrases ("a cat playing piano") into descriptive paragraphs specifying camera, lighting, materials, composition, and motion (~300-500 tokens). This rewrite step is critical to quality—skipping it produces noticeably worse results.
	  2. **Prompt encoder** (frozen MLLM, ~5B parameters): Encodes the rewritten prompt into dense token embeddings that condition the MMDiT via cross-attention in dual-stream blocks and concatenation in single-stream blocks. Both Chinese and English are natively supported; English performance is comparable to Chinese on canonical benchmarks (VBench, EvalCrafter, MovieGen-Bench).

	  Community workflows often **skip the rewriter and write expert prompts directly** to save VRAM, or substitute an alternative rewriter (Claude, GPT-4, local Qwen2.5).

	  #### Sampler and Guidance

	  HunyuanVideo uses standard ODE samplers from the Flow Matching literature (Euler, Heun, DPM-Solver++ adapted to velocity prediction). **Classifier-Free Guidance** (CFG, Ho & Salimans 2022) is applied with guidance scale typically 6.0-9.0; lower than diffusion image models because Flow Matching's straight trajectories tolerate weaker guidance. Community workflows often use **distilled CFG** or **CFG-zero** (skipping CFG on early steps) to reduce inference cost by ~40% with negligible quality loss.

  - ### Hardware Requirements and Consumer Democratisation

	  HunyuanVideo's reference inference pipeline targets data-centre GPUs but the community has aggressively pushed it to consumer hardware—one of the defining stories of late 2024 and early 2025 ComfyUI development.

	  #### Reference Hardware (Tencent Official)

	  - **NVIDIA H100 80GB (SXM5 / PCIe)**: 720p 5-second video in ~15 minutes at 50 sampling steps with full BF16 precision. VRAM consumption ~60-65GB peak (transformer + VAE decoder + prompt encoder + activations).
	  - **NVIDIA A100 80GB**: 720p 5-second in ~22 minutes; equivalent VRAM footprint.
	  - **NVIDIA H800 80GB** (China domestic): comparable to H100 with ~30% lower memory bandwidth.

	  At 540p the VRAM requirement drops to ~45GB and inference time to ~8 minutes on H100, fitting on 48GB cards (RTX 6000 Ada, L40S).

	  #### Consumer Hardware via Community Optimisation

	  Within 6-8 weeks of release the community reduced VRAM requirements to fit RTX 4090 / RTX 3090 (24GB) and even RTX 3060 (12GB) through a stack of techniques:

	  **GGUF Quantisation (city96)**: The ComfyUI-GGUF custom node by **city96** (city96/ComfyUI-GGUF on GitHub) ports llama.cpp's GGUF quantisation formats (Q2_K, Q3_K_S, Q4_K_M, Q5_K_M, Q6_K, Q8_0) to diffusion model transformer weights. Q5_K_M and Q8_0 typically produce visually indistinguishable output from BF16; Q4_K_M shows mild quality degradation acceptable for previs/exploration. A Q4_K_M HunyuanVideo fits in ~10GB transformer VRAM (+ VAE + encoder + activations totalling ~22GB), enabling 720p generation on RTX 4090 24GB.

	  **FP8 Quantisation (Kijai)**: **Kijai** (Jukka Seppänen, Finland—a single individual whose workflow contributions to the ComfyUI/Hunyuan ecosystem have been enormous) maintains FP8 e4m3fn and e5m2 conversions on Hugging Face (Kijai/HunyuanVideo_comfy). FP8 requires CUDA compute capability ≥ 8.9 (Ada Lovelace, Hopper) and is ~1.5-2× faster than BF16 with minimal quality impact.

	  **Block Swap CPU Offload (HunyuanVideoWrapper)**: Kijai's **ComfyUI-HunyuanVideoWrapper** implements **block-swap inference**, dynamically swapping transformer blocks between GPU VRAM and CPU RAM as they are needed during the forward pass. With aggressive block-swap (e.g. 30 of 60 blocks resident in CPU RAM) HunyuanVideo runs on as little as 8-10GB VRAM at the cost of 3-5× slower inference. This enables RTX 3060 12GB and even RTX 3050 8GB deployment.

	  **TeaCache (LiewFeng et al. 2024)**: **TeaCache** (Timestep Embedding Aware Cache) skips redundant transformer computations during diffusion sampling by caching activations when the difference between consecutive timestep embeddings falls below a threshold. For HunyuanVideo at threshold 0.15, TeaCache delivers ~1.9-2.2× speedup with minimal visible quality change—a "free lunch" universally adopted in community workflows. Tencent's official Twitter (@TXhunyuan, Dec 2024) explicitly endorsed TeaCache shortly after release.

	  **Sage Attention / FlashAttention-3**: **SageAttention 2** (Zhang et al. 2024) and **FlashAttention-3** (Shah et al. 2024) provide ~1.4-1.8× attention speedup on Hopper/Ada GPUs through tiling and INT8/FP8 attention. Both are standard in production HunyuanVideo deployments.

	  **Combined Consumer Stack**: A typical RTX 4090 24GB workflow combines FP8 quantisation + TeaCache + SageAttention + selective block-swap, achieving ~3-5 minute generation of 5-second 540p video and ~8-12 minutes for 720p—competitive with Tencent's reference H100 timing despite running on a £1,600 consumer card versus a £25,000+ data-centre GPU.

	  #### Inference Server Deployment

	  For production deployment Hunyuan Cloud (Tencent's commercial offering) and third-party providers (Replicate, Fal.ai, RunPod, Vast.ai, ModelsLab, Pollo AI) typically run BF16 on H100/A100 80GB with horizontal scaling. Inference cost as of Q1 2026: ~$0.02-0.05 per second of generated video (commercial provider list prices), placing HunyuanVideo at roughly half the cost of Runway Gen-3 ($0.05/s) and Kling 1.5 ($0.10/s) for equivalent output.

  - ### LoRA Ecosystem and Fine-Tuning

	  HunyuanVideo's open weights and tractable architecture have made it the centre of the **first mass-scale text-to-video LoRA ecosystem**, comparable to the StyleGAN2/Stable Diffusion 1.5 LoRA explosions in their respective eras.

	  #### Musubi-tuner Training Toolkit

	  **Musubi-tuner** (kohya-ss/musubi-tuner on GitHub) is the open-source LoRA training framework for HunyuanVideo developed by **kohya-ss** (the maintainer behind the dominant Stable Diffusion LoRA training toolkit sd-scripts and the older Kohya GUI). Musubi-tuner supports:

	  - **Rank-16/32/64/128 LoRAs** on transformer attention Q/K/V and MLP projections
	  - **Mixed-resolution training** allowing variable aspect ratios in a single training run
	  - **Cached latents** to avoid repeated VAE encoding of the same training videos
	  - **Bucket-based batching** grouping clips by aspect ratio and resolution
	  - **24GB VRAM minimum** (RTX 3090, RTX 4090) with BF16 precision and gradient checkpointing
	  - **Datasets as small as 16 video clips**, more typically 40-200 clips

	  Training time on RTX 4090 24GB: ~6-12 hours for a typical character or motion LoRA over 2000-4000 steps. Cloud training (RunPod, Vast.ai) on H100 80GB: ~1.5-3 hours for the same workload at $2-4 GPU cost.

  - ### Academic Context: Theoretical Foundations and Research Reception

	  HunyuanVideo sits at the convergence of several mature research threads.

	  #### Foundational Lineage

	  **Latent Diffusion Models (Rombach et al. 2022, Stable Diffusion)**: Established the paradigm of training diffusion models in VAE-compressed latent space rather than pixel space, reducing compute by 4-8×. HunyuanVideo extends this to video via the Causal 3D VAE.

	  **Diffusion Transformers (Peebles & Xie 2023, DiT)**: Replaced the U-Net backbone with a pure transformer operating on patch tokens. HunyuanVideo's MMDiT backbone is a direct descendant.

	  **MMDiT and Multi-Stream Transformers (Esser et al. 2024, Stable Diffusion 3 paper; Black Forest Labs FLUX.1)**: Introduced the dual-stream then single-stream pattern with separate text/image processing in early layers and joint processing in later layers. HunyuanVideo applies this to text-video.

	  **Flow Matching (Lipman et al. 2023; Liu et al. 2023 Rectified Flow; Tong et al. 2023 Conditional Flow Matching)**: Replaced ε-prediction DDPM with velocity-field regression along straight ODE paths. Adopted by FLUX.1, SD3, and HunyuanVideo as the de facto modern diffusion objective.

	  **Causal 3D VAE for Video (CogVideoX, OpenAI Sora system card 2024)**: Sora's system card disclosed a causal 3D video tokeniser as a key architectural component; HunyuanVideo's design is widely interpreted as a Sora-inspired open implementation.

	  **RoPE for High-Dimensional Inputs (Su et al. 2021; later 3D RoPE extensions in CogVideoX, OpenSora)**: Extending Rotary Position Embedding from 1D sequences to 3D spatiotemporal patches.

	  #### Technical Report and Citation Impact

	  **Kong et al. (2024) "HunyuanVideo: A Systematic Framework For Large Video Generative Models"** (arXiv:2412.03603), a 41-page technical report authored by 100+ Tencent researchers, accumulated **800+ citations within 4 months of release**, making it one of the fastest-cited video generation papers in history—comparable to the Stable Diffusion 3 and FLUX.1 papers in citation velocity.

	  The report's contributions cited by follow-on work include:
	  - Detailed ablations on dual-stream depth vs single-stream depth ratios
	  - The MLLM-based prompt encoder design (subsequently adopted by Wan 2.1 and several academic systems)
	  - Empirical evidence that 13B is approaching the saturation point for current video training datasets (further scaling shows diminishing returns without curated additional data)
	  - Open release of model card, evaluation results, and training data composition statistics (rare for production-scale systems)

	  #### Benchmark Position

	  HunyuanVideo has been evaluated on the major video generation benchmarks:

	  - **VBench (Huang et al. 2024)**: 80.0+ aggregate score, top-3 among open models at release, narrowing to top-5 by Q2 2025 as Wan 2.1 and Step-Video-T2V launched
	  - **EvalCrafter (Liu et al. 2023)**: Strong performance on object consistency, motion smoothness, prompt-following
	  - **MovieGen-Bench (Meta 2024)**: HunyuanVideo achieves 70-80% of Meta's MovieGen 30B closed model performance with 43% of the parameters
	  - **Human-preference studies (LMSYS Chatbot Arena style)**: Comparable to Kling 1.5 (closed), MiniMax Hailuo (closed), notably above Mochi 1 (open)

	  #### Subsequent Tencent Releases

	  - **Hunyuan Video-I2V (March 2025)**: Image-to-video variant fine-tuning HunyuanVideo to accept a starting frame; produces 5-second animations from a single still image, used heavily for "still-to-motion" workflows in advertising and social media
	  - **HunyuanVideo-Avatar (Feb-Mar 2025)**: Digital human specialisation supporting full-body and head-and-shoulders avatar animation conditioned on audio (lip-sync) and reference image
	  - **Hunyuan-Large (Nov 2024)**: 389B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts LLM, competitive with GPT-4-class systems; provides the prompt-rewriter capability of the Hunyuan Video pipeline
	  - **Hunyuan T1 (March 2025)**: Reasoning model (long chain-of-thought) competitive with DeepSeek R1

	  - **Hunyuan3D-2.0 (Jan 2025)**: Text-to-3D and image-to-3D generation, complementing the video model

  - ### Future Directions (2026-2030)

	  HunyuanVideo and its successors will be shaped by five intersecting trajectories.

	  #### Continued Scaling and Quality Convergence with Closed Models

	  **Hunyuan Video 2.0 / 3.0 trajectory**: Tencent is expected to release follow-on open versions through 2026-2027 at 20-30B parameter scale, narrowing the gap with closed Sora 2 / Veo 3. Industry analysts (a16z, Sequoia AI reports 2025-2026) project that open-source video quality will be **at or above 2024 commercial leaders** by Q4 2026 and **within 10-15% of Q4 2025 commercial leaders** by end-2027.

	  #### Real-Time and Low-Latency Generation

	  HunyuanVideo's multi-minute generation time on consumer hardware is the major friction point. Trajectories:
	  - **Distillation**: Adversarial Diffusion Distillation, Latent Consistency Models, Score Distillation porting to HunyuanVideo will reduce sampling steps from 50 to 4-8 with minimal quality loss—reducing generation time to <30 seconds on RTX 4090
	  - **Streaming generation**: Causal 3D VAE design enables streaming inference; community efforts to deliver true real-time generation are well underway
	  - **Edge deployment**: Apple Vision Pro 2, Meta Quest 4 (anticipated 2026-2027) will deploy distilled video generation locally for AR/VR content creation

	  **Projected Impact (2026-2028)**: Real-time open-weight video generation becomes mainstream; ~70% of HunyuanVideo workflows use distilled fast samplers by 2028.

	  #### Long-Form Generation (>30 seconds)

	  HunyuanVideo's 5-second training context is the dominant length restriction. Approaches:
	  - **Sliding-window inference**: Generate overlapping 5-second clips and stitch (already common, with visible artefacts)
	  - **Native long-context fine-tuning**: Train HunyuanVideo at 30+ second contexts (resource-intensive)
	  - **Hierarchical generation**: Outline + scene-level generation (Hunyuan-Storyteller-style extensions in development)
	  - **RIFLEx and context-length scaling**: Mathematical tricks to extend pretrained context windows

	  **Projected Impact (2026-2028)**: Native 30-60 second open-source video generation achievable by 2027; full short films (~3-5 minutes) by 2028-2029.

	  #### Audio-Video Joint Generation

	  HunyuanVideo currently generates silent video; audio is added separately (Stable Audio, MMAudio, Suno). Native audio-video joint generation is the next frontier:
	  - **MovieGen-style approaches** (Meta 2024): Joint text-conditional audio-video generation
	  - **Tencent's roadmap**: HunyuanVideo + audio likely Q3-Q4 2026
	  - **Sora 2 audio**: OpenAI added native audio in Sora 2 (Q1 2026), pressuring open-source counterparts

	  **Projected Impact (2026-2028)**: Open-source audio-video joint generation matures by 2027; native sync becomes the default.

	  #### Provenance, Watermarking, and Regulation

	  As HunyuanVideo continues to power both legitimate creative work and malicious deepfakes, the regulatory environment tightens:
	  - **C2PA adoption**: Open-weight models pressured to embed C2PA credentials at generation time
	  - **Mandatory watermarking**: EU AI Act Article 50, US Executive Order successor frameworks, UK Online Safety Act updates all moving toward mandatory provenance markers
	  - **Forensic detection**: GAN-trace-style analysis adapted to diffusion transformer artefacts achieving 95-99% AUROC on HunyuanVideo outputs

	  **Projected Impact (2026-2030)**: Open-weight models increasingly ship with optional but socially expected watermarking; community resistance to mandatory watermarking persists.
