### Technical Details
Key capabilities include:
- **Credential Storage**: Secure storage for government IDs, driving licences, healthcare records, professional certificates
- **Selective Disclosure**: Control over which credential attributes to share for each interaction
- **Decentralized Architecture**: Data distributed across blockchain rather than centralized servers
- **Standards Support**: W3C Verifiable Credentials, DIDs, ISO/IEC 18013-5 for mobile driver's licences, OpenID4VC

### Market Growth
The Digital ID Wallet market was valued at $3.40 billion in 2024, projected to reach $8.78 billion by 2030 at 17.09% CAGR. Juniper Research predicts installed digital ID apps will rise from 2.8 billion in 2025 to 6.2 billion by 2030 (121% increase).

### EU Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI)
Under eIDAS 2.0, EU member states must provide national digital ID apps by end of 2026. Regulated businesses must accept them for strong authentication by mid-2027. Over 350 companies and public authorities across 26 member states participate in large-scale pilots.

### Enterprise Applications
Digital ID wallets enable faster customer onboarding, reduced fraud, lower verification costs, and new trust-based business models. 1Kosmos secured $57 million in 2025 Series B funding for decentralized identity solutions. Ontology announced $10 million fund for digital ID technology adoption.
