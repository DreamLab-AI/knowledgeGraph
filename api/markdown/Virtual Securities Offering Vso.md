- ## Overview
	- Virtual Securities Offerings represent the application of security token offering (STO) frameworks to virtual world assets. STOs distribute blockchain-based tokens representing ownership of assets ranging from real estate and art to company equity. The tokenized securities market was valued at $202 billion in 2023 and is expected to reach $301 billion by 2030 with 5.96% CAGR. Unlike ICOs, STOs ensure investor protection through regulatory compliance.
- ## Technical Details
	- ### Token Structure
		- **Security Classification**: Tokens treated as regulated financial instruments
		- **Smart Contracts**: Automated compliance and transfer restrictions
		- **Fractional Ownership**: Assets split into smaller tradable units
		- **Blockchain Recording**: Immutable transaction and ownership history
	- ### Regulatory Framework
		- SEC oversight in the United States under traditional securities laws
		- EU regulations including MiCA for crypto-assets
		- UK crypto business legislation refinements in 2024
		- KYC/AML requirements for investor verification
	- ### Compliance Requirements
		- Investor accreditation verification
		- Anti-money laundering procedures
		- Know Your Customer identity checks
		- Securities registration or exemption filing
	- ### Platform Infrastructure
		- Security token exchanges for secondary trading
		- Custody solutions for token storage
		- Transfer agents for ownership records
		- Compliance automation systems
- ## Applications
	- Virtual land and real estate tokenization
	- Metaverse platform equity offerings
	- Digital art and collectible securities
	- Virtual world infrastructure investment
	- Cross-border virtual asset investment access
