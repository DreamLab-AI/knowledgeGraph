The measurable effects of AI adoption on organizational output, work intensity, and economic growth.

### Overview

- A study by Berkeley Haas professors Aruna Ranganathan and Shixi Maggie Ye, published in the Harvard Business Review, found that AI use in a 200-employee technology company increased and intensified work rather than reducing it. *(Source: Aruna Ranganathan and Shixi Maggie Ye (Berkeley Haas) via Harvard Business Review, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-25)*
