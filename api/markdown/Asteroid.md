An asteroid is a rocky remnant of the formation of the solar system, sometimes called a minor planet.[^1][^2]

NASA places most asteroids in the main asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. Unlike the planets, which grew large and spherical, asteroids remained as broken building blocks that preserve some of the raw materials from which planets and other bodies formed.[^1][^2]

Some asteroids reach the neighbourhood of Earth. NASA's CNEOS states that the vast majority of near-Earth objects are asteroids, which it calls Near-Earth Asteroids.[^3]

Near-Earth status is an orbital category and does not imply a particular size or hazard. The sources used here do not establish one composition for all asteroids, and they do not set the boundary between an asteroid and other small bodies.
