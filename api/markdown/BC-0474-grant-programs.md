### Technical Details
Key program structures include:
- **Committee-Based**: Elected reviewers evaluate and approve applications
- **Community Voting**: Token holders vote on grant proposals
- **Quadratic Funding**: Matching funds based on number of unique contributors
- **Retroactive Funding**: Rewards for demonstrated impact after the fact

### Major Programs
- **Gitcoin Grants**: Quadratic funding rounds for public goods
- **Optimism RetroPGF**: Over $100 million allocated across four rounds
- **Ethereum Foundation**: Ecosystem development grants
- **Uniswap Grants**: Protocol development and community initiatives
- **Compound Grants**: DeFi ecosystem development

### Funding Mechanisms
- **Milestone-Based**: Payments released upon deliverable completion
- **Streaming**: Continuous funding through protocols like Sablier
- **Retroactive**: Payment after demonstrated value creation
- **Matching Pools**: Amplifying small donations through algorithmic matching

### Best Practices
- Clear eligibility criteria and application processes
- Transparent evaluation and decision-making
- Regular reporting and accountability requirements
- Impact measurement and program iteration
