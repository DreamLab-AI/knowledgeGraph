A spectrum is the distribution of a quantity, such as radiant energy or signal amplitude, across wavelength or frequency [^1] [^2]. SWEET treats the spectrum as a kind of spectral analysis [^3].

NASA describes sunlight passing through a prism to produce the spectrum, each band of colour being composed of many individual wavelengths that sensitive instruments such as spectrometers can detect [^1]. In signal processing an amplitude spectrum is a plot of sinusoid amplitudes as a function of frequency, with a phase spectrum plotting their phases [^2].

SWEET also records the power spectrum as equivalent to the spectrum class [^3]. NASA names the study of the production, measurement and interpretation of electromagnetic spectra as spectroscopy [^1].

Nothing in the retained sources establishes how an Earth observation spectrum differs from a spectral signature or spectral band, which the corpus keeps as separate terms.
