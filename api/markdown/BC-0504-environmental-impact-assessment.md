### Technical Details
Key assessment approaches include:
- **Systematic Literature Review**: PRISMA methodology analyzing technical and policy papers
- **Bibliometric Analysis**: VOSviewer and BERT topic modeling for discourse analysis
- **Consensus Mechanism Comparison**: PoW, PoS, DPoS, PoA, PoSpace, DAGs, BFT, PoH evaluation
- **Design Science Methodology**: Blockchain-based frameworks for automated data collection

### Assessment Frameworks

**PwC Blockchain Sustainability Framework**
First-of-its-kind framework for environmental footprint evaluation. Includes:
- Environmental impact quantification
- Comparative assessments using common framework
- Blockchain simulation modeling for future projections
- MiCAR reporting addendum

### Research Methodology
2024 systematic reviews synthesized 53 peer-reviewed studies and industry reports from 2018-2024. Focus on eight consensus mechanisms and architectural solutions including sharding, rollups, and modular blockchain frameworks.

### Regulatory Alignment
EU MiCA regulation includes sustainability indicators in mandatory reporting. Assessment methodology must align with:
- SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals)
- IPCC climate guidelines
- ESMA and ISSB standards
- GRI, TCFD, ESRS, IFRS S1/S2 frameworks

### Blockchain Benefits for EIA
- Secure storage of impact assessment data
- Integrity and traceability guarantees
- Precise tracking of carbon emissions in supply chains
- Enhanced compliance with ESG standards
