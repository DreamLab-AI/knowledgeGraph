### Technical Details
Key blockchain applications include:
- **Digital Product Passports (DPPs)**: Digital twins updated in real-time with manufacturing data, journey history, sustainability metrics, repairs, and degradation
- **Material Traceability**: Secure records for material sourcing, processing, and end-of-life recovery
- **Tokenized Incentives**: Smart contracts rewarding circular behavior like recycling and repair
- **Energy Traceability**: Tracking energy consumption, sources, and environmental impact throughout product lifecycle

### Research Progress
From 2021 to 2023, technical architecture development introduced blockchain and AI components with repair-service APIs for provenance tracking and lifecycle extension. Simulations indicate 25% increase in average product lifespan. In 2024, governance refinement applied smart contracts and tokenized circular incentives promoting ethical alignment.

### Industry Applications
A 2024 study showed blockchain-based supply chains considering circular economy and Industry 5.0 principles lead to waste reduction, improved inventory management, battery design optimization, decreased raw material usage, and enhanced efficiency for electric vehicle batteries.

### Environmental Considerations
Most public blockchains use less energy-intensive consensus methods like Proof-of-Stake rather than Bitcoin's Proof-of-Work. Academic interest in blockchain for circular economy grew from 5 articles in 2019 to 147 in 2023, with 166 total articles by April 2024.
