
Consumer trust is the confidence buyers place in a brand, product, or supply chain to behave reliably, safely, and ethically. It is built through verifiable provenance, transparent practices, consistent quality, and accountable handling of grievances. In supply-chain contexts it is increasingly underpinned by traceability data and ethical-sourcing attestations.

- ### Content
  - Trust is earned through verifiable provenance, transparent labelling, and reliable redress, and eroded by opacity or scandal. Digital traceability, certifications, and tamper-evident records let firms substantiate claims about origin, labour conditions, and environmental impact, converting trust from a reputational asset into auditable evidence.
