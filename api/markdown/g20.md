- ### Definition
  - A forum for [[Economic Governance]]
  - Drives [[International Cooperation]]
  - Works alongside the [[International Monetary Fund]]
  - Mandates standards via the [[FSB]]
- ### Overview
  - The G20 emerged as the premier forum for international economic cooperation, especially after the 2008 crisis.
  - It lacks a permanent secretariat and operates through a rotating presidency and tracks of working groups.
  - Its consensus communiqués steer the work of standard-setting and multilateral financial institutions.
- ### Key aspects
  - Leaders' summits and rotating presidency
  - Finance and Sherpa tracks of negotiation
  - Coordination of macroeconomic policy
  - Mandates to the FSB, IMF and Basel Committee
  - Engagement groups (Business, Labour, Civil)
- ### Applications
  - Global financial stability coordination
  - Crisis response and stimulus alignment
  - International tax and regulatory reform
  - Development, climate finance and debt relief
