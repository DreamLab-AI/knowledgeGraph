A spaceflight programme is a planned process to execute a series of spaceflights.[^1]

This is the definition of the class labelled spaceflight program in the Space Life Sciences Ontology, an application ontology intended to support the operation of NASA's Life Sciences Data Archive and other systems that contain space life science research data.[^1][^2]

Such ontology covers aspects of investigation design and execution that are specific or unique to space environments, and NASA describes it as developed with the OBO Foundry.[^3] Its purpose is to describe and organise space life science metadata so that data can be curated and integrated across systems.[^3][^2]

Such a definition is an ontology modelling choice, not an agency's organisational or legal definition of a programme. These sources do not say how a programme relates to missions, projects or investigations in practice.[^1][^3]
