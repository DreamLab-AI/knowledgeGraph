- ### Content
  - Cambridge Judge Business School is the graduate business school of the University of Cambridge, offering MBA, executive and doctoral programmes alongside research centres in areas such as alternative finance, entrepreneurship and risk. Its Centre for Alternative Finance produces widely cited studies on cryptocurrency and fintech markets.
  - The school combines management education with research that informs policy and industry, including benchmarking of crypto-asset and blockchain activity. It sits within the wider University of Cambridge and collaborates across its departments.
