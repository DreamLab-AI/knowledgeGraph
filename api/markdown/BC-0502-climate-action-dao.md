### Technical Details
Key DAO mechanisms include:
- **Carbon Token Backing**: Each token backed by minimum one carbon tonne in treasury
- **On-Chain Retirement**: Transparent, verifiable carbon credit retirement
- **Governance Voting**: Token holder decisions on project funding and protocol development
- **Treasury Management**: Collective allocation of carbon assets and funding

### KlimaDAO Overview
Established August 2021 on Polygon blockchain (Layer-2 for efficiency). Every KLIMA token backed by at least one carbon tonne. Treasury holds 18+ million tokenized carbon credits. $1.6 million allocated to funding new carbon projects.

**2024 Developments**
- Carbon-neutral NFT marketplace launched
- Automatic 120% emission offset on transactions
- Enhanced role as climate finance capital allocator

### Other Climate DAOs

**Regen Network**
DAO model since 2021 with REGEN token governance. Focuses on ecological asset tokenization and regenerative practices.

**DAO IPCI**
Distributed ledger for environmental asset allocation and management. Integration with carbon market institutions and registries.

**Toucan Protocol**
Bridges real-world carbon credits to blockchain as Tokenized CO2 (TCO2). Infrastructure for carbon offsetting.

**Energy Web Foundation**
Blockchain for tracking carbon offsets across supply chains with transaction reliability.

### Benefits of Blockchain Carbon Markets
- Immutable transaction records eliminate fraud
- Transparency builds market trust
- Smart contracts automate verification
- Decentralized access democratizes participation
