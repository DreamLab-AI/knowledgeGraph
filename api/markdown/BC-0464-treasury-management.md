### Technical Details
Key treasury management practices include:
- **Multi-Signature Wallets**: Gnosis Safe and similar tools requiring multiple approvals
- **Governance Voting**: Token holder votes on fund allocations and expenditures
- **Smart Contract Automation**: Automated fund management and decision execution
- **Diversification Strategies**: Balancing native tokens with stablecoins and other assets

### Treasury Composition (2024-2025)
Current DAO treasury statistics:
- **Total Holdings**: Approximately $21.4 billion in liquid assets, $24.5 billion total
- **Average Size**: $1.2 million per DAO
- **Native Tokens**: 67.3% (high concentration risk)
- **Stablecoins**: 18.2% for liquidity
- **Other Assets**: 14.5% diversification

### Risk Factors
- **Single-Asset Concentration**: 85% of DAOs maintain dangerous single-asset positions
- **Security Incidents**: Over $2.2 billion stolen across 303 incidents in 2024
- **Volatility Exposure**: Native token treasuries subject to market fluctuations
- **Regulatory Uncertainty**: Legal and tax risks for DAO participants

### Best Practices
- Set aside stablecoin reserves for operational continuity
- Create treasury diversification plans to reduce volatility risk
- Implement multi-sig controls with distributed key holders
- Establish clear governance processes for fund allocation

### Platform Tools
- **Gnosis Safe**: Secure multi-signature wallet management
- **Snapshot**: Off-chain voting on treasury proposals
- **Aragon**: Integrated finance and governance management
- **Tally**: On-chain governance with treasury visibility
