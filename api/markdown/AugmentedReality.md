## Definition

Augmented Reality for telecollaboration provides real-time overlay of digital information onto physical environments, enabling remote experts to guide on-site workers through shared spatial annotations and virtual presence. AR systems combine computer vision, simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), spatial computing, and network connectivity to create persistent shared coordinate systems across distributed locations. Modern AR collaboration platforms utilize optical see-through displays (Microsoft HoloLens, Magic Leap) and video see-through devices (smartphones, tablets) with cloud anchors and spatial mapping services for multi-user experiences. Key capabilities include 3D annotation tools, gesture-based interaction, voice commands, real-time object recognition, and spatial audio for natural communication. Industry applications span remote assistance, collaborative design reviews, training simulations, field service support, and healthcare consultations. Advanced implementations integrate 5G edge computing for low-latency rendering, AI-powered scene understanding for context-aware assistance, and WebXR standards for cross-platform AR collaboration accessible through web browsers.
