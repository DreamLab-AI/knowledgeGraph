### Technical Details
Key blockchain applications include:
- **Goods Passport ID (GPID)**: Standardized data set containing seller, buyer, invoice value, quantity, origin, and classification information
- **Real-Time Verification**: Document authentication and data validation with customs authorities
- **Automated Risk Assessment**: Smart contracts reduce manual intervention and human errors
- **Document Digitization**: Invoices, certificates of origin, and trade documents on immutable ledger

### Major Initiatives
- **Dubai Customs**: Gitex 2024 showcase of smart contracts streamlining trade document verification
- **Singapore TradeTrust**: Blockchain-based authentication of commercial documents reducing fraud
- **Korea Customs Service**: Digitization initiative improving traceability and operational efficiency
- **Open Customs Blockchain**: Multi-stakeholder initiative developing open-source customs data exchange components

### International Collaboration
- **Cadena Platform**: Information sharing on Authorized Economic Operators among Costa Rica, Mexico, and Peru for Mutual Recognition Arrangements
- **Saudi Arabia**: Exploring common blockchain platform for real-time customs declaration exchange
- **Morocco PortNet**: Blockchain implementation facilitating and streamlining import customs processes

### Benefits
Blockchain simplifies customs procedures, improves global supply chain management, prevents customs-related risks, enables real-time product verification, creates transparency and trust in cross-border networks, and reduces paper-based documentation.
