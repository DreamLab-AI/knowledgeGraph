### Technical Details
Key components include:
- **AI Watermarking**: Invisible markers embedded for identification and origin tracing
- **Content Provenance**: Metadata encoding source of origin and alteration history
- **C2PA Standard**: Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity open technical specification
- **Tamper-Evident Records**: Blockchain and PKI ensuring alteration attempts are detectable

### Regulatory Framework
- **EU AI Act (2024)**: Article 50 requires AI outputs marked in machine-readable format
- **California Provenance Act**: From February 2025, requires imperceptible watermarks in synthetic content
