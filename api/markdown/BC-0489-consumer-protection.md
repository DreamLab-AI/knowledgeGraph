### Technical Details
Key protection principles include:
- **Segregation**: Client assets held separately from company assets
- **Custody Controls**: Assets maintained with qualified custodians
- **Activity Separation**: Custody separated from trading operations
- **Recourse**: Clear avenues for dispute resolution

### Regulatory Developments

**SEC Custody Rule**
September 2024 enforcement action against investment adviser for failing to maintain crypto assets with qualified custodian. $225,000 penalty after FTX-related losses.

**New York DFS Guidance**
Custodians must separately account for customer virtual currency, treat it as belonging solely to customers, and maintain clear records for tracing.

**South Korea VAUPA**
Virtual Asset User Protection Act (July 2024) requires exchanges to segregate client assets, maintain insurance, and report suspicious activity.

### Protection Gaps
- No SIPC protection for non-security crypto assets
- Regulatory fragmentation between jurisdictions
- Inconsistent custody standards across platforms
- Limited deposit insurance for crypto holdings

### IMF Recommendations
Strong AML/CFT, prudential, and conduct rules should cover all entities related to issuance, trading, custody, or transfer of crypto. Comprehensive policies needed to protect both investors and financial system stability.

### Custodian Industry Scale
Traditional custodian banks held over $234 trillion in customer assets globally in 2024, maintaining 80-year track record of safeguarding through segregation, separation, and proper controls.
