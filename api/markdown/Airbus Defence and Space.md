Airbus Defence and Space is the Airbus space business, providing spacecraft and electronic systems for orbital missions.[^1][^2]

Airbus makes Sentinel-2, MetOp and communications and observation satellites.[^1] ESA engaged Airbus Defence and Space to build the Orion European Service Module with partners from Europe.[^3] UK records show Airbus Defence and Space UK for SKYNET 6A; Airbus Defence and Space made SKYNET 5 as Paradigm Secure Communications.[^4]

In January 2026 Airbus Defence and Space and Hisdesat agreed to extend their collaboration in radar Earth observation by commercialising imagery and applications from future PAZ-2 satellites.[^2] That release describes a commercial agreement but does not confirm who builds PAZ-2.

Sources cited here document main-contractor roles in named programmes and the company's own description of its work. Corporate structure, capitalization, financial results and comparative ranking among space primes are not covered. The SKYNET 6A launch date is a plan stated on the UK government page and may change.[^4]
