- ## Overview
	- Blockchain technology revolutionizes waste management by providing transparent, immutable tracking of materials from generation through disposal or recycling. Coca-Cola uses blockchain to record and reward informal waste collectors in Africa. Plastic Bank engages communities by paying tokens for collected plastics. The TRACKCYCLE consortium (TotalEnergies, Recycling Technologies, Circulor) provides fully traceable recycled plastic materials using blockchain.
- ## Technical Details
	- ### Blockchain Platforms
		- **Ethereum**: Smart contracts for recycling automation and carbon credits
		- **VeChain**: Supply chain tracking with IoT integration for e-waste
		- **Custom Solutions**: Waste24 blockchain for municipal waste services
	- ### Key Applications
		- **Material Traceability**: Track waste from generation to final processing
		- **Incentive Systems**: Token-based rewards for recycling participation
		- **Digital Product Passports**: Material composition and disposal recommendations
		- **Compliance Verification**: Automated regulatory reporting
	- ### Implementation Examples
		- Plastic Bank token rewards for ocean plastic collection
		- Smart container monitoring for fill levels and collection
		- Recycled material certification and provenance tracking
		- Grey-market prevention for electronics waste
- ## Applications
	- Municipal waste tracking and optimization
	- Corporate sustainability reporting
	- Recycling incentive programs
