### Technical Details
Key regulatory components include:
- **Crypto-Asset Service Providers (CASPs)**: Authorization required to operate across EU
- **Asset-Referenced Tokens (ARTs)**: Stablecoins pegged to multiple assets or commodities
- **E-Money Tokens (EMTs)**: Stablecoins pegged to single fiat currency
- **Utility Tokens**: Functional tokens providing access to services

### Implementation Timeline
- **June 30, 2024**: Stablecoin rules (ARTs and EMTs) became applicable
- **December 30, 2024**: Full MiCA application for CASPs
- **July 1, 2026**: End of transitional period for grandfathered entities
- **18-month transition**: Compliance alignment period for existing providers

### Key Requirements
- **Whitepaper Publication**: Disclosure documents for crypto-asset offerings
- **Reserve Requirements**: Asset backing for stablecoin issuers
- **Market Abuse Prevention**: Insider trading and manipulation rules
- **Consumer Protection**: Disclosure, suitability, and custody safeguards

### Benefits
- **Passporting Rights**: Single authorization valid across all 27 EU member states
- **Market of 450 Million**: Access to unified European crypto market
- **Regulatory Certainty**: Clear compliance requirements and enforcement expectations

### Exclusions
NFTs are generally excluded unless features requalify them as covered crypto-assets. DeFi protocols may require additional legislation expected by mid-2025.
