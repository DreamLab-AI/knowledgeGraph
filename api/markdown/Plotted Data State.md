A plotted data state is the condition of data after they have been rendered as a plot, such as a map, line plot or contour plot.[^1][^2]

Giovanni lists time-averaged maps, comparison plots, vertical plots and animations among its services, and Panoply creates colour contour plots and line plots from arrays.[^1][^2] The state therefore describes a presentation of the data and not a new measurement or derived variable.

No retained source defines a data state of this name, and they do not say whether a plot is recorded as a product with its own provenance. They give no figures.[^1][^2]

[^1]: NASA Earthdata, “Giovanni”. https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/giovanni
[^2]: NASA GISS, “Panoply 5 netCDF, HDF and GRIB Data Viewer”. https://www.giss.nasa.gov/tools/panoply/
