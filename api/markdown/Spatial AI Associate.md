## Overview

Spatial AI Associates represent the convergence of AI and spatial computing, enabling intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, and act within three-dimensional physical and virtual environments. These agents move beyond passive tool functionality to become active collaborators that recognise and respond to individual and team-level needs in real time.

## Key Capabilities
	- **Scene Understanding**: Real-time reconstruction and identification of surfaces, objects, and their properties
	- **Natural Language Interaction**: Conversational speech-based interaction with AR environments
	- **Context Awareness**: Anticipating user needs based on behaviour, location, and environmental context
	- **Visual Guidance**: Overlaying annotated instructions directly onto physical equipment
	- **Team Coordination**: Dynamic generation of collaborative resources like virtual blackboards and shared mental models

## 2025 Applications
	- **Healthcare (SAIMA)**: Surgical planning with 3D brain models from MRI/CT scans overlaid in AR glasses
	- **Remote Expertise**: Real-time expert guidance with annotations streamed to field technicians
	- **Manufacturing**: Predictive maintenance and quality assurance through sensor data interpretation
	- **Autonomous Navigation**: Real-time perception and path planning for vehicles and robots
