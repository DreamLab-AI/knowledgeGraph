- ## Overview
	- Virtual assets are digital representations of value regulated under FATF guidelines for anti-money laundering purposes. As of 2024, 75% of jurisdictions remain only partially compliant with FATF virtual asset standards, with ongoing concerns about stablecoins and DeFi platforms.
- ## Technical Details
	- ### FATF Definition
		- Digital representation of value
		- Can be digitally traded or transferred
		- Can be used for payment or investment
		- Does not include digital fiat currency (CBDCs)
	- ### Types of Virtual Assets
		- **Cryptocurrencies**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins
		- **Stablecoins**: USDT, USDC, DAI
		- **NFTs**: Non-fungible tokens for digital collectibles
		- **Security Tokens**: Tokenized securities
		- **Utility Tokens**: Platform access tokens
	- ### Regulatory Frameworks
		- FATF Recommendation 15 for VASPs
		- Travel Rule for transaction transparency
		- EU MiCA regulation
		- US SEC/CFTC jurisdiction
- ## Applications
	- Cryptocurrency payments and transfers
	- Digital collectible ownership
	- DeFi protocol participation
	- Metaverse economy transactions
	- Cross-border value transfer
