Hydrosphere Feature is a distinguishable component of Earth's water system, such as a body of water, a water surface or a channel margin, as grouped under that class in SWEET.[^1]

SWEET supplies the feature grouping. In its hydrology file the classes bank and groundwater level are each declared subclasses of hydrosphere feature, and the class itself sits under planetary realm.[^1] This page therefore stays above those children, which are defined on Hydrographic Bank and Groundwater Level.[^1]

NASA's glossary defines the hydrosphere itself as all of Earth's water, including surface water in oceans, lakes and rivers, groundwater, snow cover, ice and atmospheric water vapour.[^2] NASA Earthdata narrows the terrestrial hydrosphere to water on the land surface and underground in lakes, rivers and groundwater, together with total water storage.[^3]

Retained sources define the hydrosphere and do not define a feature of it, so the membership rule for the class rests on the SWEET structure and not on a glossary entry.[^1][^2][^3]
