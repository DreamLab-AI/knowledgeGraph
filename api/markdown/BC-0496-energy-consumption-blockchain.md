### Technical Details
Key energy metrics include:
- **Bitcoin (PoW)**: Approximately 200 TWh annually, comparable to Sweden's total consumption
- **Ethereum (PoS)**: Approximately 0.0026 TWh annually after The Merge
- **Single Transaction**: Bitcoin ~830 kWh vs Algorand ~0.000008 kWh
- **Equivalence**: One Bitcoin transaction equals ~680,000 Visa transactions

### Consensus Mechanism Impact
- **Proof-of-Work (PoW)**: Energy-intensive mining competition requiring specialized hardware
- **Proof-of-Stake (PoS)**: Validators selected by staked tokens, minimal energy requirements
- **Ethereum Transition**: 99.95% energy reduction from PoW to PoS

### Energy-Efficient Platforms
- **Cardano**: PoS design with household-equivalent energy consumption
- **Algorand**: Pure PoS with near-zero transaction energy
- **Hedera**: Hashgraph consensus supporting 100,000 TPS with minimal footprint
- **IOTA**: Tangle architecture without traditional mining

### Regulatory Pressure
Swedish and Norwegian regulators petitioned EU for PoW mining ban in 2021, citing renewable energy competition. Environmental concerns drive regulatory scrutiny and investor ESG requirements.

### Sustainability Solutions
- Renewable energy-powered mining operations
- Carbon offset programs for blockchain networks
- Protocol migration from PoW to PoS
- Energy-efficient consensus mechanism adoption
