Photosphere is the layer of the Sun commonly called its surface, and the first layer of the solar atmosphere.[^1]

Our Sun has no solid surface, so the term is a convention. NASA gives its temperature as about 5,500 degrees Celsius, cooler than the core and far cooler than the corona. Most of the Sun's radiation escapes outward from it into space.[^1]

SWEET ontology classes it as a subclass of stellar atmosphere, so the term is applied to stars generally. NASA's coverage addresses the Sun only and does not describe other stars.[^2]
