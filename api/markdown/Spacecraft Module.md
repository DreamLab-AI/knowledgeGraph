A spacecraft module is a self-contained unit of a spacecraft.[^1]

No size, pressurisation or separation criteria are given in the ontology term, so it covers units of different kinds. On the International Space Station, NASA describes Harmony as a module that works as a utility hub, supplying air, electrical power, water and other systems needed to support life on the station.[^1][^2]

This example shows a module defined by its role in the vehicle rather than by a fixed design. Sources do not establish a general classification of modules, and other agencies may divide their vehicles differently.[^1][^2]

[^2]: NASA, “Harmony Module”. https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/harmony-module/
