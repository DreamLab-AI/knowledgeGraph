Surrey Satellite Technology Limited (SSTL) is a UK company, based in Guildford, that designs and builds small satellites.[^1][^2]

SSTL describes itself on its home page as a builder of small satellites with a long record, and the page carries a news headline about an ESA prime contractor award for excellence.[^1] The linked announcement was not opened, so the basis and scope of that award are not established here.

A UK Government release says SSTL designed and built the HydroGNSS satellites with funding from the UK Space Agency, and the same release thanks SSTL as the mission's prime contractor.[^2] The UK Space Agency annual report also credits SSTL with building HydroGNSS.[^3] A 2024 release says the Ministry of Defence awarded SSTL the Juno satellite contract through competitive procurement and that SSTL manufactured the earlier Tyche satellite. The launch date in that release was an expectation, and current status was not checked.[^4]

These sources describe particular programmes and do not establish SSTL's wider market position or ownership.
