
The UK AI Opportunities Action Plan is a 2025 UK Government strategy setting out recommendations to accelerate national adoption of artificial intelligence across the public and private sectors. It covers building sovereign compute and data infrastructure, expanding AI skills, establishing AI Growth Zones, and embedding AI in public services to drive productivity and economic growth.

- ### Content
  - The plan emphasises sovereign capability, including expanded public compute and AI Growth Zones, alongside talent pipelines and regulatory enablement. It positions AI as a lever for economic growth and public-service reform, framing government as an early adopter and infrastructure provider rather than purely a regulator.
