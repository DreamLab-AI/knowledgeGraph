Uncertainty propagation is the combination of the standard uncertainties of input estimates into a combined standard uncertainty for a result computed from them.[^1]

In the Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement, the combined standard uncertainty is the estimated standard deviation of the result, equal to the positive square root of a combined variance. That variance is built from variance and covariance components using the law of propagation of uncertainty, so correlated inputs enter through their covariances.[^1]

Earth observation quality frameworks apply the same metrological ideas. QA4EO asks that data and derived products carry an indicator of quality traceable to reference standards, and names uncertainty analysis among the supporting principles.[^2]

The Guide addresses measurement in general. The cited sources do not give a worked retrieval chain for satellite data, so how inputs and their correlations are chosen for a particular product remains a matter for that product's documentation.[^1][^2]
