An organizational approach where the Chief Executive Officer provides direct, high-level oversight and strategic direction for artificial intelligence initiatives to ensure alignment with business goals.

### Overview

- KPMG's Global AI Pulse Survey for Q2 found that CEO-led AI efforts were 3x more likely to produce ROI than efforts where the CEO was less involved. *(Source: KPMG Global AI Pulse Survey (Q2), via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
