### Technical Details
Key blockchain ESG applications include:
- **Immutable Record Keeping**: Tamper-proof sustainability data storage
- **Smart Contract Automation**: Automated data collection, analysis, and reporting
- **Real-Time Dashboards**: Live updates for regulators, investors, stakeholders
- **Carbon Credit Tokenization**: Fractional ownership and transparent trading

### Regulatory Framework

**EU CSRD**
Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive mandates comprehensive emissions disclosure from 2024. 96% of world's top 250 companies issue regular sustainability reports.

**International Standards**
ISSB sustainability disclosure standards, GRI, SASB, and TCFD frameworks provide reporting guidance. EU sustainable finance taxonomy operational since July 2020.

### Anti-Greenwashing Benefits
2020 European Commission research found 42% of green claims were exaggerated, false, or deceptive. 2022 Google Cloud poll showed 58% of executives believed their companies engaged in greenwashing. Blockchain provides verification to combat false claims.

### Market Growth
ESG assets under management projected to reach USD 40 trillion by 2030 (25% of global AuM). Blockchain market growing from USD 17.6 billion (2023) to projected USD 1,879.3 billion by 2034.

### Industry Tools
- **PwC Blockchain Sustainability Framework**: First-of-its-kind assessment framework with MiCAR addendum
- **DiginexClimate**: Blockchain-based ESG reporting for GRI, SASB, TCFD compliance
- **Carbon Credit Trading**: Tokenized green bonds and renewable energy credits
