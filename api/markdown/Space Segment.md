In the usage of the European Space Agency's mission-operations organisation, the space segment comprises the launch vehicle and the satellite.[^1]

ESA's brochure defines the ground segment as all elements, facilities and services needed in support of mission operations on the ground, and uses that term to distinguish those elements from the space segment. Its list of major ground elements includes a ground station network, control centre facilities and communications systems linking the elements together.[^1]

Within the satellite, NASA's small-spacecraft report says each spacecraft relies on a bus that provides essential services to the payload.[^2] NASA's Spacecraft Basics places the bus within the structure subsystem of the spacecraft.[^3] These sources describe the spacecraft side of the boundary and do not define the segment itself.

One brochure from 2001 provides the primary definition, and the retained sources do not show how other organisations use the term. Some usage may count only the orbiting spacecraft and not the launch vehicle, and the sources do not address a separate user segment.
