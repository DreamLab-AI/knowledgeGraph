The reduction or elimination of human labor roles due to the automation and efficiency gains provided by artificial intelligence systems.

### Overview

- **[Emerging signal]** A world where AI displaces 8 million workers may be harder to handle socially and economically than a world where it displaces 80 million, because limited displacement does not force a wholesale economic restructuring. *(Source: Ezra Klein, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
