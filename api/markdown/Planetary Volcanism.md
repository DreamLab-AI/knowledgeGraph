Planetary volcanism is volcanic activity on a body other than Earth, and NASA describes Jupiter’s moon Io as the most volcanically active world in the solar system.[^1]

NASA says Io has hundreds of volcanoes, some erupting lava fountains dozens of miles high, and lakes of molten silicate lava on its surface.[^1] It also says Io’s eruptions are at times powerful enough to be seen with large telescopes on Earth.[^1]

NASA reports that Mars has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, standing more than 25 miles tall from base to summit.[^2] The page does not say whether Olympus Mons is active today. The opened sources do not explain the mechanisms driving volcanism on either body.
