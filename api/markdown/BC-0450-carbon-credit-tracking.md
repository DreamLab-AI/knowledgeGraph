### Technical Details
Key mechanisms include:
- **Bridging Platforms**: Transfer credits from registries (Verra, Gold Standard) to custodial accounts, then mint corresponding tokens (Toucan Protocol, Flowcarbon)
- **Unique Token Identity**: Each carbon credit represented by unique digital token preventing duplicate claims
- **Smart Contract Automation**: Verification, transfer, and retirement of carbon credits based on predefined conditions
- **Registry Integration**: Real-world Verified Carbon Units linked to on-chain tokens

### Major Platforms
- **Toucan Protocol**: Converts retired carbon credits into Tokenized CO2 (TCO2) tokens
- **KlimaDAO**: Treasury holds Base Carbon Tonne (BCT) corresponding to Verra's Verified Carbon Units (established August 2021)
- **Nori**: Each Nori Removal Tonne (NRT) represents one tonne of removed CO2, stored minimum ten years, independently verified

### Market Growth
The blockchain-based carbon credit platform market was valued at $325 million in 2024, projected to reach $567 million by 2031 at 8.9% CAGR. Companies like Hivelance and Blockchain App Factory reported 40-50% year-over-year revenue growth in 2024.

### Verification Standards
Credits must be real, additional, permanent, robustly quantified, independently verified, and uniquely claimed. Standards like Verified Carbon Standard (VCS) and Gold Standard provide rigorous methodologies for project validation, monitoring, and verification.
