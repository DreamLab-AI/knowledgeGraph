Spherical harmonic analysis is the expansion of a function defined on a sphere in spherical harmonic basis functions, and SWEET classes it as a spectral analysis whose basis functions are spherical harmonic functions [^1].

Earth's gravity field is the clearest retained use, and the GRACE handbook states that a Level-2 gravity field product is a set of spherical harmonic coefficients of the exterior geopotential [^2]. Because the expansion is formally infinite, practice limits the summation to a maximum degree [^2].

Retained sources give no general mathematical definition and do not cover other geoscience applications such as magnetic field or topography models. They also leave open how SWEET intends analysis to relate to the inverse step of synthesising a field from its coefficients.
