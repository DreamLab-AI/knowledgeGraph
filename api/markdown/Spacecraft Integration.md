Spacecraft integration is the work of bringing a spacecraft's hardware and on-board systems together and making the assembled spacecraft compatible with its launch vehicle.[^1][^2]

NASA describes integration with the launch vehicle as a complex and critical part of a small-spacecraft mission. The effort typically involves the launch service provider, the spacecraft manufacturer, the customer and the launch range, and sometimes a broker or integrator who performs analyses and physical integration services.[^1]

Within the spacecraft, the bus structure provides points for holding and moving the spacecraft during construction, testing, transportation and launch. Where SpaceWire is used, ground support equipment can plug directly into the on-board data handling system to support integration and test.[^3][^2]

Integration sits inside the wider verification process, which ECSS applies from single equipment to the overall system. The sources are mainly about small spacecraft and do not set out a fixed sequence of integration steps.[^4]
