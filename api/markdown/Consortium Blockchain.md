- ## Technical Details
	- **Key Characteristics**:
		- Pre-approved membership with controlled access
		- Shared governance among multiple organizations
		- Higher throughput and lower latency than public blockchains
		- Enhanced security through limited node participation
	- **Consensus Mechanisms**: Proof of Vote (PoV), Proof of Authority (PoA), Raft, Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT)
	- **Leading Platforms**:
		- **Hyperledger Fabric**: Modular, customizable enterprise solution
		- **R3 Corda**: 200+ financial institutions, focused on banking and trade
		- **Quorum**: Ethereum-based with privacy enhancements (JPMorgan)
		- **Energy Web Chain**: Enterprise-grade platform for energy sector
	- **Enterprise Examples**:
		- R3 Consortium: Goldman Sachs, Credit Suisse, JP Morgan (2014)
		- IBM Food Trust: Walmart supply chain tracking on Hyperledger Fabric
		- Healthcare insurance claim processing across hospital-insurer networks
- ## Applications
	- Financial services settlement
	- Supply chain traceability
	- Healthcare data sharing
	- Trade finance processing
	- Cross-organizational document verification
