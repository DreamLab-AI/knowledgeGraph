### Technical Details
Key jurisdictional frameworks include:
- **Japan FSA**: Payment Services Act, Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, JVCEA self-regulation
- **Singapore MAS**: Major Payment Institution licenses, stablecoin framework, asset tokenization
- **Hong Kong SFC**: Virtual Asset Trading Platform licenses, spot crypto ETFs, stablecoin sandbox
- **South Korea**: Virtual Asset User Protection Act (July 2024)

### Regional Highlights

**Japan**
First major economy with comprehensive crypto regulation. Real estate tokenization under FIEA since November 2024. Licensed exchanges operated by JVCEA members.

**Singapore**
Granted 30+ Major Payment Institution licenses. No capital gains tax on crypto for individuals. Focus on institutional tokenization and DeFi innovation.

**Hong Kong**
First Asian jurisdiction approving spot Bitcoin and Ether ETFs. Territorial tax system with 16.5% on local profits only. 10 licensed Virtual Asset Trading Platforms.

**South Korea**
Most comprehensive consumer protection law in the region. Exchanges must segregate client assets, maintain insurance, and report suspicious activity.

### Market Position
Asia-Pacific accounts for over one-third of global crypto market share and remains the fastest-expanding region by activity, driven by exchange hubs in Singapore and Hong Kong.
