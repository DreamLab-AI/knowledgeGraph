A remediation plan is a structured set of corrective actions, owners, and deadlines drawn up to address identified non-conformities, risks, or harms discovered through audit or monitoring. It links each finding to a specific fix and tracks progress to closure. In compliance and ethical-sourcing contexts it is the mechanism that turns a violation into demonstrable improvement.

### Content

- Each plan assigns root-cause-based actions to accountable owners with target dates and verification criteria, then tracks them to closure. In supply-chain settings it documents how a supplier will correct labour or environmental breaches, providing evidence of continuous improvement to buyers and regulators.
