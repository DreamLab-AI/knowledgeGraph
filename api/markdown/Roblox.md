- ### Definition
- Roblox is an online platform for creating, publishing and playing user-generated 3D experiences. It is a [[Metaverse Platform]] that bundles a [[Game Engine]], a [[Virtual World]] and a [[Creator Economy]], and players enter experiences as a customisable [[Avatar]].
- ### Overview
- Roblox is simultaneously a development platform, a distribution storefront and a social network.
- Creators build experiences in an integrated authoring environment and publish them to a shared catalogue accessible across devices.
- An in-platform currency lets developers earn from engagement, anchoring a large [[Creator Economy]] and [[Virtual Economy]].
- ### Key aspects
- User-generated content is the core: the platform's value comes from creator-built experiences rather than first-party titles.
- Social presence: avatars, friends and shared spaces make it a [[Social Platform]] as much as a game catalogue.
- Cross-platform reach across mobile, desktop and console.
- ### Mechanisms
- The authoring environment exposes a scripting and physics layer over the [[Game Engine]].
- Cloud hosting runs creator experiences as multiplayer sessions for arbitrary numbers of users.
- The virtual currency mediates purchases and creator payouts, implementing the [[Virtual Economy]].
- ### Applications
- Social gaming and shared interactive spaces for younger audiences.
- Brand activations and virtual events within hosted experiences.
- A training ground and revenue channel for independent creators and studios.
