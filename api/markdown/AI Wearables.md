
A category of consumer hardware devices that integrate artificial intelligence capabilities directly into wearable form factors such as pendants, glasses, or rings to provide ambient assistance and data capture.

- ### Overview
  - Meta acquired AI device startup Limitless to integrate its team into Reality Labs, while discontinuing the sale of Limitless's pendant device and sunsetting its rewind software immediately. *(Source: Meta, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
  - **[Industry analysis]** The AI wearables category remains in a pre-product market fit stage, with multiple companies being acquired for their talent rather than their hardware, leading to devices falling by the wayside. *(Source: Host Analysis, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
