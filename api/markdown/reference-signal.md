- ### Definition
  - A reference signal is the setpoint or target trajectory that a [[Feedback Loop]] tries to track, sitting at the heart of [[Control Theory]] as the desired system behaviour.
- ### Content
  - Reference signals may be constant setpoints, step changes, or smooth time-varying trajectories generated by a planner. Feasibility constraints such as actuator limits and dynamics mean references are often shaped or filtered to remain trackable without saturating the system.
