A meteoroid is a rock in space, ranging in size from dust grains to small asteroids.[^1]

CNEOS generally defines it as an asteroid or comet fragment that orbits the Sun, with an approximate size between ten microns and a metre or so.[^2] The two statements frame size as an informal range rather than a sharp boundary.[^1][^2]

Meteoroids may be rocky, metallic or a combination of rock and metal.[^3]

Meteoroid applies while the object is in space. On entering an atmosphere the visible event is a meteor, and a survivor on the ground is a meteorite.[^1]
