- ## Overview
	- The metaverse economy is a burgeoning digital realm where economic activities occur within virtual environments. In 2024, the market value reached USD 105.4 billion, with projections exceeding USD 930 billion by 2030. Over USD 2 billion in NFT-based real estate was traded in 2024 alone. Major brands across fashion, automotive, technology, and entertainment have established metaverse presences through virtual stores and exclusive experiences.
- ## Technical Details
	- ### Core Components
		- **Digital Currencies**: Cryptocurrencies and platform-specific tokens
		- **Virtual Real Estate**: Blockchain-recorded land parcels as NFTs
		- **Digital Goods**: Avatar items, skins, virtual fashion, and collectibles
		- **Virtual Services**: Concerts, tourism, events, and professional services
	- ### Enabling Technologies
		- Blockchain for asset ownership and transaction verification
		- Non-fungible tokens (NFTs) for unique digital items
		- Smart contracts for automated agreements
		- Decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols
	- ### Economic Activities
		- Virtual real estate development and trading
		- Digital content creation and monetization
		- Virtual goods manufacturing and sales
		- In-world service provision and consulting
- ## Applications
	- Virtual real estate investment and development
	- Digital fashion and avatar customization markets
	- Gaming economies and play-to-earn models
	- Virtual events and entertainment monetization
