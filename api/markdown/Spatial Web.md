## Overview

The Spatial Web blurs boundaries between digital and geospatial physical objects, building on original W3C scopes around devices in the real world. It encompasses AR, VR, and mixed reality technologies while enabling both the construction of digital twins in virtual realms and the projection of digital information into physical environments.

## 2025 Developments
	- The collapse of walled gardens has enabled interoperability across platforms through standardised protocols for digital object persistence, user identity, and universal asset translation
	- Digital content created in one platform can now be placed, purchased, and viewed in another user's AR space regardless of hardware
	- The Hyper Space Transaction Protocol (HSTP) is emerging as the foundational protocol for 3D immersive browsing, analogous to HTTP for Web 2.0
	- Enterprise adoption accelerating with AR workflow implementations improving technician performance by 34%
	- Digital Twins 2.0 integrating spatial computing for real-time visualisation, predictive fault analysis, and guided maintenance

## Key Technologies
	- Extended Reality (XR): AR, VR, and Mixed Reality
	- Digital Twins: Real-time virtual representations of physical objects
	- Edge Computing: Local processing for low-latency spatial interactions
	- Blockchain: Decentralised ownership and asset authentication
	- 5G/6G: High-bandwidth, low-latency connectivity
