  - ## Technical Details
  - **Market Projections**: Metaverse market to reach $861.77B by 2030 (2024 report)
  - **Enabling Technologies**:
		- Blockchain for decentralized virtual economies and NFT ownership
		- Cryptocurrencies for seamless cross-platform transactions
		- Digital wallets for secure asset management
		- Interoperable digital identity systems
  - **Interoperability Benefits**:
		- Users move identity, preferences, digital assets across platforms
		- Cross-chain NFT transactions via interoperability networks
		- DMA regulation as test case for digital commerce governance
  - **Challenges**:

		- Universal data standards development
		- Security, scalability, and regulation concerns

  - Virtual goods marketplaces
  - Digital fashion and wearables
  - Gaming asset exchanges
