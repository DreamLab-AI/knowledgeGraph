- ## Technical Details
	- **Energy Efficiency**: Proof of Stake networks use tens of thousands of times less energy than Proof of Work (Bitcoin consumes 121+ TWh annually)
	- **Ethereum Transition**: PoS merge reduced energy consumption by 99.988% and carbon footprint by 99.992%
	- **Carbon Neutral Strategies**:
		- Energy-efficient consensus (PoS, DPoS, PPoS)
		- Renewable energy sourcing
		- Carbon offset programs
		- Proof of Green mechanisms
	- **Leading Green Blockchains**:
		- **Algorand**: Pure PoS with carbon offset programs
		- **Hedera**: Carbon neutral, targeting carbon negative
		- **Cardano**: Peer-reviewed PoS (Ouroboros)
		- **Avalanche**: Advanced consensus with minimal energy
	- **Industry Initiatives**: Crypto Climate Accord (250+ signatories, net-zero by 2030)
- ## Applications
