### Technical Details
Key measurement approaches include:
- **Energy-Based Calculation**: Total kWh consumed x emission factor (e.g., 0.85 kg CO2e/kWh)
- **ISO 14064 Alignment**: Standardized GHG accounting and verification
- **Network-Level Analysis**: Aggregate energy consumption across all nodes
- **Transaction-Level Attribution**: Emissions per transaction or user activity

### Measurement Organizations

**Crypto Carbon Ratings Institute (CCRI)**
Developed Crypto Climate Impact Accounting Framework with South Pole. Provides MiCA-compliant sustainability indicators. Example: Stellar network estimated at 481,324 kWh/year resulting in 173,243 kg CO2/year.

**Global Blockchain Business Council (GBBC)**
Global Standards Mapping Initiative (GSMI 5.0) released December 2024 for ecosystem analysis.

### EU MiCA Requirements
Sustainability indicators are mandatory in reporting for crypto asset issuers and service providers under MiCA regulation. Disclosure requirements effective from end of 2024.

### Measurement Challenges
- Heterogeneous energy measurements unable to be compared
- Varying results across different methodologies
- Lack of standardized protocols for recording carbon data
- Need for industry-wide standards for interoperability

### Key Metrics
- Energy consumption (kWh per transaction, annual total)
- Carbon intensity (kg CO2e per transaction)
- Renewable energy percentage
- Net emissions after offsets
