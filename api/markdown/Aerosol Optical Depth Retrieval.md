Aerosol optical depth retrieval is the derivation of aerosol optical depth, a measure of the aerosol loading of the atmospheric column, from remote observations.[^1][^2]

The MODIS aerosol products apply several algorithms over diverse surfaces: Deep Blue and Dark Target for land, and Dark Target for ocean and inland waters. Their primary data product is aerosol optical depth at a wavelength of 550 nm, with additional information such as spectral optical depth and quality assurance data.[^1]

MODIS products cover cloud-free, snow-free and ice-free surfaces, so retrievals are absent where those conditions fail.[^1] Ground-based sun photometer measurements of direct solar radiation give columnar optical depth by a different route, and AERONET processing includes quality levels in which the highest quality data come after final calibration.[^2]

Neither source gives retrieval accuracy, and the two routes are not interchangeable: one is a satellite retrieval and the other a ground measurement.[^1][^2]

[^2]: NASA AERONET, “Aerosol Optical Depth (V3) - Solar”. https://aeronet.gsfc.nasa.gov/new_web/aerosols.html
