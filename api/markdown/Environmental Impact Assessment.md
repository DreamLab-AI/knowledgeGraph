## Assessment Frameworks

### PwC Blockchain Sustainability Framework
- First-of-its-kind assessment framework
- Measures cumulative environmental footprint of blockchain operations
- Factors assessed: electricity use, GHG emissions, e-waste, embodied carbon
- Accounts for differences in consensus mechanisms

### Case Study: Stellar Network
- Annual electricity consumption: 481,324 kWh
- Annual CO2 emissions: approximately 173,243 kg
- Equivalent to 34 average US homes electricity use

## Key Environmental Variables

### Direct Impacts
- Electricity consumption (mining, validation, transactions)
- Greenhouse gas emissions from energy use
- E-waste from hardware obsolescence
- Water consumption for cooling systems

### Indirect Impacts
- Embodied carbon in hardware manufacturing
- Supply chain emissions
- Data centre construction footprint
- Network infrastructure requirements
