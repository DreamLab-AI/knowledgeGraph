A UK space prime is a prime contractor in the United Kingdom space sector, a term that the sources retained here use only in general terms and do not define as a formal category.[^1]

The UK Space Agency annual report for 2025 to 2026 identifies the scale-up gap (the difficulty of transitioning smaller companies into mid-tier and prime contractors) as an unresolved challenge. It also records HydroGNSS, a UK-built mission developed by SSTL with agency support, as an example. The report does not state whether SSTL performed as a prime contractor in the procurement sense.[^1]

UK government space procurement is coordinated through a cross-government category, which the Ministry of Defence guidance says grew out of defence work on the defence space supply chain. The guidance concerns how partners engage with government and does not list primes or set criteria for being one.[^2]

No official list of UK space primes was found in these sources. The page therefore treats the idea as a role in the supply chain rather than a registered status, and the company examples that belong under it are a matter for further sourcing.[^1][^2]
