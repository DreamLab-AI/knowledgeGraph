The European Parliament is the directly elected legislative body of the European Union, sharing law-making and budgetary authority with the Council of the EU. It debates, amends, and adopts EU legislation across domains including financial regulation, data protection, AI, and digital-currency policy. As a co-legislator it is a primary source of the binding rules that shape technology governance across member states.

### Content

- Together with the Council it amends and enacts EU law spanning finance, data, and AI. Its votes determine the regulatory frameworks, including digital-currency and consumer-protection rules, that organisations across the bloc must comply with.
