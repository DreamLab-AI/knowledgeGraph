Data archiving is the activity of preserving information in an archive so that a designated user community can access and use it over the long term.[^1][^2]

The CCSDS and ISO reference model defines an archive as an organisation that intends to preserve information for access and use by a Designated Community, and defines long term preservation as maintaining information, independently understandable and with evidence supporting its authenticity.[^1] Its Open Archival Information System is an archive consisting of hardware, software, information and policy-based processes operated by an organisation and its staff.[^1]

In SWEET, archiving has no stated parent, while archive is a subclass of collection and is declared equivalent to archiving.[^2] The ontology also gives an archived data state tied to an archive process.[^3]

The OAIS model says the word long term is meant to cover changing technologies and may extend indefinitely.[^1] Retained sources do not establish retention periods or Earth observation specific archive practice, and the sibling page on the Earth observation data archive covers the organisation rather than the activity.
