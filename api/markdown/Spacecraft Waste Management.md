Spacecraft waste management is the collection, handling and disposal or processing of crew and vehicle waste as one function of a life support system.[^1]

NASA lists waste management among the functions of its Environmental Control and Life Support System, alongside pressure control, ventilation, fire detection and water supply.[^1] The Universal Waste Management System is a toilet that NASA first installed on the International Space Station in 2020, with installation completed in 2021, after technical issues delayed its operation.[^2]

On long-duration platforms such as the station, NASA says pretreated urine is passed to a regenerative system that recycles water, whereas shorter missions may store waste for disposal.[^3] The sources describe NASA hardware only. They do not establish how the toilet has performed since the 2022 paper, or how other agencies handle waste.[^2][^3]
