A microscope is an instrument designed to produce magnified visual or photographic images of objects too small to be seen with the naked eye.[^1]

A microscope has to magnify the specimen, separate the details in the image and render those details visible to the eye or a camera. Designs range from compound microscopes with objectives and condensers to very simple single-lens instruments.[^1] Magnification alone does not settle whether details can be told apart, since the primer treats resolution and contrast as separate requirements.[^1]

Under the metrology vocabulary a microscope would count as a measuring instrument only where it is used for making measurements.[^2] The sources used here describe optical microscopes and do not cover electron or probe microscopes.

Neither source ties microscopes to satellite Earth observation. The link to Earth Observation in this ontology is a relation of the corpus and is not evidenced by the retained sources.
