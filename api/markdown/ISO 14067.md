ISO 14067 is an international standard that specifies principles, requirements, and guidelines for quantifying and reporting the carbon footprint of a product based on life-cycle assessment. It defines how greenhouse-gas emissions associated with a product's life cycle are calculated and communicated, expressed in CO2-equivalent. It provides the methodological backbone for credible product-level carbon-footprint measurement and disclosure.

### Content

- Building on the ISO 14040/14044 LCA framework, it sets requirements for system boundaries, data quality, and the treatment of emissions and removals across raw material, production, use, and end-of-life stages. Consistent application makes product carbon claims comparable and auditable for disclosure and labelling.
