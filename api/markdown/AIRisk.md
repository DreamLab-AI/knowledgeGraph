	- #### Key Risk Categories
	  collapsed:: true
		- **Malicious Use**: Intentional misuse for cyberattacks, disinformation, or harmful content generation
		- **AI Races**: Competitive pressures leading to reduced safety standards and oversight
		- **Organizational Risks**: Inadequate governance, insufficient expertise, or misaligned incentives
		- **Rogue AIs**: Systems behaving in unintended or uncontrollable ways
		- **Accumulative Risks**: Social harms compounding over time into systemic threats
	- #### Types of AI Risk
	  collapsed:: true
		- **Safety Risks**: Unintended behaviors, reliability failures, robustness issues
		- **Security Risks**: Adversarial attacks, model theft, data breaches
		- **Societal Risks**: Bias, discrimination, job displacement, misinformation
		- **Existential Risks (X-risks)**: Potential outcomes causing extinction or unrecoverable civilizational decline
		- **Dual-Use Risks**: Technologies enabling both beneficial and harmful applications
	- #### 2025 AI Safety Index Findings
