A meteorite is a meteoroid that survives a trip through the atmosphere and hits the ground.[^1]

NASA names three major types, the irons, the stonys and the stony-irons.[^2] Meteoroids can be rocky, metallic or a combination, which bears on what is recovered.[^2]

Fragments are sometimes recovered after fireball events, though the CNEOS page does not say that recovery follows every event.[^3]

Meteorite status applies only after landing. Before entry the object is a meteoroid and during the bright passage the event is a meteor.[^1] The sources here do not give a finer classification scheme.
