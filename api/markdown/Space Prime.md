A space prime is a prime contractor in the space sector, the organisation that in the sources used here receives and integrates the contributions of its suppliers into a space system.[^1][^2]

The UK Enterprise Space Category invites views from primes alongside small and medium enterprises, new entrants and niche specialists, which treats primes as one participant type in the space market.[^1] In ESA development practice the prime contractor receives and integrates elements from industry across Europe.[^2]

A UK Government note on space supply chain risk says buyers have largely relied on Tier 1 suppliers and primes to manage the supply chain, and that buyers have poor visibility of it.[^3] UK Space Agency reporting says the transition of smaller companies into mid-tier and prime contractors has not been resolved.[^4]

None of these sources gives a formal definition or a list of space primes, so which firms count depends on the programme and the customer. A prime on one mission can be a supplier on another.[^2][^3]
