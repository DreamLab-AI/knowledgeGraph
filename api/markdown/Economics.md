## Subdisciplines
- **Macroeconomics**: Study of aggregate economic behavior, monetary policy, and systemic effects
- **Microeconomics**: Analysis of individual agent decisions, markets, and price mechanisms
- **Cryptoeconomics**: Cross-disciplinary approach integrating economics, cryptography, computer science, and game theory for decentralized system design
- **Tokenomics**: Theory of tokenized goods and services distribution, analyzing existing or designing new token economies

## Blockchain Economics Context
- Cryptocurrency price shocks are quantitatively important drivers of fluctuations in traditional markets (equities, commodities)
- Digital asset movements carry direct implications for monetary policy and inflation forecasting
- US dollar and gold prices adversely affect Bitcoin and cryptocurrency metrics
- Bitcoin hash rate demonstrates greater market sensitivity than its price

## Token Economics
- Tokenomics defines how tokens function, generate, retain, and distribute value within decentralized systems
- Combines elements of monetary policy, behavioral game theory, and system design encoded in blockchain protocols
- Staking ratio positively predicts token returns
- Over $210 billion USD locked in DeFi protocols as of January 2025

## Regulatory Landscape
- El Salvador became first country to accept Bitcoin as legal tender (June 2021)
- Cuba recognized and regulated cryptocurrencies (August 2021)
- China declared all cryptocurrency transactions illegal (September 2021)
- Ongoing global regulatory framework development affecting blockchain economics
