The measurable impact of artificial intelligence adoption on labor market outcomes, including job displacement, hiring rates, and wage structures across different skill levels and sectors.

### Overview

- **[Industry analysis]** Erik Brynjolfsson and colleagues concluded in a follow-up to their 'Canaries in the Coal Mine' paper that interest rates do not adequately explain the disproportionate decline in entry-level hiring in AI-exposed occupations, and that the timing of the employment decline becomes statistically significant only in 2024 when broad controls are included. *(Source: Erik Brynjolfsson and colleagues, via AI Daily Brief, 2026-08-24)*
