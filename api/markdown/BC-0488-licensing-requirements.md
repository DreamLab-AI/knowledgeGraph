### Technical Details
Key licensing requirements include:
- **Capital Requirements**: Minimum EUR 125,000+ depending on jurisdiction
- **Fit and Proper Test**: Directors and shareholders meet regulatory standards
- **AML/CFT Program**: Documented policies, procedures, and controls
- **IT Security**: Secure systems for customer data and asset protection

### EU MiCA Transition (2024)
The term VASP is being replaced by CASP (Crypto Asset Service Provider) under MiCA regulation. Full effect from end of 2024 with transition period until July 1, 2026. Uniform licensing valid across all EU member states.

### Jurisdictional Comparison

**EU MiCA**: Single passport for all 27 member states. CASPs include exchanges, custody, advisors, portfolio managers.

**Non-EU Europe**: Switzerland, UK, Gibraltar maintain separate VASP regimes following FATF recommendations.

**Timeline**: Typical VASP license takes 1-6 months depending on jurisdiction. Estonia and Lithuania historically faster at 1-3 months.

**Costs**: Range from $10,000 to $100,000+ including government fees, legal services, local hiring, and compliance infrastructure.

### 2024 Market Trends
Lithuania saw 156 fewer licenses in 2024 due to increased capital requirements (EUR 125,000 minimum). Companies migrating to other EU jurisdictions or pursuing MiCA CASP authorization.
