### Technical Details
Key features include:
- **Cloud-Based Infrastructure**: Ready-to-use blockchain networks hosted by vendors
- **Multi-Protocol Support**: Ethereum, Hyperledger Fabric, Corda, and other platforms
- **Development Tools**: APIs, SDKs, and pre-built smart contract templates
- **Managed Services**: Network maintenance, security updates, and scalability handled by provider

### Major Providers
- **IBM Blockchain Platform**: Built on Hyperledger Fabric
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**: Managed Blockchain with Chainlink partnership (Q3 2024)
- **Microsoft Azure**: Azure Blockchain Service
- **Oracle**: BaaS for government agencies (Q3 2024)
- **Alibaba Cloud**: Enterprise-level BaaS platform

### Market Context
BaaS market valued at $3.25 billion in 2024, projected to reach $199.15 billion by 2033 (58% CAGR). Leading applications include supply chain management and financial services.
