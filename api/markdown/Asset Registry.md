- ### Definition
  - An [[Asset Registry]] is a blockchain-based transparent system that records, authenticates, and transfers ownership rights for physical or digital assets through [[Smart Contracts]] and [[Distributed Ledger]] mechanisms, enabling permanent, auditable provenance chains and eliminating intermediary verification costs.

- ### Content

  Asset registries fundamentally streamline asset ownership verification by replacing manual registration systems with cryptographically-secured, permanently-auditable records. Rather than relying on government registries, notaries, or centralised databases, blockchain registries enable anyone to verify asset provenance, ownership history, and authenticity by querying the immutable ledger.

  Applications span physical goods (luxury goods, pharmaceuticals, fine art) and digital assets (domain names, intellectual property, securities). Supply chains benefit from transparent tracking enabling rapid identification of counterfeit goods. Real estate ownership transfers can be completed in minutes rather than weeks of paperwork and intermediary coordination. Digital artwork provenance chains prove artistic authenticity and ownership succession.

  Asset registries introduce challenges including initial digitisation (converting physical assets into blockchain-tracked digital representations), jurisdictional coordination (blockchain ownership claims must align with legal ownership systems), and the long-term maintenance burden of keeping registry information accurate and up-to-date.
