- ### Definition
  - ISO 20400 gives guidance on embedding sustainability into procurement and supply-chain decisions. It is the reference standard implemented by [[Ethical Sourcing]] programmes.
- ### Content
  - The standard extends organisational policy into supplier selection, contract terms, and performance monitoring, covering environmental impact, labour practices, and economic fairness. Being guidance rather than auditable certification, it is typically used to design responsible-sourcing strategies and supplier codes of conduct.
