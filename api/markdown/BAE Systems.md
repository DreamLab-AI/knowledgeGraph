BAE Systems is a UK-based defence contractor whose space activity in the retained sources includes the Space and Mission Systems business formed from its purchase of Ball's aerospace business and the prime contractor role in the space lot of a UK Ministry of Defence research framework.[^1][^2]

GovCon Wire reported on 20 February 2024 that BAE Systems had completed the purchase of Ball's aerospace business for approximately $5.5 billion, and that the business would operate as Space and Mission Systems within BAE's Electronic Systems reporting segment.[^1] According to the same source, the purchase is part of BAE's effort to accelerate its space market strategy and expand its technology offering for the US government.[^1]

Dstl's Serapis framework lists BAE Systems Applied Intelligence as prime contractor for its Space lot. This lot covers defence and security space systems, including fundamental and applied space research, design and manufacture of space equipment and subsystems, space launch and range, and through-life operation of satellites including ground stations.[^2] A framework lot is a route for placing research and development tasks and does not describe the full extent of BAE's space business.

BAE's own description of its space business, its current organisation and its UK space work beyond this framework are therefore not established here.
