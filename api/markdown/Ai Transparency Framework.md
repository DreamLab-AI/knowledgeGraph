### Technical Details
Key components include:
- **Explainability**: Plain-language explanations of AI logic and decision-making processes
- **Interpretability**: Ability for AI systems to be understood by humans in non-technical language
- **Accountability**: Traceability mechanisms assigning clear responsibility for AI decisions and errors
- **Algorithmic Audits**: Independent technical review of AI system behavior and outcomes

### Regulatory Framework
- **EU AI Act**: Effective August 2024, compliance required by August 2026, with penalties up to 35M EUR or 7% global turnover
- **OECD AI Principles**: Values-based principles for trustworthy, transparent AI adopted across G7 and six continents
- **NIST Trustworthy AI**: Attributes including validity, safety, security, privacy, explainability, and fairness
