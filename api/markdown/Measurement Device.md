A measurement device is a device used for making measurements, whether on its own or together with supplementary devices.[^1]

Metrology vocabulary defines measuring instrument rather than listing measurement device as a separate term, so this page uses device in that generic sense. A measuring instrument which can be used alone is a measuring system, and describes a measuring system as able to include other devices and reagents.[^1]

In Earth observation, remote sensing is described as acquiring information about the Earth's surface without contact, by sensing and recording reflected or emitted energy.[^2] Instruments carried on platforms are grouped as passive, which detect energy emitted from the natural environment, or active, which emit energy and collect data from the return signal.[^3]

Sources used here do not define a taxonomy of devices, and the NASA page is a general data-basics description rather than a formal definition. Calibration, uncertainty and traceability of a device are outside what these sources establish.
