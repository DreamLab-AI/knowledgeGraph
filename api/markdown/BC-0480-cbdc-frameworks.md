### Technical Details
Key framework components include:
- **Retail CBDC**: Digital currency for public use replacing or complementing cash
- **Wholesale CBDC (wCBDC)**: Interbank settlement and financial market infrastructure
- **Token-Based vs Account-Based**: Different access and verification models
- **Programmability**: Smart contract capabilities for automated compliance

### Global Status (2024)
- **137 countries** exploring CBDCs representing 98% of global GDP
- **72 countries** in advanced phases (development, pilot, or launch)
- **49 pilot projects** active worldwide
- **3 launched CBDCs**: Bahamas (Sand Dollar), Jamaica (JAM-DEX), Nigeria (eNaira)

### Major Initiatives
- **Digital Yuan (e-CNY)**: Largest pilot with 7 trillion e-CNY ($986B) transaction volume across 17 regions
- **IMF REDI Framework**: Regulatory, Education, Design, and Incentive strategies for adoption
- **BIS Survey 2024**: 91% of 93 surveyed central banks exploring retail or wholesale CBDCs

### Design Considerations
- **Privacy Balance**: Protecting user data while enabling compliance
- **Offline Capability**: Functionality without constant network connectivity
- **Interoperability**: Cross-border payment integration
- **Cyber Resilience**: Protection against security threats

### Regulatory Developments
U.S. Executive Order 14178 (January 2025) prohibited federal agencies from establishing, issuing, or promoting CBDCs, representing a significant policy shift in the largest economy.
