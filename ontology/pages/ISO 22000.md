- ### Definition
  - ISO 22000 specifies requirements for a food safety management system spanning the whole food chain. It is the standard implemented by operations such as [[Cold Chain Monitoring]].
- ### Content
  - It combines hazard analysis (HACCP), prerequisite programmes, and a Plan-Do-Check-Act management framework so organisations identify, control, and continually improve food-safety risks. Certification gives downstream partners assurance that temperature-sensitive and perishable goods are handled within validated controls.
