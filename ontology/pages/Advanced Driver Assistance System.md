- ### Definition
	- An Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS) is a suite of sensor-based technologies built into a vehicle that assist a human driver with perception, warning, or partial control tasks such as lane keeping, adaptive cruise control, and automatic emergency braking. It sits below full autonomy on the driving-automation scale, keeping the human driver responsible for the overall task. Standards such as SAE J3016 define the levels of automation ADAS features correspond to.
